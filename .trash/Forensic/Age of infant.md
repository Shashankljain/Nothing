### **1. Definition of Viability** ⏳

- **Viability** is defined as the capability of a fetus to maintain a separate, independent existence outside the maternal womb .
- ★ **Important Exam Point:** Legally and medically, viability is attained at completed **28 weeks (7 months) of intrauterine life (IUL)** .

---

### **2. Ossification Centers of Viability (7th Month)** 🦴

To prove a fetus had reached the age of viability (28 weeks/7 months) at the time of birth, the following ossification centers must be visualized radiologically or during autopsy :

|**Ossification Center**|**Anatomical Bone**|**Age of Appearance (IUL)**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|★ **Talus**|Tarsal bone of the foot|**7th Month (28 Weeks)**|
|★ **2nd & 3rd Sternebrae**|Segments of the body of the sternum|**7th Month (28 Weeks)**|

> 🧠 **Mnemonic for 7th Month Centers: "SevenTH"**
> 
> - **Seven** = **7**th Month
> - **T** = **T**alus
> - **H** = **H**alf of the sternal body segments (**2nd and 3rd pieces**)

---

### **3. Pre- & Post-Viability Centers (For Chronological Comparison)** 📊

To precisely estimate the gestational age, the forensic pathologist compares the viability centers with earlier and later markers :

- **Pre-Viability Centers (< 28 weeks):**
    - _Clavicle:_ **5 to 6 weeks IUL** (1st bone in the body to ossify).
    - _Calcaneum (Tarsal bone):_ **5th Month IUL**.
    - _Manubrium & 1st Sternebra:_ **5th Month IUL**.
- **Post-Viability / Term Centers (> 28 weeks):**
    - _Lower end of Femur:_ **36 Weeks / 9 Months IUL** (indicates maturity).
    - _Cuboid:_ **9 Months IUL**.
    - _Upper end of Tibia:_ **At Birth / Full Term**.

---

### **4. Medico-Legal Importance** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Infanticide vs. Feticide:** If a dead infant is found abandoned, demonstrating these 7th-month ossification centers proves the child was viable . Any active destruction of a viable child is prosecuted under **Section 103 BNS, 2023** (Punishment for Murder/Infanticide) or **Section 91 BNS, 2023** (preventing a child from being born alive) .
