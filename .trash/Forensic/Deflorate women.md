[[Virginity,preg]]
**Genital Findings: Virgin vs. Deflorate Women** 🩺📊

The **loss of virginity**, classically characterized by the **rupture/tearing of the hymen** as a result of the first sexual act

★ **Important Exam Point:** Recreating this classic morphological comparison table is highly favored by university external examiners to score maximum marks.

| **Anatomical Landmark**     | **Virginity (Intact Status)** 👩‍⚕️                            | **Defloration (Ruptured Status)** 💔                                  |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Hymen**                | **Intact**; continuous, smooth, and elastic.                   | **Torn** (typically posterolaterally).                                |
| **2. Labia Majora**         | **Closely apposed** (completely covers the vaginal introitus). | **Not so apposed** (the introitus appears exposed and gaping).        |
| **3. Labia Minora**         | Pink, soft, delicate, and highly sensitive.                    | Pigmented, slightly thickened/flabby, and less sensitive.             |
| **4. Posterior Commissure** | **Intact** and tight.                                          | **Torn** or permanently scarred.                                      |
| **6. Fossa Navicularis**    | **Intact**; represented as a distinct depression.              | **Disappears** due to tearing of the posterior commissure/fourchette. |
| **5. Fourchette**           | **Intact** and sharp.                                          | **Torn** or permanently scarred.                                      |
| **7. Vaginal Canal**        | **Narrow** with prominent, rough transverse **rugosities**.    | **Dilated (roomy)**; **rugosities are absent** or flattened.          |
| **8. Breasts**              | Small, firm, hemispherical, and elastic.                       | Large, flabby, and pendulous.                                         |
| **9. Areola & Nipple**      | Small; nipple is flat or minimally projected.                  | Large, pigmented, and perm                                            |
