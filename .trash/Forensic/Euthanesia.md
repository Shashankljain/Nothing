### **1. Definitions**

- ★ **Euthanasia (Mercy Killing):** It is defined as producing painless death of a person suffering from hopelessly in curable and painful disease
- **Etymology:** Derived from two Greek words:
    - _Eu_ = **Good**
    - _Thanatos_ = **Death** (Hence, literally translates to "a good death") [81, 82].
- ★ **Right to Life vs. Right to Die:**
    - **Article 21 of the Indian Constitution** guarantees the fundamental **"Right to Life and Personal Liberty"** [image/slide].
    - The Supreme Court of India has repeatedly ruled that Article 21 **does not include the right to die** (except for passive euthanasia under strict judicial and medical supervision). Active euthanasia violates Article 21 [image/slide].

## **PART II: CLASSICAL CLASSIFICATION OF EUTHANASIA** 📊

Euthanasia is classified based on the **mechanism of action** and the **will of the patient**:

```
                              [CLASSIFICATION OF EUTHANASIA]
                                            │
         ┌──────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                
 [Based on Action]                                 [Based on Patient's Will]
   • **Active (Aggressive)**: Lethal drugs.    • **Voluntary**: Patient's                                                     explicit consent.
   • **Passive (Negative)**: Withdraw life-support.    • **Non-Voluntary**:                                                     Patient unconscious (Coma).
                                   • **Involuntary**: Against patient's will.
```

### **1. Based on the Mechanism of Action** 🧪

#### **A. Active Euthanasia (Positive / Aggressive Euthanasia)**

- **Mechanism:** Actively and deliberately administering a lethal substance or performing an intervention to cause immediate, painless death [image/slide].
- ★ **Commonly Used Agents:** - **Active Euthanasia Agents: "P-O-P-I"**

- **P** – **P**henobarbitone (M/C) [image/slide]
- **O** – **O**pium / Morphine [215, 216]
- **P** – **P**otassium Chloride (KCl) [image/slide]
- **I** – **I**nsulin [image/slide]

    - **Phenobarbitone (Most Common / M/C)** [image/slide]
    - **Thiopental Sodium (IV)** (Dutch/Belgian protocol: Thiopental to induce coma, followed by pancuronium bromide to paralyze muscles) [224]
    - **Morphine / Opium** [215, 216]
    - **Potassium Chloride (KCl)** (induces cardiac arrest in diastole) [image/slide]
    - **Insulin** (causes fatal hypoglycemia) [image/slide]
- **Legal Status in India:** **Strictly illegal** and prosecuted as criminal homicide [image/slide].

#### **B. Passive Euthanasia (Negative Euthanasia)**

- **Mechanism:** Painlessly letting a patient die by **withholding (not starting) or withdrawing (stopping)** life-sustaining medical interventions (e.g., turning off a mechanical ventilator, disconnecting an ICU monitor, or removing a feeding tube) [image/slide].
- _Historical Landmark:_ The **Netherlands** was the first country in the world to legalize passive euthanasia [image/slide].
- **Legal Status in India:** **Legally permitted** under strict medical and judicial guidelines [image/slide].

---

### **2. Based on the Will of the Patient** 🗣️

- ★ **Voluntary Euthanasia:** Carried out at the explicit, competent, and persistent request of the patient themselves (using a _Living Will_ or verbal request) [image/slide].
- ★ **Non-Voluntary Euthanasia:** Done when the patient is physically or mentally **incapable of giving valid consent** or expressing their will (e.g., patients in a deep coma, Persistent Vegetative State [PVS], or anencephalic infants) [87, image/slide].
- ★ **Involuntary Euthanasia:** Done **without the consent of the patient** or against their explicitly expressed will [image/slide]. Medically and legally, this is equated directly to **murder**in all jurisdictions.
