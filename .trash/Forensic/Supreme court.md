[[legal procedure]]
### **Supreme Court of India**

---

#### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition** The **Supreme Court of India** is the highest judicial tribunal and the final court of appeal under the Constitution of India.

**Introduction** Located in New Delhi, it is the "Biggest in the country". It serves as the ultimate guardian of the Constitution and has the power of judicial review over all other courts and government actions.

**Objectives**

- To serve as the final authority in interpreting the law.
- To hear appeals against the judgments of the High Courts.
- To protect the fundamental rights of citizens through writ jurisdiction.

---

#### **Medico-Legal Importance**

**1. Commutation of Death Sentence** The Supreme Court holds the **2nd power of commutation**.

- **Process:** If a death sentence awarded by a Sessions Court is confirmed by the High Court, the convict can appeal to the Supreme Court.
- **Action:** The Supreme Court may either confirm the death sentence or change it (e.g., to life imprisonment).
- **Final Step:** If the Supreme Court confirms the sentence, the final "Power of Amnesty" (pardon) lies with the **President of India**.

**2. Consumer Protection (COPRA)** Under the **Consumer Protection Act (2019)**, if a party is unsatisfied with the decision of the National Consumer Redressal Commission, a further appeal can be filed in the **Supreme Court**.

---

#### **Landmark Medico-Legal Rulings**

The Supreme Court has issued several directives that have fundamentally changed medical and forensic practice in India:

- **Deception Detection (Narco-analysis/Polygraph):** The Court declared that tests like narco-analysis, polygraph, and brain-mapping **cannot be conducted without the consent** of the individual. Even with consent, the results are **inadmissible as evidence**, protecting the right against self-incrimination under **Article 20(3)** of the Constitution.
- **Transgender Rights:** The Court directed that all official documents (including voter and Aadhar cards) must include a third category marked **'transgender or third gender'**.
- **HIV Confidentiality:** The Court ruled that a doctor can disclose a patient's **HIV positive status** to their spouse/prospective spouse, and that knowingly exposing others to health risks is a punishable offense.
- **Acid Attacks:** In 2013, the Court ruled on the **regulation of acid sales** and mandated that both private and public hospitals must treat acid attack victims **free of charge**.
- **Viscera Preservation:** The Court mandated that in suspected poisoning cases, the viscera must be sent to a **Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL)** immediately after the postmortem.
- **NDPS Act:** Ruled that the weight of neutral substances mixed with drugs must be included when determining if the quantity is "small" or "commercial".

---

#### **Relevant Legal Concepts**

- **Article 20(3) of the Constitution:** No person accused of any offense shall be compelled to be a witness against himself. This is the basis for the Supreme Court's ruling on narco-analysis.
- **Power of Amnesty:** The ultimate authority held by the President to forgive a death sentence after all court appeals (including the Supreme Court) are exhausted.

---

#### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Final Appellate Court:** The last resort for appeals from the High Court.
- ★ **Unlimited Fine/Sentence:** Like the High Court, it can impose any legal penalty.
- ★ **Article 20(3):** The Constitutional provision used to ban involuntary narco-analysis.
- ★ **Landmark Case (Viscera):** Mandatory immediate FSL dispatch for viscera.

#### **Viva Questions**

1. **Who has the power to commute a death sentence after the Supreme Court confirms it?**
    - _Ans:_ The President of India (Power of Amnesty).
2. **Can narco-analysis results be used as evidence if the patient consented?**
    - _Ans:_ No. The Supreme Court ruled they are not admissible as evidence, even with consent.
3. **To which court do you appeal a decision from the National Consumer Commission?**
    - _Ans:_ The Supreme Court.
4. **What did the Supreme Court rule regarding the treatment of acid attack victims?**
    - _Ans:_ It mandated that all hospitals (private and public) must provide treatment free of charge.

#### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **Status:** Highest Court of India.
- **Hierarchy:** Supreme Court > High Court > Sessions Court.
- **Appeals:** Final court for COPRA and Criminal appeals.
- **Death Penalty:** Final judicial word before the President's pardon.
- **Key Law:** Upholds **Article 20(3)** of the Constitution (Self-incrimination).
- **Social Impact:** Directed 3rd gender recognition and regulated acid sales.
