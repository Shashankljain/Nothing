[[Mechanical injuries]]
### **Definitions**

- ★ **Bruise (Contusion):** An injury characterized by the **extravasation (leakage) of blood** into the subcutaneous, subepithelial, or interstitial tissues due to the **rupture of blood vessels (usually capillaries and venules)**, caused by the application of a **blunt force or pressure**, with the overlying skin/epithelium remaining **intact** [220].
- ★ **Bruise vs. Contusion (The Spatial Distinction):**
    - **Bruise:** Refers to the extravasation of blood observed directly through the **intact skin** as a bluish-purple discoloration and localized swelling [220, 221].
    - **Contusion:** Refers to a bruise located **deeply within internal organs or tissues** (e.g., contusion of the brain, muscles, liver, or mesentery), which may not be visible on the skin surface [220, 221].

## **PART VI: MEDICO-LEGAL IMPORTANCE (MLI)** ⚖️🩺

### **1. Why is a Bruise Legally Important?**

- **Estimation of Age of Injury:** Helps match the timeline of the assault with the statements of the victim or suspect [237].
- **Estimation of the Degree of Force:** Larger, deeper, or multiple bruises reflect a higher level of violent physical impact [237].
- **Identification of the Weapon:** Patterned bruises (e.g., tram-lines, tyre treads, shoe sole imprints) directly link a suspect's weapon or vehicle to the victim [211, 227, 237].
- ★ **Manner and Distribution of Assault:**
    - _Grip Marks on Arms:_ Indicates restraint (3-4 finger bruises on one side, large thumb bruise on the other) [240].
    - _Bruises on Inner Thighs / Breasts:_ Strongly indicative of **sexual assault** [228, 241].
    - _Bruises on Shoulder Blades:_ Suggests the victim was forcibly pinned against the ground or a hard wall [241].
    - _Six-penny (Coin-shaped) Bruises:_ Produced by forceful, localized poking of fingertips (classically seen on the neck in **manual strangulation/throttling**) [173, 241].
    - _Multiple Bruises of Varying Ages:_ Found in different stages of healing on the cheeks, trunk, or buttocks of a child, suggesting **Child Abuse (Battered Baby Syndrome)** [241, 242, 276].
