# ⚖️🩺 **CONTRIBUTORY NEGLIGENCE (3-MARK MODEL ANSWER)** 🩺⚖️

---

### **1. Definition** 📚

- ★ **Contributory Negligence:** It is defined as a omission on the part of the **patient** that along  with the Doctors negligence contributed to injury complaint of, as a direct cause and without which the injury would not have occurred
- 
- It occurs when the patient fails to exercise reasonable care for their own safety during the course of medical treatment, history-taking, or follow-up instructions [Image 70].

---

### **2. Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Partial Defense in Civil Cases:** Contributory negligence acts as a **partial defense** for doctors in **civil negligence (tort/consumer) lawsuits** [Image 70].
    - _The Damage Reduction Rule:_ If proven, the court reduces the amount of financial compensation (damages) the doctor is ordered to pay, in proportion to the patient’s share of the blame [Image 70].
- ★ **No Defense in Criminal Cases:** It is **strictly not applicable** as a defense in criminal trials under **Section 106 BNS, 2023** (formerly Section 304A IPC) [Image 70, 72]. The patient's negligence does not excuse a doctor's reckless/criminal actions.
- ★ **Burden of Proof:** The legal burden of proving contributory negligence lies **entirely on the defendant (doctor)**[Image 70].

---

### **3. Two Crucial Limitations (The Doctrines)** 🔄

The application of this defense is governed and restricted by two primary legal rules [Image 70]:

```
                     [LIMITATIONS TO THE DEFENSE]
                                  │
         ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                 ▼
  [Last Clear Chance Doctrine]                [Avoidable Consequences Rule]
 • Doctor had the final opportunity to     • Patient's failure to follow advice
   prevent harm but missed it.           aggravated an already existing injury.
 • **Doctor is held liable**.         • **Patient is liable** for aggravation.
```

1. ★ **Last Clear Chance Doctrine:** If the doctor had the final opportunity (the "last clear chance") to prevent the complication but missed it due to carelessness, the doctor is held fully responsible, and the defense of contributory negligence is rejected [Image 70].
2. ★ **Avoidable Consequences Rule:** If the patient's subsequent negligence (e.g., refusing to clean a wound or take prescribed antibiotics) aggravates an injury initially caused by the doctor's mistake, the patient is held liable for that aggravated portion of the damage [Image 70].
