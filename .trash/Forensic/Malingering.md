### **Definitions**

- ★ **Malingering:** The **conscious, intentional, and planned feigning** To having a disease in order to achieve specific goal 
- ★ **External Incentives (Motives):** Unlike other psychiatric disorders, malingering is always driven by clear-cut secondary gains, such as [168, 178, 228]:
    1. Evading criminal trial, prosecution, or corporal punishment [228].
    2. Escaping difficult, hazardous, or unpleasant duties (e.g., soldiers or prisoners avoiding work) [168, 169].
    3. Obtaining financial compensation (e.g., insurance frauds or injury lawsuits) [168, 169].
    4. Simulating a criminal offense to file false charges against an enemy [168].

## **PART IV: MALINGERING OF CLINICAL SYSTEMIC ILLNESS** 🌡️🧪

Malingerers may ingest or topically apply specific substances to simulate systemic clinical diseases:

1. ★ **The "Tobacco-in-Axilla" Trick (Feigned Fever/Tachycardia):**
    - _The Method:_ Soaking tobacco leaves in water and placing them directly in the axillae overnight [443].
    - _The Mechanism:_ Nicotine is rapidly absorbed through the thin, vascular axillary skin into the systemic circulation [443].
    - _The Feigned Symptoms:_ By morning, the individual exhibits systemic nicotine toxicity: **fever, cold clammy sweats, vomiting, and a rapid, weak, irregular pulse**, simulating a severe febrile illness or acute cardiac distress [443].
2. **Feigned Conjunctivitis:**
    - _The Method:_ Instilling the irritating juice of the **Marking Nut** (_Semecarpus anacardium_) or _Calotropis_ latex into the eyes [348, 350].
    - _The Feigned Symptoms:_ Simulates severe acute conjunctivitis or trauma to support a claim of physical assault [348].

## **PART V: RELEVANT LAW (LATEST 2023 FORENSIC CODES)** ⚖️

★ **Important Exam Point:** Replacing outdated IPC sections with the modern **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** codes is essential to secure top marks.

```
                      [LEGAL PENALTIES FOR MALINGERING]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                            ▼                            ▼
  [Section 228 BNS]            [Section 234 BNS]            [Section 22 BNS]
 • Fabricating false evidence. • False medical certificates.• Defense of                                                                    Insanity.
 • **Max 7 years prison**.     • Punishes complicit doctor.  • Requires                                                                 psychiatric board.
```

- ★ **Section 228 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 193 IPC) – Fabricating False Evidence:**
    - _The Law:_ Anyone who intentionally fabricates false evidence (including fabricating wounds or artificial bruises on their body to incriminate another) is liable for criminal prosecution [62].
    - _Punishment:_ Up to **7 years of imprisonment and a fine** [62].
- ★ **Section 234 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 197 IPC) – Issuing False Medical Certificates:**
    - _The Law:_ If a registered medical practitioner assists a malingerer by knowingly issuing a false medical, sickness, or injury certificate, the doctor is criminally liable.
    - _Punishment:_ Heavy criminal fines, imprisonment, and disciplinary **Penal Erasure** (loss of registration) by the State Medical Council [29, Image 65].
- ★ **Section 22 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 84 IPC) – Pleas of Insanity:**
    - Governs the legal test of insanity (McNaughten's Rule) [240]. If a criminal feigns insanity under Section 22 BNS, they must undergo a compulsory clinical evaluation by a government psychiatric board under **Sections 328/329 CrPC (retained in BNSS, 2023)** [245, 246]
