[[Death]]
**Sudden Death** is defined as death occurring instantaneously or within **1 hour** of the onset of morbid symptoms (though the WHO allows a limit of **24 hours** for statistical purposes). It typically occurs in a person who was not previously known to be suffering from any dangerous disease or injury.

**Prevalence** Cardiovascular causes are the most common reason for sudden natural death, accounting for **44–50%** of all such cases.

---

**2. Classification of Cardiovascular Causes**

Cardiovascular causes of sudden death can be classified into the following categories:

|**Category**|**Specific Conditions**|
|---|---|
|**Ischemic Heart Disease**|**Coronary artery atherosclerosis** (Most common cause), Myocardial Infarction (MI).|
|**Hypertensive Disease**|Hypertensive heart disease, Left ventricular hypertrophy.|
|**Valvular Diseases**|Aortic stenosis, Mitral valve prolapse.|
|**Congenital Defects**|Bicuspid aortic valve, Coarctation of the aorta, Septal defects.|
|**Inflammatory/Infectious**|Myocarditis, Pericarditis, Infective endocarditis.|
|**Vascular Lesions**|**Aortic aneurysm rupture**, Dissecting aneurysm.|
|**Miscellaneous**|Cardiac tamponade, Cardiomyopathies, Conduction system lesions.|
