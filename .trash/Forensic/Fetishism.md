## **PART I: BASIC CONCEPTS & DEFINITION** 📚

### **1. Definition**

- ★ **Fetishism:** It is a type of **sexual perversion (Paraphilia)** where sexual arousal, excitement, and gratification are dependent upon or significantly enhanced by the use of **inanimate objects** (the "fetish") or non-genital body parts [image/slide].

---

### **2. The "6-W" Analytical Framework** ❓

- **What?** An abnormal sexual urge where gratification is achieved through inanimate objects (most commonly female undergarments like **bras and underpants**, shoes, or gloves) of the opposite sex [image/slide].
- **Why?** Attributed to deep-seated psychological conditioning during early childhood or puberty where an inanimate object gets falsely associated with sexual pleasure.
- **How?** The individual achieves orgasm/gratification by touching, holding, smelling, looking at, or masturbating with the fetish object [image/slide].
- **When?** The condition typically manifests around **puberty** and persists as a chronic pattern in adulthood.
- **Where?** Commonly encountered clinically in psychiatric setups or forensically when the individual resorts to criminal acts to obtain the fetish.
- **Who?** ★ **Males are predominantly affected** compared to females (**M > F**) [image/slide].

## **PART III: MEDICO-LEGAL IMPORTANCE (MLI) & RELEVANT LAW**⚖️🩺

Although Fetishism is primarily a psychiatric condition, it has significant forensic implications when it drives illegal behavior:

1. ★ **Theft & Trespassing:** Fetishists often steal female undergarments from clotheslines, open yards, or break into houses, making them liable for prosecution under:
    - **Section 303 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 379 IPC) — Theft:** Punishable by up to 3 years of imprisonment or a fine.
2. ★ **Obscene Acts in Public:** Practicing fetishistic acts in public spaces to shock others is prosecuted under:
    - **Section 296 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 294 IPC) — Obscenity:** Punishable with up to **3 months of imprisonment**, a fine, or both [image/slide].
3. **Domestic & Marital Discord:** Can be used as a strong ground for judicial separation or divorce by an aggrieved spouse due to mental cruelty.

## **PART IV: HIGH-YIELD EXAM CORNER & VIVA QUESTIONS** ★

### **Viva Questions & Model Answers** 💬

**Q1: What is the difference between Fetishism and Transvestic Fetishism?**  
**Answer:** In pure **Fetishism**, the individual is sexually aroused by the inanimate object itself (e.g., holding a bra) [image/slide]. In **Transvestic Fetishism**, the individual achieves arousal specifically by **wearing** the clothes of the opposite sex [image/slide].

**Q2: What is the legal status of consensual adult paraphilias in India under the BNS, 2023?**  
**Answer:** Consensual, private paraphilic acts between adults that do not harm others are **not punishable** [image/slide]. However, if they involve theft of objects, trespassing, public obscenity (under Sec. 296 BNS) [image/slide], or involve minors (under the **POCSO Act**), they attract strict criminal liability [image/slide].
