# 🧬👴 **ATAVISM (3-MARK MODEL UNIVERSITY ANSWER)** 👴🧬

---

### **1. Definition & Origin** 📚

- **Atavism** (derived from the Latin word _atavus_, meaning **ancestor**) refers to the genetic phenomenon where an individual manifests physical, physiological, or pathological characteristics of a remote ancestor (such as grandparents) rather than their immediate parents [Image 141].

---

### **2. Biological Mechanism** ⚙️

- **No Parental Resemblance:** The child does not resemble their immediate biological parents [Image 141].
- **Grandparental Resemblance:** The child resembles their grandparents or more distant ancestors [Image 141].
- **Genetic Basis:** This occurs due to the **recombination and transmission of recessive genes** [Image 141]. These traits remain silent (dormant) in the parental generation but express phenotypically in the offspring when matching alleles pair up [Image 141].

---

### **3. Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Important Exam Point: Disputed Paternity & Legitimacy:**
    - If a child is born with physical traits (such as a different skin complexion, iris colour, hair texture, or a rare congenital anomaly) that do not match their immediate parents, it can trigger false allegations of **adultery or illegitimacy** [Image 140, 141].
    - Understanding **atavism** provides a vital scientific defense in court, proving that these differences are due to normal ancestral genetic transmission [Image 141].
- ★ **Resolution:** In modern forensic practice, any confusion caused by atavistic traits is resolved definitively using **DNA Fingerprinting** [Image 244].
