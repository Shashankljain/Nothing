**III. Lochial Discharge (The Lochia)** 📊

Lochia is the physiological vaginal discharge following delivery, containing ==blood, necrotic decidual tissue, and mucus==. Its color progression is the **gold-standard clinical marker** used to date a delivery [Image 10]:

```
                     [POST-DELIVERY LOCHIAL TIMELINE]
                                    │
         ┌──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┐
         ▼                          ▼                          ▼
  [Lochia Rubra]             [Lochia Serosa]            [Lochia Alba]
  • Days 1 to 4              • Days 4 to 10             • Days 10 to 28
  • Bright Red               • Pinkish-Brown            • White / Yellow
```

|**Type of Lochia**|**Duration Post-Delivery** [Image 10]|**Color & Characteristics** [Image 10]|**Forensic & Diagnostic Value**|
|---|---|---|---|
|★ **Lochia Rubra**|**1 to 3–4 days**|**Bright Red**; rich in fresh blood, decidual shreds, and fetal membranes.|Confirms delivery occurred within the last 72 hours.|
|★ **Lochia Serosa**|**4 to 10 days**|**Pinkish-brown**; contains serous exudate, leucocytes, mucus, and less blood.|Establishes a post-delivery interval of roughly 1 week.|
|★ **Lochia Alba**|**10 to 28 days**|**White / Pale Yellow**; rich in leucocytes, epithelial cells, and cervical mucus.|Confirms a post-delivery interval of 2 to 4 weeks.|
