[[Sexual offence]]
For an MBBS student, the topic of **Unnatural Sexual Offences** is historically significant in Forensic Medicine. Under the new Indian legal framework—the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**—the classification and legal handling of these acts have undergone a major transformation.

---

### **1. Basic Concepts**

**Definition** In forensic terms, a natural sexual offence follows the **peno-vaginal route**. An **unnatural sexual offence**refers to sexual acts performed via a **non-peno-vaginal route**, such as the oral cavity, anal canal, or with animals.

**Classification of Unnatural Acts**

1. **Sodomy (Anal Sex/Buggery):** Sexual intercourse per anum between male-male or male-female.
2. **Buccal Coitus (Oral Sex):** Coitus per os, including Fellatio (oral stimulation of male genitalia) and Cunnilingus (oral stimulation of female genitalia).
3. **Bestiality:** Sexual contact between a human and a lower animal.
4. **Lesbianism (Sapphism/Tribadism):** Sexual gratification between two females.

---

### **2. Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Criminal Investigation:** Identifying the perpetrator through DNA or injury patterns in non-consensual acts.
- **Civil Cases:** Historically and currently, such acts may be used as grounds for **divorce**.
- **★ Important Exam Point:** Under the new BNS, 2023, the focus has shifted from the "nature" of the act to the **issue of consent**.

---

### **3. Relevant Law (BNS, 2023)**

**The Major Shift**

- **IPC 377 (Old Law):** Previously, "unnatural offences" were a separate category.
- **BNS 2023 Update:** The category of "Unnatural Offences" has been **deleted** as a standalone section.
- **Current Status:** Consensual acts between adults (>18 years) are generally **not punishable**.

**Relevant BNS Section**

- **Section 63 BNS (Rape):** The new definition of rape now includes the non-consensual penetration of the **anal canal, oral cavity, or urethra** with a penis or any object.
- **Medico-legal Relevance:** If a man performs sodomy or oral sex on a woman or child without valid consent, he is charged with **Rape** under Section 63 BNS, rather than an "unnatural offence".

---

### **4. Medico-Legal Examination: Sodomy**

In cases of alleged sodomy (non-consensual anal intercourse), the medical officer must examine the **passive agent**(victim) and the **active agent** (accused).

**Examination Procedure**

- **Position:** The subject is usually examined in the **Knee-Elbow position**.
- **Lateral Buttock Traction Test:** The buttocks are pulled apart to inspect the anus. In a normal anus, the sphincter contracts; in habitual sodomy, it may show **Paradoxical dilatation**.

**Differentiating Habitual vs. Non-Habitual Passive Agents**

|**Feature**|**Habitual Passive Agent**|**Non-Habitual Passive Agent**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Pain/Tenderness**|Absent.|**Present** and acute.|
|**Anal Opening**|Funnel-shaped/depressed.|Fresh bleeding/tears.|
|**Sphincter**|Relaxed/Dilated.|Spasmodic or torn.|
|**Perianal Skin**|Thickened and keratinized.|Bruised (Tyre sign/hematoma).|
|**Rugosity**|Loss of mucosal rugae.|Rugae are preserved.|

---

### **5. Specimen Collection**

Evidence must be collected to prove sexual contact:

- **Anal Swabs:** Must be collected to detect **spermatozoa** (motile or non-motile) or seminal markers like **p30**.
- **DNA Profiling:** Swabs are used for DNA matching between the victim and the suspect.
- **Clothing:** Preserve garments for the detection of blood, semen, or faecal matter.

---

### **6. Paraphilias (Sexual Perversions)**

While not always "offences," these are abnormal acts for sexual gratification often tested alongside this topic:

- **Sadism:** Deriving pleasure from giving pain.
- **Masochism:** Deriving pleasure from receiving pain.
- **Voyeurism:** Watching others in private acts (Punishable under **Section 77 BNS**).
- **Exhibitionism:** Showing private parts (Punishable under **Section 296 BNS**).

---

### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **BNS 63:** Now covers non-consensual oral and anal penetration under Rape.
- ★ **Lateral Buttock Traction Test:** Used to identify the habitual passive agent.
- ★ **Paradoxical Dilatation:** When the anal sphincter dilates instead of contracting upon traction.
- ★ **Tyre Sign:** An anal hematoma/bruise seen in forceful, non-consensual sodomy.

---

### **Viva Questions**

**Q1: Is consensual sodomy between adults a crime under BNS?** **Answer:** No. Consensual acts between adults (>18 years) are not punishable under the new BNS framework.

**Q2: What is "Paradoxical Dilatation"?** **Answer:** It is a finding in habitual passive agents of sodomy where the anal sphincter dilates upon bimanual traction of the buttocks, rather than contracting.

**Q3: Which section of BNS covers non-consensual oral sex?** **Answer:** It is covered under the definition of **Rape** in **Section 63 BNS**.

### **Model University Answer: 5 Marks**

**Q: Describe the medical examination findings in a habitual passive agent of sodomy.**

1. **Position:** Examine in the knee-elbow position.
2. **External Appearance:** The anus appears funnel-shaped and the perianal fat is absorbed.
3. **Skin Changes:** Perianal skin becomes thickened, smooth, and loses its normal rugosity (folds).
4. **Sphincter Tone:** The sphincter is relaxed. The **Lateral Buttock Traction Test** shows **Paradoxical Dilatation** (the anus opens instead of closing).
5. **Pathology:** There may be chronic fissures or prolapse of the rectal mucosa.
6. **Evidence:** Collect anal swabs for DNA and seminal analysis.
