# 👶🍂 **FETUS COMPRESSUS (3-MARK MODEL ANSWER)** 🍂👶

---

### **1. Definition & Synonym** 📚

- **Fetus Compressus** (also classically known as **Fetus Papyraceus**) refers to a dead, mummified, flattened, and paper-thin (parchment-like) fetus retained in the uterine cavity alongside a living co-twin [`input_file_140.png`].

---

### **2. Etiology & Biomechanical Mechanism** ⚙️

- **Occurs In:** Twin pregnancies (most commonly dizygotic twins associated with **Superfecundation** or, exceptionally rarely, **Superfoetation**) [`input_file_140.png`].
- **Mechanism:**
    - One fetus dies early in utero (typically in the late first or early second trimester) and undergoes aseptic autolysis/mummification [`input_file_140.png`].
    - As the amniotic fluid of the dead twin is completely absorbed, the **normally growing, live co-twin exerts mechanical pressure**, compressing and flattening the dead fetus against the uterine wall or the placenta [`input_file_140.png`].

---

### **3. Medico-Legal & Autopsy Significance** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Proof of Multi-Gestation:** Confirms a multiple/twin pregnancy even if only one healthy neonate is delivered alive at term [`input_file_140.png`].
- ★ **Estimation of Time of Death:** Differentiating it from fresh intrauterine death (**maceration** which has skin slippage and joint hypermobility) proves that intrauterine fetal demise occurred **weeks to months prior** to delivery [`input_file_11.png`].
- **Excluding Criminal Interference:** Proves that the fetal death was natural, intrauterine, and aseptic, immediately exonerating the mother of any allegations of feticide, criminal abortion, or maternal physical trauma [`input_file_11.png`, `input_file_140.png`].
