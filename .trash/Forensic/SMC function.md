# 🏛️🩺 **STATE MEDICAL COUNCIL (SMC): POWERS, FUNCTIONS, AND DISCIPLINARY CONTROL** 🩺🏛️

---


## **PART II: CORE FUNCTIONS OF THE SMC**(RED 🍎) 📊

The primary functions of the State Medical Council are divided into three major categories: **Registration**, **Disciplinary Control**, and **Erasure** [Image 65].

```

                                             │
         ┌───────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┐
  ▼                         ▼                                   ▼
   [Registration]               [Disciplinary Action]          [Erasure]
 • State Medical Register.   • Complaints & Convictions.            •                                                                    Death/Untraceable.
 • Enroll new members (NMC).  • **Odd-numbered Sub-Committee**.      • **Penal                                                        Erasure** (Misconduct).
```

### **1. Registration (Most Important Function)** ✍️

- ★ **State Medical Register:** The SMC has the absolute duty to compile, maintain, and update a comprehensive **State Medical Register** containing the names, addresses, and qualifications of all RMPs licensed to practice within the state [Image 65].
- **Central Sync:** This list is synchronized and updated with the centralized **National Medical Register** maintained by the NMC [Image 65].
- **Enrollment:** Enrolls new medical graduates as members upon verification of their qualifying degrees [Image 65].

---

### **2. Disciplinary Action Against RMPs** 🔨

- ★ **Inquiry Initiation:** The SMC registers and acts upon formal complaints of professional misconduct (infamous conduct) filed against any RMP [Image 65].
- ★ **Suo-Moto Cognizance:** The SMC can independently initiate disciplinary proceedings even without a formal complaint if a doctor is convicted of a criminal offense or if misconduct is reported in public domains [Image 65].
- ★ **The Disciplinary Sub-Committee:**
    - To investigate charges, the President of the SMC constitutes a dedicated sub-committee [Image 65].
    - **SMC Exam Point:** The sub-committee **MUST consist of an ODD number of doctors of good repute** to ensure a majority decision can be reached without ties [Image 65].
- **Outcome of Enquiry:**
    - _If Not Proven:_ The RMP is fully acquitted of all charges [Image 65].
    - _If Proven:_ Disciplinary penalties are imposed [Image 65, 291].

---

### **3. Erasure of Names from the Register** ❌

The SMC is empowered to remove a doctor's name from the active register under four conditions [Image 65]:

1. **Death of the Doctor:** Automatic removal upon receipt of death notification [Image 65].
2. **Untraceable Doctor:** If a doctor cannot be contacted or traced at their registered address over a prolonged period [Image 65].
3. **Error in Details:** If it is discovered that the name was entered due to clerical errors, misrepresentation, or falsified documentation [Image 65].
4. ★ **Penal Erasure:** Disciplinary removal of a doctor’s name as punishment for professional misconduct [Image 65, 291].

---

## **PART III: PENAL ERASURE AND THE PUNISHMENT SPECTRUM** 🚨

If the disciplinary sub-committee finds an RMP guilty of **"Infamous Conduct"** (unethical/disgraceful acts), the SMC can award the following punishments [Image 65, 291]:

|**Punishment Type**|**Clinical & Legal Description**|
|:--|:--|
|**1. Warning**|A formal, written admonition warning the doctor **not to repeat** the unethical behavior [Image 65, 291]. Used for minor first-time infractions.|
|**2. Temporary Erasure**|The doctor’s license is **suspended for a fixed duration** (e.g., 6 months, 2 years) [Image 65, 291]. They are legally barred from practicing during this period.|
|**3. Permanent Erasure**★ _Professional Death Sentence_|The doctor's name is **permanently struck off** the medical register [Image 65, 291]. They completely lose their registration and are legally prohibited from practicing medicine ever again [Image 65].|

---

## **PART IV: THE LEGAL APPEALS PATHWAY** ⚖️

If a doctor is aggrieved by the disciplinary verdict or penal erasure ordered by the SMC, they have a statutory right to appeal [Image 65, 291]:

```
                     [DISCIPLINARY APPEAL FLOWCHART]
                                    │
                        [State Medical Council (SMC)]
                                    │
                                    ▼ (Appeal within 60 days)
             [Ethics and Medical Registration Board (EMRB)]
                                    │
                                    ▼ (If not acquitted)
                    [National Medical Commission (NMC)]
                                    │
                                    ▼ (Alternative Administrative Route)
                   [Central Health Ministry (CHM)]
```

1. **First Appeal:** Filed directly with the **Ethics and Medical Registration Board (EMRB)** of the NMC [Image 65].
2. **Second Appeal:** If not acquitted by the EMRB, the doctor can file a final appeal to the apex **National Medical Commission (NMC)** itself [Image 65].
3. **Alternative Route:** Under certain provisions, the appeal against penal erasure can also be directed to the **Central Health Ministry (CHM)**.
