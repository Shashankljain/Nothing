[[legal procedure]]
### **Summons (Subpoena)**

---

#### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition** A **summons** (also known as a **subpoena**) is a legal document issued by a court compelling a witness to attend court at a specific time, date, and place to provide testimony or produce documents [1, 63; 13].

**Introduction** Under the new Indian criminal codes, the procedure for issuing summons is governed by the **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023**. It is the primary tool used by the judicial system to ensure that necessary evidence, including expert medical testimony, is presented during a trial [1, 2; 20].

**Importance in Medico-Legal Practice** For a medical practitioner, receiving a summons is a legal obligation. A doctor is usually called as an **Expert Witness** (under **Section 39 BSA**) because of their specialized knowledge used to interpret medical facts for the court [9; 20].

---

#### **Classification of Subpoena**

★ **Important Exam Point: Types of Subpoena**

|**Type of Subpoena**|**Literal Meaning**|**Purpose**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Subpoena Ad Testificandum**|"Under penalty to testify"|Compels the witness to appear in person to give **oral evidence** [1, 63; 13].|
|**Subpoena Duces Tecum**|"Under penalty to bring with you"|Compels the witness to appear and **produce specific documents** or records (e.g., hospital case sheets, X-rays) [1, 63; 13; 14].|

---

#### **Relevant Law (2023 Provisions)**

|**Act**|**Section**|**Medico-Legal Significance**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**BNSS**|**63–71**|Outlines the legal procedure for issuing and serving a summons [1, 2; 13; 20].|
|**BNS**|**208**|**Penalty for Non-attendance:** If a doctor wilfully ignores a summons, they can be punished with up to **6 months imprisonment**, a fine of **₹10,000**, or both [1, 62; 13].|
|**BSA**|**39**|Recognizes the doctor as an **Expert Witness** qualified to give opinions [9; 20].|

#### **Priority of Attendance (Summons from Two Courts)**

★ **High-Yield Exam Point: What to do if summoned by two courts for the same day?**

If a medical practitioner receives two summons for the same date and time, they must follow a specific hierarchy of priority [1, 63; 13]:

1. **Court Level:** A summons from a **higher court** takes precedence over a summons from a lower court (e.g., a High Court summons must be attended before a Sessions Court summons) [1, 63; 13].
2. **Nature of Case:** If the courts are at the same level, a **criminal case** takes precedence over a civil case [1, 63; 13].
3. **Time of Receipt:** If both courts are of the same level and both are criminal (or both civil), the doctor must attend the summons that was **received first** [1, 63; 13].

**Action to be taken:** The doctor must immediately inform the other court (the one they cannot attend) by sending a written communication along with a copy of the priority summons to request an adjournment.

#### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Section 63–71 BNSS:** The updated law for summons.
- ★ **Section 208 BNS:** The "penalty clause" for failing to attend court.
- ★ **Expert Witness:** Defined under **Section 39 BSA**.
- ★ **Conduct Money (Diet Money):** In **civil cases**, the party calling the witness pays for travel and expenses. In **criminal cases**, this is generally not paid, though experts may be compensated by the court.

#### **Viva Questions**

1. **What is a Subpoena Duces Tecum?**
    - _Ans:_ It is a court order specifically requiring a witness to bring and produce documents or physical evidence in court [13; 14].
2. **What happens if you ignore a summons from a Magistrate?**
    - _Ans:_ You can be prosecuted under **Section 208 BNS**, resulting in a fine or imprisonment [1, 62; 13].
3. **If you receive a summons from the High Court and a District Court for 10:00 AM on Monday, which do you attend?**
    - _Ans:_ I will attend the High Court first, as it is the superior court [1, 61, 63; 13].
4. **Is a child required to take an oath when summoned?**
    - _Ans:_ No, a child **below 12 years** of age is exempt from taking an oath in the witness box [1, 62; 13].

#### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **Definition:** Legal command to attend court (BNSS 63–71) [1, 63; 13; 20].
- **Types:** Ad Testificandum (Oral) and Duces Tecum (Documents) [13; 14].
- **Failure to Attend:** Punishable under **Section 208 BNS** [1, 62; 13].
- **Priority Rule:** Higher Court > Criminal Case > First Received [1, 63; 13].
- **Doctor's Status:** Expert Witness under **Section 39 BSA** [9; 20].
