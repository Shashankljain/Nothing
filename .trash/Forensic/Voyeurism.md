[[Sexual offence]]
**Voyeurism** (also known as **Scoptophilia** or being a **"Peeping Tom"**) is a paraphilia characterized by 

“desire to observe unsuspecting people who are naked, undressing, or engaging in sexual activities to obtain erotic excitement and sexual gratification.”

**Introduction**

- **Synonyms:** Scoptophilia, Jags.
- **The Act:** The observer usually watches people through windows, in changing rooms, or in public lavatories.
- **Gender:** This behavior is most commonly seen in males.

**Variations**

- **Troilism:** A specific form where an individual derives sexual gratification by watching their ==own== partner (e.g., wife) being ==seduced== or ==engaged== in sex with another person.
- **Video Voyeurism:** The act of secretly recording or capturing images of someone in an intimate or private state.

---

**2. Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Psychiatric Context:** It is often associated with sociopathic personality disorders.
- **Childhood History:** Psychoanalysts suggest it may stem from a child witnessing their parents engaged in sexual intercourse (the "Primal Scene").
- **Legal Violation:** It is a criminal offense because it involves the violation of a person's privacy without their consent.

★ **Important Exam Point:** Voyeurism involves watching **unsuspecting** persons. If the person being watched is aware and consenting, it does not fall under this criminal definition.

---

**3. Relevant Law: Section 77 BNS, 2023**

★ **Important Exam Point:** Under the new **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**, Voyeurism is governed by **Section 77** (formerly Section 354C of the IPC).

**Essential Ingredients of the Offense:**

1. A man watching or capturing the image of a woman.
2. The woman must be engaged in a **private act**.
3. The circumstances must be such where she would **reasonably expect not to be observed** (e.g., in a bathroom, changing room, or during sexual intercourse).
4. Disseminating such images is also a crime under this section.

---

**4. Punishments under BNS**

The law provides for increasingly severe punishments for repeat offenders:

|**Offense Category**|**Punishment (Section 77 BNS)**|**Bail Status**|
|---|---|---|
|**First Conviction**|Imprisonment for **1 to 3 years** + Fine|**Bailable**|
|**Second/Subsequent Conviction**|Imprisonment for **3 to 7 years** + Fine|**Non-Bailable**|

---

**5. Medico-Legal Examination**

While there are no specific physical findings for a voyeur, the medical officer may be asked to:

- Evaluate the **mental state** of the accused to determine if they suffer from a paraphilic disorder or sociopathic tendencies.
- Assess for any co-existing paraphilias, such as exhibitionism or frotteurism.
