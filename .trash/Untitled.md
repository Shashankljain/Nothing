Q5. What is the significance of dilated veins over the flanks?

Q6. How do you determine the direction of blood flow in dilated abdominal veins?

By the two-finger emptying method.

Two-finger method for determining direction of venous flow

1. Select a visible dilated vein.
2. Place two fingers over a segment of the vein.
3. Press and empty the intervening segment.
4. Keep both fingers pressed.
5. Release one finger.
6. Observe the direction and speed of filling.
7. Empty the segment again.
8. Release the other finger.
9. Compare the filling pattern.

The direction of blood flow can thus be determined.

Q7. In which position should this test be performed?

The professor emphasized that it should be performed in the supine position in this clinical context.

The student initially described examining the patient in the standing position.

The professor corrected this because the rest of the abdominal examination was performed supine and the veins, if visible in that position, can be assessed without unnecessarily changing the patient’s position.

Q8. Who is associated with this test?
