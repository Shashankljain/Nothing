[[NUTRITION]]
### **I. Introduction & Definition** ★ Important Exam Point

- **Definition:** Iodized salt is table salt fortified with trace amounts of iodine, serving as a classic example of **food fortification**.
- **Public Health Purpose:** It is a cost-effective community-level intervention aimed at preventing **Iodine Deficiency Disorders (IDD)** and eliminating **"hidden hunger"** (micronutrient malnutrition).

---

1. **Prevention of Endemic Goitre:** ★ Important Exam Point  
    Prevents compensatory enlargement of the thyroid gland (goitre) caused by environmental iodine deficiency.
2. **Protection of Cognitive & Brain Development:**
    - **First 1,000 Days:** Crucial from conception to 2 years of age to prevent permanent cognitive deficits.
    - **Children & Adolescents:** Prevents mental impairment, reduced learning capacity, and impaired mental development.
3. **Reduction of Adverse Perinatal & Maternal Outcomes:**
    - Lowers the risk of maternal mortality and perinatal complications during pregnancy.
    - Reduces the incidence of **low birth weight (LBW)** infants.
4. **Adult Productivity & Well-being:**
    - Prevents fatigue, mental sluggishness, and reduced adult economic productivity.
    - Reduces cognitive impairment in the elderly.

### **High-Yield Exam Points** ★

- ★ **Food Fortification Vehicle:** Iodized salt is the primary public health tool for universal food fortification against IDD.
- ★ **First 1,000 Days Window:** Iodine is most critical from conception through the first 2 years of life.
- ★ **Double Fortification:** Salt can also be doubly fortified with **iron and iodine** to address both anaemia and IDD simultaneously.

### **Frequently Asked Viva Questions**

- **Q: What is "hidden hunger"?**
    - _A:_ It refers to micronutrient deficiencies (such as iodine, iron, zinc, and vitamin A) where signs may not be immediately visible, but long-term health and cognitive development are severely affected.
- **Q: Why is salt selected as the primary vehicle for iodine fortification?**
    - _A:_ Because salt is consumed in relatively uniform, daily amounts by all socio-economic groups across the population.

### **Common Mistakes Students Make**

- **Confusing Iodine with Fluoride:** Stating that iodized salt prevents dental caries. _(Fluoride prevents dental caries; iodine prevents goitre and mental impairment)._
- **Omitting Cognitive Effects:** Focusing only on goitre and forgetting mental impairment and reduced learning capacity, which carry high scoring weight in exams.
