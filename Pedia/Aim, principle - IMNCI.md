# Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness (IMNCI) — Aim & Principles [⭐ High-Yield]

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness (IMNCI)** is an evidence-based, holistic strategy developed jointly by WHO and UNICEF (adapted by India in 2003) that integrates preventive, promotive, and curative care to reduce under-5 child mortality, morbidity, and disability.

---

### Aim & Core Principles [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Primary Aim**: To drastically reduce under-5 mortality, morbidity, and disability caused by major childhood conditions (pneumonia, diarrhea, neonatal sepsis, birth asphyxia, measles, and malnutrition) while promoting healthy growth and development.
- **Principle 1 — Holistic Symptom-Based Triage**: Evaluates sick children using clinical signs to classify illness into an action-oriented, color-coded matrix (**Pink** = urgent pre-referral treatment and hospital referral; **Yellow** = outpatient treatment at facility; **Green** = home care and counseling).
- **Principle 2 — Age-Specific Stratification**: Categorizes children into two distinct operational tiers with specialized assessment algorithms: young infants (**0–59 days**) and older children (**2–59 months**).
- **Principle 3 — Three Core Components**: Combines improving health worker clinical skills, strengthening health facility infrastructure/drug supplies, and enhancing community/household health practices through Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC) and Home-Based Care for Young Child (HBYC) visits.
- **Principle 4 — Promotive & Preventive Integration**: Directly pairs acute curative care with essential preventive interventions, including exclusive breastfeeding, Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) counseling, routine immunization, and Vitamin A/Zinc supplementation.

### Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: A-I-M-S

- **A** - **Age tiers**: **0–59 days** (young infants) and **2–59 months**
- **I** - **Integrated care**: Curative + preventive + promotive interventions
- **M** - **Mortality reduction**: Targeted at pneumonia, diarrhea, sepsis, and malnutrition
- **S** - **Systematic triage**: **Pink**, **Yellow**, and **Green** color-coded action protocols

---

### Quick Revision

- **Core Goal**: Reduce under-5 mortality and promote healthy child development.
- **Target Age Groups**: **0–59 days** (young infants) and **2–59 months**.
- **Three Pillars**: Worker skills, health system infrastructure, and family/community practices.
- **Color Triage**: **Pink** (Urgent Referral), **Yellow** (Outpatient Care), **Green** (Home Care).
