# Iron Deficiency Anemia (IDA) — Investigations & Treatment [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition

- **Iron Deficiency Anemia (IDA)** is a microcytic hypochromic anemia resulting from inadequate systemic iron stores required for normal hemoglobin synthesis and erythrocyte production.

---

### 2. Investigations / Peripheral Smear Findings [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Microcytic Hypochromic Erythrocytes**: Markedly reduced Mean Corpuscular Volume (MCV <80 fL) and Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin (MCH <27 pg) with central pallor exceeding one-third of the RBC diameter.
- **Anisopoikilocytosis**: Prominent variation in red cell size (increased Red Cell Distribution Width / RDW >15%) and variation in shape.
- **Pencil Cells & Target Cells**: Characteristic elongated, cigar-shaped "pencil cells" (elliptocytes) and thin target cells.
- **Biochemical Findings**: Low serum ferritin (<12–15 μg/L; most specific early indicator of depleted iron stores), low serum iron (<30 μg/dL), elevated Total Iron Binding Capacity (TIBC >350–400 μg/dL), and low transferrin saturation (<15%).

---

### 3. Treatment / Management [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Oral Iron Therapy

- **Elemental Iron Dose**: **3 to 6 mg/kg/day** of elemental oral iron (e.g., ferrous sulfate, ferrous fumarate, or ferrous ascorbate) divided into 2 or 3 daily doses between meals.
- **Duration of Therapy**: Continue oral iron for **2 to 3 months** after Hemoglobin normalizes to fully replenish depleted tissue iron stores.
- **Administration Rule**: Give with Vitamin C / citrus juice to enhance duodenal absorption; avoid co-administration with unboiled cow's milk, calcium, or antacids.

#### B. Supportive Measures

- Timely introduction of iron-rich complementary foods (green leafy vegetables, jaggery, fortified cereals) and deworming with single-dose **Albendazole**.

---

### 4. Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: I-R-O-N

- **I** - **Intestinal (duodenal) absorption**: Enhanced by Vitamin C / ascorbic acid
- **R** - **Reduced MCV (<80 fL) & Ferritin (<12 μg/L)**: Primary diagnostic hallmarks
- **O** - **Oral elemental iron 3–6 mg/kg/day**: Standard therapeutic dosage
- **N** - **Nine to twelve weeks (2–3 months)**: Duration of iron therapy after Hb normalizes

---

### Quick Revision

- **Peripheral Smear**: Microcytic hypochromic RBCs, anisopoikilocytosis, pencil cells, and high RDW.
- **Best Biochemical Marker**: Low serum ferritin (<12–15 μg/L) with elevated TIBC.
- **Oral Dosage**: **3 to 6 mg/kg/day** of elemental iron given between meals.
- **Duration**: Continue for **2 to 3 months post-Hb normalization** to replenish iron stores.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Iron deficiency anemia is the predominant nutritional anemia of childhood, diagnosed on peripheral smear by microcytic hypochromic erythrocytes and pencil cells, and treated with 3 to 6 mg/kg/day of elemental oral iron for 2 to 3 months after hemoglobin normalization to restore tissue iron stores.**
