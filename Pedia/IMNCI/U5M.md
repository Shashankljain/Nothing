# Under-Five Mortality Rate (U5MR) [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Under-Five Mortality Rate (U5MR)** is defined as the probability of a child dying between birth and exactly **5 years of age** (0 to 59 months), expressed per **1000 live births** in a given population during a specific year.
- It is the global benchmark indicator of overall child survival, healthcare delivery quality, maternal health, nutritional status, and social development.

\[\text{Under-Five Mortality Rate (U5MR)} = \frac{\text{Number of deaths of children } <5 \text{ years of age in a given year}}{\text{Total number of live births in the same year}} \times 1000\]

- **Current Epidemiological Indices in India (SRS 2020 Data)**:
    - **Under-5 Mortality Rate (U5MR)**: **32 per 1000 live births**.
    - **Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)**: **28 per 1000 live births**.
    - **Neonatal Mortality Rate (NMR)**: **20 per 1000 live births** (accounts for **63%** of U5MR).
    - **Early Neonatal Mortality Rate (ENMR)**: **15 per 1000 live births** (accounts for **47%** of U5MR).
    - **Late Neonatal Mortality Rate (LNMR)**: **5 per 1000 live births**.

---

### 2. Etiology / Causes / Risk Factors [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Seven Primary Proximate Causes in India

1. **Prematurity & Preterm Complications**: **27%**.
2. **Neonatal Infections / Sepsis**: **13%**.
3. **Birth Asphyxia & Birth Trauma**: **11%**.
4. **Pneumonia (Acute Lower Respiratory Infections)**: **11%**.
5. **Diarrheal Diseases**: **9%**.
6. **Congenital Anomalies**: **9%**.
7. **Injuries & Accidents**: **3%**.

#### B. Underlying / Intermediate Risk Factors

- **Childhood Undernutrition**: Major underlying factor associated with approximately **45% of all under-5 deaths**. Undernutrition impairs immunity, predisposing children to severe infections.
- **Maternal Factors**: Severe maternal anemia (**57%** prevalence), teenage pregnancies, short birth spacing (**<24 months**), and lack of formal maternal education.
- **Socio-environmental Factors**: Poverty, illiteracy, lack of safe drinking water, unhygienic sanitation, and delay in seeking medical care ("Three Delays Model").

---

### 3. Classification / Age-Wise Breakdown [⭐ High-Yield]

Under-5 mortality is divided chronologically into three key periods:

1. **Neonatal Mortality (0 to 28 Days)**:
    - Contributes **63%** of all under-5 deaths in India.
    - **Early Neonatal (0 to 6 Days)**: Contributes **47%** of all under-5 deaths.
    - **Late Neonatal (7 to 28 Days)**: Contributes remaining **16%** of under-5 deaths.
2. **Post-Neonatal Mortality (29 Days to 1 Year)**:
    - Contributes **24%** of under-5 deaths (driven by pneumonia, diarrhea, and severe infections).
3. **Child Mortality (1 Year to 5 Years)**:
    - Contributes **13%** of under-5 deaths (driven by malnutrition, diarrhea, pneumonia, and injuries).

### 5. Clinical Features / Danger Signs in Under-5 Children [⭐ High-Yield]

- **IMNCI General Danger Signs (2 Months to 5 Years)**:
    - Inability to drink or breastfeed.
    - Vomiting everything.
    - Convulsions during current illness.
    - Lethargy or unconsciousness.
- **Respiratory Signs**: Tachypnea (respiratory rate **>50/min** in 2–12 months, **>40/min** in 12–50 months), severe chest indrawing, grunting, and cyanosis (**SpO2 <90%**).
- **Gastrointestinal / Dehydration Signs**: Sunken eyes, skin pinch going back **very slowly (>2 seconds)**, lethargy, and bloody stools (dysentery).
- **Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) Signs**: Visible severe wasting, bilateral pitting pedal edema, MUAC **<11.5 cm**, or weight-for-height **<-3 SDS**.

---

### 6. Diagnosis / Monitoring & Surveillance

- **Sample Registration System (SRS)**: Primary national demographic survey tracking U5MR in India.
- **Child Death Review (CDR) / MPDSR**: Mandatory facility and community verbal autopsies to identify causes and systemic healthcare gaps.
- **Anthropometric Screening**: Routine growth monitoring using WHO growth charts to identify stunting, wasting, and SAM.

1. **Antenatal & Intrapartum Package**:
    - **PMSMA**: Minimum 4 ANC visits, IFA for anemia, screening for pre-eclampsia/GDM.
    - **Institutional Deliveries (89% achieved)**: Promoted via **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** and **LaQshya**labor room guidelines.
    - **Navjat Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (NSSK)**: Training birth attendants in **"Golden Minute"** resuscitation.
2. **Facility & Community Newborn Care**:
    - **Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC)** for low birth weight (**<2000 g**) infants.
    - **Special Newborn Care Units (SNCU)** network for sick/preterm neonates **<1800 g**.
    - **Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC)**: ASHA visits on days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42.
3. **Child Health & Nutritional Initiatives**:
    - **Universal Immunization Program (UIP)**: Free vaccination against 12 VPDs (Rotavirus, PCV, MR, Pentavalent).
    - **Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness (IMNCI)**: Color-coded triage (**Pink, Yellow, Green**) and management of diarrhea (ORS + **Zinc for 14 days**), pneumonia, and sepsis.
    - **Home-Based Care for Young Child (HBYC)**: Extended ASHA visits at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 months to ensure appropriate complementary feeding.
    - **Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK)**: Early screening for **4Ds** (Defects, Deficiencies, Diseases, Delays).

### 9. Complications of High U5MR

- Demographic instability and high fertility rates (families replace lost children).
- Impaired economic productivity and loss of human capital.

---

### 10. Prognosis & National Policy Targets

- **National Health Policy (NHP 2017) Target**: Reduce U5MR to **23 per 1000 live births by 2025**.
- **Sustainable Development Goal (SDG 3.2) Target**: Reduce U5MR to **≤25 per 1000 live births by 2030**.
- **Historical Progress**: India reduced U5MR by **75% between 1990 and 2020** (from 126 down to 32 per 1000 live births).

### Clinical Pearl

> **Over 60% of under-5 child deaths occur during the first 28 days of life.** Therefore, health interventions focused on the neonatal period—such as NSSK "Golden Minute" resuscitation, KMC for low birth weight infants, and SNCU care—yield the highest reduction in overall under-5 mortality.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-up**: Confusing the relative contribution of **neonatal deaths** vs. **post-neonatal deaths** to U5MR.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: **Neonatal deaths (<28 days)** account for **63%** of all under-5 deaths in India, while **post-neonatal and child deaths (28 days to 5 years)** account for the remaining **37%**.
