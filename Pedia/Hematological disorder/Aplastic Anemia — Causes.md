# Aplastic Anemia — Causes [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Aplastic Anemia** is a life-threatening hematological disorder characterized by peripheral **pancytopenia** (anemia, leukopenia/neutropenia, and thrombocytopenia) resulting from severe bone marrow hypocellularity and hematopoietic stem cell failure.

---

### 2. Etiology / Causes / Risk Factors [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Inherited / Constitutional Causes**: Genetic bone marrow failure syndromes, predominantly **Fanconi anemia**, **Diamond-Blackfan anemia**, **Shwachman-Diamond syndrome**, **Kostmann syndrome**, and **Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH)**.
- **Chemical & Physical Insults**: Direct bone marrow injury from exposure to **ionizing radiation**, **benzene**, heavy metals, paint/pesticides, and cytotoxic/alkylating drugs (e.g., cyclophosphamide).
- **Infectious Triggers**: Viral infections causing stem cell destruction or transient aplastic crises, notably **Parvovirus B19** (direct tropism for pronormoblasts) and **Epstein-Barr virus (EBV)**.
- **Acquired Autoimmune (Idiopathic)**: T-cell mediated autoimmune destruction of hematopoietic stem cells (accounts for >70–80% of acquired pediatric cases).

### Clinical Pearl

> **Fanconi anemia is the most common inherited cause of aplastic anemia.** Always look for associated physical anomalies—such as short stature, thumb or radial ray defects, microcephaly, and hyperpigmented café-au-lait spots—in any child presenting with unexplained pancytopenia.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-up**: Confusing **Fanconi Anemia** (inherited aplastic anemia) with **Fanconi Syndrome** (proximal renal tubular defect).
- **Distinguishing Fact**: **Fanconi Anemia** is a primary DNA repair disorder causing bone marrow failure and pancytopenia. **Fanconi Syndrome** is a renal tubular disorder causing urinary loss of bicarbonate, glucose, phosphate, and amino acids.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: Bone marrow failure causing peripheral pancytopenia and hypocellular marrow.
- **Inherited Causes**: Fanconi anemia, Diamond-Blackfan anemia, Shwachman-Diamond syndrome.
- **Acquired Causes**: Ionizing radiation, benzene, chemotherapy, and viral infections (Parvovirus B19, EBV).
- **Primary Mechanism**: T-cell mediated autoimmune destruction of hematopoietic progenitor cells.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Aplastic anemia is a life-threatening bone marrow failure disorder characterized by pancytopenia, arising from inherited stem cell defects like Fanconi anemia or acquired destruction due to radiation, benzene, and post-viral autoimmune failure.**
