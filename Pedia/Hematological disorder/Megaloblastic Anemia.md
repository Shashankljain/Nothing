# Megaloblastic Anemia [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Megaloblastic Anemia** is a macrocytic anemia characterized by defective DNA synthesis and nuclear-cytoplasmic asynchrony in hematopoietic precursors, resulting from **Vitamin B₁₂ (cobalamin)** or **Folate (Vitamin B₉)**deficiency.

---

### 2. Key Clinical & Laboratory Features [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Peripheral Smear Findings**: Presence of macro-ovalocytes (Mean Corpuscular Volume **MCV >100 fL**) and pathognomonic **hypersegmented neutrophils** (defined as ≥5 lobes in at least 5% of neutrophils or a single neutrophil with ≥6 lobes).
- **Clinical Presentation**: Progressive pallor, smooth tongue (glossitis), hyperpigmentation over the knuckles/dorsum of hands, and neurological manifestations in B₁₂ deficiency (developmental delay/regression, hypotonia, fine tremors / infantile tremor syndrome).
- **Biochemical Confirmation**: Low serum Vitamin B₁₂ (**<200 pg/mL**) or low serum/RBC folate (**<3 μg/L**), accompanied by elevated serum homocysteine and methylmalonic acid levels.
- **Treatment Protocol**:
    - **Vitamin B₁₂ Deficiency**: Intramuscular Cyanocobalamin / Hydroxocobalamin at **500–1000 μg/day** or thrice weekly for 2 weeks, followed by weekly doses for 6 weeks, then monthly maintenance.
    - **Folate Deficiency**: Oral Folic Acid **0.5–1 mg/day** for 3–4 weeks _(Note: B₁₂ deficiency must be ruled out before initiating folate monotherapy to prevent precipitating or aggravating irreversible neurological degeneration)_.

---

### 3. Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: M-E-G-A

- **M** - **Macrocytic RBCs (MCV >100 fL)**: Ovalocytes and pancytopenia on peripheral smear
- **E** - **Elevated Homocysteine & Methylmalonic Acid**: Key biochemical diagnostic markers
- **G** - **Granulocyte hypersegmentation**: Neutrophils containing ≥5 nuclear lobes
- **A** - **Administration of IM B₁₂ (500–1000 μg) or Oral Folate (0.5–1 mg/day)**: Specific replacement therapy

---

### Quick Revision

- **Etiology**: Impaired DNA synthesis due to Vitamin B₁₂ or Folate deficiency.
- **Peripheral Smear**: Oval macrocytes (MCV >100 fL) and hypersegmented neutrophils.
- **Diagnostic Trap**: Giving folate alone in uncorrected B₁₂ deficiency worsens subacute combined degeneration of the spinal cord.
- **Therapy**: IM Cyanocobalamin 500–1000 μg for B₁₂ deficiency; Oral Folic Acid 0.5–1 mg/day for folate deficiency.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Megaloblastic anemia is a macrocytic anemia caused by Vitamin B₁₂ or folate deficiency impairing DNA synthesis, characterized on peripheral smear by oval macrocytes and hypersegmented neutrophils, and treated with specific parenteral cobalamin or oral folate replacement.**
