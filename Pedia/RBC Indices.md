# Red Blood Cell (RBC) Indices [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **RBC Indices** are quantitative clinical parameters calculated from hemoglobin, hematocrit (packed cell volume), and total red blood cell count that define the average physical size, hemoglobin content, and hemoglobin concentration of circulating erythrocytes to aid in the morphological classification of anemias.

---

### 2. Key Components & Diagnostic Significance [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Mean Corpuscular Volume (MCV)** [⭐ High-Yield]:
    - **Formula**: MCV (fL) = [Packed Cell Volume (%) × 10] ÷ RBC count (millions/μL)
    - **Normal Range**: **80–100 fL** (age-dependent in pediatric populations).
    - **Clinical Utility**: Categorizes anemia into **microcytic** (<80 fL; e.g., Iron Deficiency Anemia, Thalassemia trait) or **macrocytic** (>100 fL; e.g., Vitamin B₁₂ / Folate deficiency).
- **Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin (MCH)**:
    - **Formula**: MCH (pg) = [Hemoglobin (g/dL) × 10] ÷ RBC count (millions/μL)
    - **Normal Range**: **27–32 pg**.
    - **Clinical Utility**: Reflects average mass of hemoglobin per erythrocyte; reduced in hypochromic anemias.
- **Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration (MCHC)**:
    - **Formula**: MCHC (g/dL) = [Hemoglobin (g/dL) × 100] ÷ Packed Cell Volume (%)
    - **Normal Range**: **32–36 g/dL**.
    - **Clinical Utility**: Measures average concentration of hemoglobin in a given volume of packed red cells; reduced in hypochromia and elevated in Hereditary Spherocytosis.
- **Red Cell Distribution Width (RDW)**:
    - **Normal Range**: **11.5–14.5%**.
    - **Clinical Utility**: Measures degree of variation in RBC size (anisocytosis); elevated (>15%) in Iron Deficiency Anemia, helping differentiate it from Beta-Thalassemia Trait (where RDW is typically normal).

---

### Quick Revision

- **MCV (80–100 fL)**: Classifies anemia as microcytic (<80 fL) or macrocytic (>100 fL).
- **MCH (27–32 pg) & MCHC (32–36 g/dL)**: Assess cellular hemoglobin content and hypochromia.
- **RDW (11.5–14.5%)**: Quantifies anisocytosis; high in Iron Deficiency Anemia.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Red blood cell indices (MCV, MCH, MCHC, and RDW) are automated diagnostic parameters derived from the hemogram that provide essential morphological classification of anemias into microcytic, normocytic, or macrocytic etiologies.**
