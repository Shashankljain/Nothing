# Capillary Refill Time (CRT) / Capillary Filling Time [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Capillary Refill Time (CRT)** is a rapid, non-invasive bedside clinical assessment of microvascular circulation and peripheral tissue perfusion, defined as the time required for skin color to return to normal after blanching pressure is applied and released.

---

### Core Clinical Points [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Standard Assessment Technique**: Measured over the sternum (or forehead/forefinger) by pressing the skin firmly with the ball of a finger or thumb for **5 seconds** to produce blanching, then releasing and measuring the exact time taken for full capillary repinking.
- **Normal Reference Value**: **≤2 to 3 seconds** in healthy neonates, infants, and children.
- **Dehydration Grading**:
    - **No Dehydration**: CRT **2–3 seconds**.
    - **Some Dehydration**: CRT **3–4 seconds**.
    - **Severe Dehydration**: CRT **>4 seconds** (signals severe intravascular depletion and impending hypovolemic collapse).
- **Clinical Significance of Prolonged CRT (>3 Seconds)**: Indicates peripheral vasoconstriction and poor tissue perfusion secondary to **hypovolemic or septic shock**, **hypothermia**, or **cold stress**.
- **Monitoring Resuscitation Response**: Serves as a vital bedside parameter during fluid bolus administration (10–20 mL/kg normal saline); acceleration of capillary refill indicates successful volume restoration and improved cardiac output.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Technique**: Press sternum for **5 seconds**, release, and time color return.
- **Normal Range**: **≤2 to 3 seconds**.
- **Prolonged CRT (>3 s)**: Key sign of poor circulation, shock, or severe hypothermia.
- **Severe Dehydration**: CRT **>4 seconds**.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Capillary refill time (CRT) is a cardinal clinical parameter of microvascular perfusion performed by blanching the sternum for 5 seconds, where a value exceeding 3 seconds signifies impaired tissue perfusion, shock, severe dehydration, or hypothermia.**
