[[Immunisation]]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Haemophilus Influenzae Type B (Hib) Vaccine** is a T-cell dependent **conjugate polysaccharide vaccine**developed to confer active protection against invasive systemic infections caused by _Haemophilus influenzae_ type b.
- Before widespread immunization, Hib was the leading cause of bacterial meningitis, acute epiglottitis, septic arthritis, and severe pneumonia in infants and young children **<5 years of age**.

---

### 2. Etiology & Target Organism Profile [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Causative Pathogen**: _Haemophilus influenzae_ type b, a Gram-negative coccobacillus encapsulated by a virulence-defining **Polyribosylribitol Phosphate (PRP)** capsular polysaccharide.
- **Invasive Target Diseases Prevented by Hib Vaccine**:
    1. **Pyogenic Meningitis**: Predominantly affects infants between **2 and 24 months** of age.
    2. **Acute Epiglottitis**: Medical emergency presenting with the classic "3 Ds" (Dysphagia, Drooling, Dyspnea) and "thumb sign" on lateral neck X-ray (most common etiology in India).
    3. **Severe Pneumonia & Empyema**: Leading contributor to under-5 respiratory mortality.
    4. **Bacteremia, Septic Arthritis, Osteomyelitis, and Cellulitis**.

---

### 3. Classification & Vaccine Chemistry [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Vaccine Category**: **Conjugate Subunit Polysaccharide Vaccine**.
- **Chemistry of Conjugation**:
    - Purified capsular **PRP polysaccharide** is covalently bound to an immunogenic protein carrier (e.g., **Diphtheria Toxoid CRM197**, **Tetanus Toxoid**, or _Neisseria meningitidis_ **Outer Membrane Protein - OMP**).
    - Unconjugated PRP polysaccharide alone is a T-cell independent antigen that fails to induce immune memory or protective antibody titers in children **<2 years** of age due to an immature splenic marginal zone.
    - Conjugation transforms the antigen into a **T-cell dependent antigen**, stimulating CD4⁺ T-helper cells, class switching (IgM → IgG), mucosal immunity, and long-lived B-cell memory even in neonates and young infants.
- **Formulations**:
    - **Monovalent Hib Vaccine**.
    - **Pentavalent Vaccine (DPT + HepB + Hib)**: Standard liquid combination vaccine used in India's Universal Immunization Program (UIP).
    - **Hexavalent Vaccine (DPT + HepB + Hib + IPV)**.

### 5. Clinical Indications & Target Diseases

#### A. Target Clinical Features Prevented by Vaccination

- **Acute Bacterial Meningitis**: High-grade fever, irritability, bulging anterior fontanelle, neck stiffness, Kernig and Brudzinski signs, and GTCS seizures.
- **Acute Epiglottitis**: High fever, stridor, tripod posture (leaning forward), toxic appearance, dysphagia, and drooling of saliva.
- **Severe Pneumonia**: Tachypnea, chest indrawing, grunting, and low oxygen saturation (**SpO2 <90%**).

---

### 6. Immunization Schedule & Dosage Rule [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Universal Immunization Program (UIP - India) Schedule

- Administered as part of the **Pentavalent Vaccine (DPT + HepB + Hib)**.
- **1st Primary Dose**: **6 weeks** of age.
- **2nd Primary Dose**: **10 weeks** of age.
- **3rd Primary Dose**: **14 weeks** of age.

#### B. Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) Recommended Schedule

- **Primary Series**: 3 doses at **6, 10, and 14 weeks**.
- **Booster Dose**: 1 dose at **12–15 months** of age.

#### C. Catch-up Immunization Guidelines (Unvaccinated Children)

- **Age 6–12 months**: 2 primary doses **4 weeks apart** + 1 booster dose at **12–15 months**.
- **Age 12–15 months**: 1 primary dose + 1 booster dose **8 weeks later**.
- **Age 15–59 months**: **1 single dose** is sufficient to confer protective immunity.
- **Age ≥5 years**: Not routinely recommended unless high-risk underlying conditions exist (asplenia, sickle cell disease, HIV, post-chemotherapy, or post-splenectomy).

### 7. Differential Diagnosis / Comparison Table: Unconjugated PRP vs. Conjugate Hib Vaccine

|Feature|**Unconjugated Pure PRP Polysaccharide**|**Conjugate Hib Vaccine (PRP-CRM197 / PRP-TT)**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Immune Type**|**T-cell Independent** antigen|**T-cell Dependent** antigen|
|**Efficacy in Infants <2 Years**|**Poor / Ineffective** (fails to generate response)|**Highly Effective** (robust response from 6 weeks of age)|
|**Immunological Memory**|Absent (no memory cells generated)|Present (long-lived B & T memory lymphocytes)|
|**Booster Response**|No secondary anamnestic rise|Significant antibody rise upon booster exposure|
|**Nasopharyngeal Carriage**|No effect on mucosal carriage|**Significantly reduces carriage** (produces herd immunity)|

---

### 8. Adverse Effects & Safety Profile

- **Common Mild Reactions**: Local injection site redness, mild swelling, localized pain, transient fever, restlessness, and decreased appetite.
- **Severe Reactions**: Anaphylaxis (extremely rare, <1 in 1,000,000 doses).
- **Safety Profile**: Highly safe vaccine with no documented risk of severe neurological sequelae.

---

### 9. Complications of Vaccine Omission

- High risk of fulminant **pyogenic meningitis** with severe long-term complications including **Sensorineural Hearing Loss (SNHL)**, mental retardation, hydrocephalus, and epilepsy.
- Severe respiratory failure and acute upper airway obstruction due to **acute epiglottitis**.

---

### 10. Prognosis & Impact

- **Excellent**: Vaccination achieves **>95% protective antibody levels** after completing the primary 3-dose series.
- **Public Health Impact**: Near-total elimination of invasive Hib meningitis and epiglottitis globally and drastic reduction in infant pneumonia mortality wherever universal Hib coverage is achieved.

### Clinical Pearl

> **Conjugation is the key to protecting infants <2 years.** By covalently coupling the non-immunogenic PRP capsular polysaccharide to a protein carrier (like Tetanus Toxoid or Diphtheria CRM197), the vaccine recruits CD4⁺ T-cells, enabling infants as young as 6 weeks to develop protective anti-PRP IgG antibodies and long-lasting immune memory.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-up**: Confusing _Haemophilus influenzae_ type b (Hib) vaccine with the seasonal **Influenza vaccine**.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: **Hib vaccine** is a **bacterial conjugate vaccine** targeting _Haemophilus influenzae_ type b (preventing bacterial meningitis, epiglottitis, and pneumonia). The **Influenza vaccine** ("flu shot") is a **viral vaccine**targeting seasonal Orthomyxovirus (Influenza A & B viruses).

### Quick Revision

- **Vaccine Type**: **T-cell dependent conjugate subunit vaccine** (PRP + protein carrier).
- **UIP Schedule**: Part of **Pentavalent vaccine** (DPT + HepB + Hib) at **6, 10, 14 weeks**.
- **Dose & Route**: **0.5 mL Intramuscular (IM)** in anterolateral thigh.
- **Primary Indications**: Prevents **Hib meningitis** (2–24 months), **acute epiglottitis**, and **severe pneumonia**.
- **Protective Threshold**: **≥0.15 μg/mL** (short-term) and **≥1.0 μg/mL** (long-term).
- **Cold Chain Rule**: Store at **+2°C to +8°C**; **NEVER freeze**.
