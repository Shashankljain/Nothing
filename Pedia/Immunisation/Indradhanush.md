# Mission Indradhanush [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Mission Indradhanush** is a flagship catch-up immunization campaign launched in December 2014 by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, aimed at rapidly expanding full immunization coverage to **>90%** for children under 2 years of age and pregnant women who have been missed or left out of routine Universal Immunization Program (UIP) rounds.

---

### Core Features & Key Principles [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Target Population**: Focuses on unvaccinated or partially vaccinated children up to 2 years of age (extended up to 5 years in Intensified versions) and pregnant women residing in high-risk, underserved, and hard-to-reach areas.
- **12 Disease Protection**: Provides free vaccination against **12 Vaccine-Preventable Diseases (VPDs)** under the UIP — Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Poliomyelitis, Tuberculosis, Measles, Rubella, Hepatitis B, _Haemophilus influenzae_ type b (Hib), Pneumococcal disease, Rotavirus, and Japanese Encephalitis (in endemic districts).
- **Operational Strategy**: Conducts targeted, intensive 7-day monthly catch-up drives based on rigorous head-count surveys, local microplanning, and door-to-door mobilization by ASHA, ANM, and Anganwadi Workers.
- **Intensified Mission Indradhanush (IMI)**: Successive phases (IMI 1.0 through IMI 5.0) were deployed to rapidly close immunity gaps, integrate digital tracking via the **U-WIN portal**, and achieve national child survival targets.

---

### Clinical Pearl

> **Mission Indradhanush is not a replacement for routine UIP services;** it functions as a periodic, high-intensity catch-up mechanism to track down and vaccinate every zero-dose child in geographically and socially marginalized populations.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-up**: Confusing the number of diseases covered by Mission Indradhanush originally versus today.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: Although originally named "Indradhanush" for 7 core vaccines (Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Polio, TB, Measles, HepB), it now covers **12 Vaccine-Preventable Diseases (VPDs)** with the inclusion of Rubella, Rotavirus, Hib, PCV, and JE in endemic districts.

---

### Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: I-N-D-R-A

- **I** - **Intense catch-up**: Targeted at unimmunized and partially immunized children
- **N** - **Ninety percent (>90%)**: Target national full immunization coverage benchmark
- **D** - **December 2014**: Launched by the MoHFW, Government of India
- **R** - **Reaches high-risk pockets**: Focus on urban slums, brick kilns, and nomadic populations
- **A** - **All 12 UIP vaccines**: Free protection against major pediatric infections

---

### Quick Revision

- **Launch & Objective**: Launched Dec 2014 to achieve **>90% full immunization coverage** across India.
- **Target Age Groups**: Children **<2 years** (extended up to 5 years in IMI) and pregnant women.
- **Disease Coverage**: 12 Vaccine-Preventable Diseases under the UIP.
- **Core Mechanism**: Head-count surveys, local microplanning, and intensive 7-day monthly drives.

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Mission Indradhanush is a flagship national catch-up health initiative launched to accelerate full immunization coverage to over 90% among unvaccinated infants, children, and pregnant women in India.**
