# Complications of Blood Transfusion

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

A **blood transfusion reaction** refers to any adverse or noxious clinical response occurring in a recipient during or following the intravenous administration of whole blood or blood components (packed red blood cells, fresh frozen plasma, platelets, or cryoprecipitate).

---

### Key Complications [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Incompatibility Hemolytic Transfusion Reaction**: Caused by ABO mismatch (clerical error); leads to complement-mediated intravascular hemolysis, lumbar back pain, hemoglobinuria, DIC, acute kidney injury, and shock.
- **Febrile Non-Hemolytic Transfusion Reaction (FNHTR)**: Most common reaction; mediated by host antibodies against donor leukocytes or stored cytokines; prevented using pre-storage **leukodepletion filters**.
- **Transfusion-Related Acute Lung Injury (TRALI)**: Mediated by donor anti-HLA antibodies in plasma (often from multiparous female donors) causing non-cardiogenic capillary leak, pulmonary edema, and ARDS within 6 hours.
- **Transfusion-Associated Circulatory Overload (TACO)**: Hydrostatic fluid volume overload presenting with hypertension, raised JVP, facial puffiness, and cardiogenic pulmonary edema (treated with IV Furosemide).
- **Massive Transfusion Complications**: Transfusion of >10 units in 24 hours causes **dilutional coagulopathy**, **hypothermia (<34 °C)**, **citrate toxicity** (hypocalcemia and hypomagnesemia due to citrate chelation of Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺), and **hyperkalemia** from lysed stored RBCs.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Acute Hemolytic Reaction**: ABO mismatch due to clerical error → intravascular hemolysis, shock, and lumbar back pain.
- **Febrile Non-Hemolytic Reaction**: Most common reaction; prevented using pre-storage leukodepletion filters.
- **TRALI vs TACO**: TRALI = anti-HLA antibody capillary leak (hypotension, ARDS, avoid diuretics); TACO = volume overload (hypertension, raised JVP, treat with Furosemide).
- **Massive Transfusion Hazards**: Dilutional coagulopathy, hypothermia, citrate hypocalcemia, and hyperkalemia.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Complications of blood transfusion comprise acute immune reactions such as ABO-mismatch intravascular hemolysis, FNHTR, and TRALI, alongside non-immune volume overload (TACO) and massive transfusion metabolic derangements (hypocalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypothermia), managed universally by immediately stopping the transfusion._
