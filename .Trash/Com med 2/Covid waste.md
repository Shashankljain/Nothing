[[BMW]]


### - COVID-19 Waste (Definition): 
 
Any waste generated during the diagnosis, treatment, or quarantine of suspected or confirmed COVID-19 patients, which carries a high potential for transmitting the virus via aerosols, respiratory droplets, or direct contact.
- The Principle: "Let the wastes of the sick not contaminate the lives of the healthy".

#### II. Why Special Management is Required (Epidemiological Rationale)

1. High Infectivity: SARS-CoV-2 is highly contagious and spreads primarily via respiratory droplets, aerosols, and direct contact with infected secretions.
2. Fomite Transmission: The virus can survive on common surfaces (plastics, metals, cardboards) for hours to days under varying temperatures and humidity levels.
3. Asymptomatic Spread: Individuals (including waste handlers) may be infectious up to 3 days before developing symptoms, necessitating strict, universal standard precautions.

### III. Step-by-Step Management Protocol of COVID-19 BMW ★ Important Exam Point

The management of COVID-19 waste requires additional, highly careful handling and treatment processes compared to routine bio-medical waste.

#### 1. Point-of-Generation Segregation & Containment

- **Contact Avoidance**: All bins used for COVID-19 waste collection must be equipped with foot-operated lids to prevent hand contact and minimize cross-contamination.
- **Preventing General Waste Contamination**: General solid waste (such as food waste, packaging, and office paper) must be kept strictly separate and must not be contaminated by the patient’s respiratory secretions or body fluids.
- **Secure Tying**: All solid waste bags—both wet and dry—must be tied securely before removal to prevent spillage.

#### 2. The "Double Layering" Rule ★ Important Exam Point

- Precaution: To ensure adequate physical strength and prevent any ==accidental== ==leakage== or tearing, double-layered bags (using two bags instead of one) must be used for the collection of waste from COVID-19 isolation wards, ICU beds, and testing areas.

#### 3. Labeling and Identification

- Every waste bag, container, and trolley carrying COVID-19 waste must be clearly labeled with the words "**==COVID-19 Waste==**" in addition to the standard ==biohazard== and ==cytotoxic== symbols.

#### 4. Decontamination of Waste Bags

- The exterior surface of the double-layered waste bags must be thoroughly sprayed or wiped with a freshly prepared ==**1% Sodium Hypochlorite**== solution before being transported out of the isolation area.

### V. Precautions for Waste Handlers and Sanitation Staff ★

All waste handlers must adhere strictly to infection prevention and control (IPC) protocols:

1. Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):

2. Sanitation workers must wear disposable rubber boots, heavy-duty gloves, and a triple-layer surgical mask.
3. For high-risk, aerosol-generating procedures (e.g., when cleaning spills or dealing with compromised bags), a particulate respirator (N95) and protective eyewear are required.
4. Hand Hygiene:

5. Hands must be washed immediately with soap and water following the removal of each piece of PPE.
6. Washing should follow the standard 11-step handwashing technique and take at least 15–30 seconds.
7. Equipment Decontamination:

- All cleaning equipment (mops, buckets) must be disinfected after use.
- Buckets must be disinfected by soaking in a bleach solution or rinsing in hot water. Cloths must be discarded in appropriate bags.

VI. Special Scenario: COVID-19 Dead Body Management ★

The management of deceased COVID-19 patients is tightly controlled to prevent post-mortem viral transmission:

- Orifice Plugging: All oral and nasal orifices of the dead body must be plugged with cotton to prevent the leakage of infectious body fluids.
- Leak-Proof Containment: The deceased must be placed in a leak-proof plastic body bag.
- Exterior Sanitization: The exterior of the body bag must be thoroughly decontaminated with 1% Sodium Hypochlorite solution.
- Prohibitions: Embalming of the dead body and autopsies must be avoided. Bathing, kissing, or hugging the body is strictly prohibited.
