# Stroke (in Young) — Etiology & Complications

## 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Stroke (Cerebrovascular Accident)** is a rapidly developing focal (or global) neurological deficit of vascular origin lasting **>24 hours** or leading to death [Robbins 5 p. 1254]. In young adults (age <45 years), stroke accounts for ~5–10% of cases and is dominated by non-atherosclerotic etiologies [Davidson 1 p. 502, Robbins 5 p. 1253].

---

## 2. Etiology of Stroke in Young Adults (Age <45 Years) [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Arterial Dissection & Vasculopathy:** Cervico-cranial arterial dissection (internal carotid or vertebral artery tears following trauma/sports) and **Fibromuscular Dysplasia (FMD)** [Davidson 1 p. 40, Robbins 5 p. 1253, 1260].
- **Hypercoagulable States & Thrombophilia:** Antiphospholipid Syndrome (APS), Factor V Leiden mutation, Protein C/S deficiency, oral contraceptive use, and pregnancy/postpartum state [Davidson 1 p. 48, Robbins 1 p. 126, 128].
- **Cardioembolic Sources:** Patent Foramen Ovale (PFO) with paradoxical embolism, Infective Endocarditis, Rheumatic heart disease, and atrial septal defects [Davidson 1 p. 460, Robbins 5 p. 1253].
- **Vasculitis & Drug Abuse:** Primary Angiitis of the CNS, Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE), Takayasu arteritis, and sympathomimetic drug abuse (cocaine, amphetamines) [Robbins 5 p. 1253, 1260].
- **Hematological & Genetic Disorders:** Sickle Cell Disease (vaso-occlusive crisis), CADASIL (_NOTCH3_ gene mutation), and Homocystinuria [Robbins 2 p. 153, Robbins 5 p. 1260].

---

## 3. Three Major Complications [⭐ High-Yield]

1. **Cerebral Edema & Raised ICP (with Herniation):** Cytotoxic and vasogenic brain swelling peaking at 3 to 5 days post-infarct/bleed, leading to transtentorial or tonsillar herniation and fatal brainstem displacement [Robbins 5 p. 1244, 1257].
2. **Aspiration Pneumonia:** Impaired swallowing and gag reflexes secondary to altered sensorium or bulbar palsy, resulting in chemical pneumonitis and polymicrobial lung infection [Robbins 3 p. 183, Davidson 1 p. 61].
3. **Seizures (Early & Post-Stroke Epilepsy):** Acute symptomatic or delayed tonic-clonic seizures triggered by cortical ischemic excitotoxicity or hemorrhagic blood degradation products [Davidson 1 p. 502, Robbins 5 p. 1257].

---

### Quick Revision

- **Stroke in Young Definition:** Vascular neurological event in individuals aged <45 years [Robbins 5 p. 1253, 1254].
- **Key Etiologies:** Carotid/vertebral dissection, antiphospholipid syndrome, PFO/cardioembolism, vasculitis, and illicit drug use [Davidson 1 p. 40, 460, Robbins 1 p. 126].
- **3 Core Complications:** Raised ICP with cerebral herniation, aspiration pneumonia, and post-stroke seizures [Robbins 5 p. 1244, 1257, Robbins 3 p. 183].

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

> **Stroke in young adults (age <45 years) is driven by non-atherosclerotic mechanisms—predominantly arterial dissection, hypercoagulable states, cardioembolism, and vasculitis—carrying major risks of life-threatening cerebral edema, aspiration pneumonia, and post-stroke seizures.**
