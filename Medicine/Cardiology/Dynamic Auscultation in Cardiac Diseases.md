# Dynamic Auscultation in Cardiac Diseases

## 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Dynamic Auscultation** refers to the clinical bedside technique of altering cardiac hemodynamics—using physiological maneuvers such as respiration, postural changes, or isometric exercise—to modify heart rate, venous return (preload), systemic vascular resistance (afterload), or cardiac contractility [Davidson 1 p. 447, 450]. This alters the character, timing, and intensity of heart sounds and murmurs to establish a definitive clinical diagnosis [Davidson 1 p. 450, 526].

---

## 2. Key Physiological Maneuvers & Clinical Effects [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Respiration (Inspiration vs Expiration):**
    
    - _Inspiration:_ Decreases intrathoracic pressure → ↑ venous return to right heart → **increases right-sided murmurs** (e.g., Tricuspid Regurgitation—_Carvallo's Sign_, Tricuspid Stenosis, Pulmonary Stenosis) [Davidson 1 p. 447, 450].
    - _Expiration:_ Increases blood flow to left heart → **increases left-sided murmurs** (e.g., Mitral Regurgitation, Mitral Stenosis, Aortic Stenosis, Aortic Regurgitation) [Davidson 1 p. 447, 450].
- **Valsalva Maneuver (Strain Phase) / Standing Up:**
    
    - _Mechanisms:_ Decreases venous return (preload) and reduces left ventricular end-diastolic volume [Davidson 1 p. 448, 526].
    - _Effects:_ **Increases intensity of murmurs in Hypertrophic Obstructive Cardiomyopathy (HOCM)** and causes earlier mid-systolic click/murmur in **Mitral Valve Prolapse (MVP)**; **decreases intensity of most other murmurs** (e.g., Aortic Stenosis, Mitral Regurgitation) [Davidson 1 p. 450, 526].
- **Squatting / Passive Leg Elevation:**
    
    - _Mechanisms:_ Rapidly increases venous return (preload) and systemic vascular resistance (afterload) [Davidson 1 p. 448, 526].
    - _Effects:_ **Decreases intensity of HOCM murmur** and delays MVP click/murmur toward late systole; **increases intensity of Aortic Stenosis, Mitral Regurgitation, and VSD murmurs** [Davidson 1 p. 450, 526].
- **Sustained Handgrip (Isometric Exercise):**
    
    - _Mechanisms:_ Markedly increases systemic arterial blood pressure and systemic vascular resistance (afterload) [Davidson 1 p. 450].
    - _Effects:_ **Increases intensity of regurgitant murmurs** (Mitral Regurgitation, Aortic Regurgitation, VSD) by resisting forward ejection; **decreases intensity of Aortic Stenosis and HOCM murmurs** [Davidson 1 p. 450, 526].
- **Post-Premature Ventricular Contraction (Post-PVC Pause):**
    
    - _Mechanisms:_ Prolonged diastolic filling period increases LV preload and end-diastolic volume, alongside enhanced post-extrasystolic contractility.
    - _Effects:_ **Increases intensity of ejection systolic murmurs** (Aortic Stenosis, HOCM); **does NOT change the intensity of regurgitant murmurs** (Mitral Regurgitation).

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition:** Modification of heart sounds/murmurs using bedside physical maneuvers altering preload, afterload, or respiration [Davidson 1 p. 447].
- **Respiration Rule:** Inspiration increases right-sided murmurs; expiration increases left-sided murmurs [Davidson 1 p. 447, 450].
- **HOCM & MVP Paradox:** Standing / Valsalva increases HOCM & MVP murmurs; Squatting decreases them [Davidson 1 p. 450, 526].
- **Isometric Handgrip:** Increases afterload → accentuates regurgitant murmurs (MR, AR, VSD) and softens ejection murmurs (AS, HOCM) [Davidson 1 p. 450].

### One-Line Exam Opener

> **Dynamic auscultation is a fundamental bedside clinical technique that utilizes physiological maneuvers to induce transient hemodynamic changes in cardiac loading conditions, thereby distinguishing and localizing organic heart murmurs.**
