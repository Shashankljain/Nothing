# Hepatic Encephalopathy

## 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Hepatic Encephalopathy (HE)** is a spectrum of reversible neuropsychiatric abnormalities characterized by disturbances in consciousness, cognition, behavior, and motor function, occurring in patients with acute liver failure, severe chronic liver disease (cirrhosis), or extensive portosystemic venous shunting [Robbins 3 p. 184, 185].

---
