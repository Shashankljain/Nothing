[[legal procedure]]
Conduct of the Doctor as a Witness

1. Preparation: Review original case notes and verify identification marks before testifying

2. Punctuality: Attend on the scheduled date to avoid penalties under Section 208 BNS 

3. Demeanor: Remain calm, professional, and unbiased. Use simple language for the court's understanding

Veracity: Always tell the truth. Giving false information is Perjury (Section 227 BNS) and constitutes Professional Misconduct (Penal Erasure) 1

5. Leading Questions: Be alert during cross- examination where leading questions (questions suggesting a "Yes" or "No" answer) are allowed to test your accuracy 1

6. Hostile Witness: If a doctor is "not desirous of telling the truth" or favors the opposite side, the court may declare them a Hostile Witness, allowing their own lawyer to cross-examine

them
