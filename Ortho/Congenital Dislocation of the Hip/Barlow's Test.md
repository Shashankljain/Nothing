# Barlow's Test [⭐ High-Yield]

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Barlow's Test** is a provocative clinical screening maneuver performed in neonates and young infants (<3 months) to detect **hip joint instability and dislocatability** in **Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip (DDH / CDH)**.

### 2. Clinical Method & Key Findings [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Target Age Group**: Performed as a standard clinical screening test in infants **< 3 months of age**.
- **Patient Position**: Infant lies supine; knees are fully flexed and hips are flexed to a **90° right angle**.
- **Examiner's Grip**: Examiner grasps the upper thigh with the thumb placed on the inner thigh/femoral head anteriorly and fingers behind over the greater trochanter.
- **Provocative Action**: The hip is gently **adducted** while the examiner exerts a backward/posteriorly directed force along the femoral shaft to push the femoral head out.
- **Positive Result**: An unstable femoral head rolls over the posterior acetabular lip and **slips out of the acetabulum**with a distinct, palpable "clunk" or felt posterior movement.

---

### Clinical Pearl

- **Mnemonic BAD**: **B**arlow **A**dduction **D**islocates — remember that Barlow's test uses gentle **Adduction** to push an unstable femoral head out of the acetabulum.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Confusion**: Swapping the maneuvers and goals of **Barlow's test** vs **Ortolani's test**.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: **Barlow's test** _adducts_ the hip to _dislocate_ an unstable femoral head, whereas **Ortolani's test**_abducts_ the hip to _reduce_ an already dislocated head back into the acetabulum.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Purpose**: Screening test for **hip dislocatability** in DDH in infants < 3 months.
- **Action**: Hip flexed 90° + **Adducted** + Gentle posterior pressure.
- **Positive Sign**: Palpable "clunk" as the femoral head slips out posteriorly.
- **Counterpart**: **Ortolani's test** (abduction reduces the dislocated hip).

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Barlow's test is a provocative clinical screening maneuver for Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip (DDH) in infants under 3 months, wherein gentle posterior pressure applied during hip adduction causes a dislocatable femoral head to slip out of the acetabulum with a palpable clunk.**
