# Management of Placenta Accreta [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks**

---

### Definition

- **Placenta Accreta**: A severe obstetric disorder of placentation within the Placenta Accreta Spectrum (PAS) characterized by abnormal direct attachment or infiltration of chorionic villi into the myometrium without intervening decidua basalis or Nitabuch's fibrinoid layer.

---

### Management [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Multidisciplinary & Tertiary Planning**: Delivery should be scheduled electively at **34°/7 to 35°/7 weeks** of gestation at a tertiary care center with a multidisciplinary team (senior obstetrician, pelvic surgeon, urologist, anesthetist, and blood bank facilities) following antenatal corticosteroid administration.
- **Standard Definitive Treatment (Cesarean Hysterectomy)**:
    - Perform delivery via a classical/vertical uterine incision made **above and away from the placental site**under general anesthesia.
    - **Do NOT attempt manual separation or removal of the placenta**, as this triggers torrential, life-threatening hemorrhage; close the uterine incision and proceed directly to **Cesarean Hysterectomy with the placenta left in situ**.
- **Conservative / Uterine-Sparing Management (If Fertility Desired)**:
    - Deliver the fetus through an incision avoiding the placenta, ligate the umbilical cord close to its insertion, and leave the placenta _in situ_ to autolyze spontaneously under prophylactic antibiotic cover.
    - For partial/focal accreta, partial wedge resection of the placental bed with local oversewing, intrauterine balloon tamponade, or B-Lynch compression sutures may be attempted.
- **Adjuvant Vascular & Surgical Measures**: Preoperative placement of ureteric stents to prevent urinary tract injury, combined with interventional radiology techniques such as internal iliac artery balloon occlusion or uterine artery embolization to minimize surgical blood loss.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Golden Rule**: **Never attempt manual separation of the placenta** in placenta accreta.
- **Standard Treatment**: Elective C-section at **34–35 weeks** followed by **Cesarean Hysterectomy** with placenta left _in situ_.
- **Conservative Option**: Ligate cord close to insertion, leave placenta _in situ_, close hysterotomy, and monitor for autolysis.
- **Incision Choice**: Classical/vertical incision **above the placental edge** to avoid massive fetal/maternal blood loss.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Management of placenta accreta relies on planned multidisciplinary delivery at 34–35 weeks via a C-section with incision away from the placenta, followed by Cesarean hysterectomy without attempting placental separation to prevent catastrophic hemorrhage.**
