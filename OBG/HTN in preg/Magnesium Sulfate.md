[[C18-Hypertensive disorder of preg]]
# Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO₄) [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### 1. Definition & Indications [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Definition & Status**: Magnesium sulfate (MgSO₄·7H₂O) is an essential mineral preparation and the **gold-standard anticonvulsant drug of choice** for controlling eclamptic fits and preventing seizure recurrence in severe pre-eclampsia, imminent eclampsia, and HELLP syndrome.
- **Clinical Indications**:
    1. **Seizure Control & Prophylaxis**: Eclampsia, severe pre-eclampsia, imminent eclampsia, and HELLP syndrome.
    2. **Fetal Neuroprotection**: Administered in preterm labor **<32 weeks of gestation** to reduce the risk of cerebral palsy in neonates.
    3. **Tocolysis**: Inhibits uterine contractions in preterm labor (dose-dependent).

---

### 2. Mechanism of Action [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Central Action**: Acts as a neuroprotector and membrane stabilizer by blocking central **N-methyl-D-aspartate (NMDA) glutamatergic receptors**, decreasing presynaptic release of glutamate, and inhibiting neuronal calcium (Ca²⁺) influx.
- **Neuromuscular Action**: Decreases presynaptic release of **acetylcholine (ACh)** at the neuromuscular junction and reduces motor endplate sensitivity to acetylcholine.
- **Vascular Action**: Induces cerebral and uterine vasodilation, increasing uteroplacental blood flow **without depressing maternal sensorium** or causing neonatal depression.

---

### 3. Dosage Regimens & Monitoring [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Therapeutic Plasma Level**: **4 to 7 mEq/L** (4.8 to 8.4 mg/dL or 2 to 3.5 mmol/L).
- **Pritchard Regimen (IM Regimen — Standard in India)**:
    - **Loading Dose**: **4 g IV** (20% solution slowly over 5–10 min at 1 g/min) + **10 g IM** (50% solution, 5 g deep IM in upper outer quadrant of each buttock). _(Given immediately irrespective of renal status)_.
    - **Maintenance Dose**: **5 g IM** (50% solution) every 4 hours in alternate buttocks, continued until **24 hours after delivery OR 24 hours after the last seizure**, whichever is later.
- **Zuspan Regimen (IV Regimen)**: Loading dose **4 to 6 g IV** slowly over 15–20 min, followed by **1 to 2 g/hour**continuous IV infusion.
- **3 Mandatory Pre-Requisite Clinical Checks Before Each Maintenance Dose** [⭐ High-Yield]:
    1. **Patellar Reflex (Knee Jerk)**: Must be **PRESENT**.
    2. **Respiratory Rate**: Must be **≥12 breaths/minute** (or ≥16/min).
    3. **Urine Output**: Must be **≥30 mL/hour** (or ≥100 mL over 4 hours).

---

### 4. Toxicity Spectrum & Antidote [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Progressive Toxicity Ladder**:
    - **7 to 10 mEq/L (>9 mg/dL)**: **Loss of Deep Tendon Reflexes (Patellar Reflex / Knee Jerk)** — _earliest clinical warning sign_.
    - **≥10 mEq/L (>12 mg/dL)**: **Respiratory Depression**.
    - **≥15 mEq/L (>17 mg/dL)**: **Altered Cardiac Conduction / Heart Block**.
    - **≥25 mEq/L (>30 mg/dL)**: **Cardiac Arrest** in diastole.
- **Specific Antidote**: **Injection Calcium Gluconate 10% — 10 mL IV given slowly over 10 minutes**.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Drug of Choice**: Anticonvulsant for eclampsia, severe pre-eclampsia, imminent eclampsia, and HELLP syndrome.
- **Therapeutic Plasma Level**: **4 to 7 mEq/L** (4.8 to 8.4 mg/dL).
- **Pritchard Regimen**: Loading dose = 4 g IV + 10 g IM; Maintenance = 5 g IM 4-hourly in alternate buttocks for 24 hours postpartum/last fit.
- **3 Clinical Checks**: Knee jerk present, RR ≥12/min, Urine output ≥30 mL/hr.
- **Antidote**: **10% Calcium Gluconate 10 mL IV slowly over 10 minutes**.

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Magnesium sulfate (MgSO₄) is the neuroprotective drug of choice in obstetric practice for the prevention and control of seizures in eclampsia, severe pre-eclampsia, and HELLP syndrome, acting via central NMDA receptor blockade and peripheral motor endplate acetylcholine inhibition without altering maternal consciousness.**
