[[C7-Diagnosis of Pregnancy]]
# Quickening [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Quickening ("Feeling of Life")**: Defined as the first subjective perception of active fetal movements felt by the pregnant mother during pregnancy.

---

### Key Clinical Features [⭐ High-Yield]

1. **Timing of Perception**:
    - **Primigravidae**: Perceived between **18 and 20 weeks** (typically at ~18th week) of gestation.
    - **Multigravidae**: Perceived about **2 weeks earlier**, between **16 and 18 weeks** of gestation.
2. **Diagnostic Classification**: Classified as a **presumptive symptom** of pregnancy in the second trimester.
3. **Calculation of Expected Date of Delivery (EDD)**: Used to estimate gestational age and EDD when the Last Menstrual Period (LMP) is unknown or inaccurate:
    - _Primigravida_: Date of Quickening + **22 weeks** = Estimated EDD.
    - _Multigravida_: Date of Quickening + **24 weeks** = Estimated EDD.
4. **Clinical Nature**: Described initially as a delicate fluttering, tapping, or bubbling sensation in the lower abdomen, gradually progressing to distinct kicks and movements as pregnancy advances.
5. **Clinical Pitfall**: May occasionally be mimicked by excessive intestinal peristalsis, gas movement, or abdominal muscle twitches, and can be falsely reported in pseudocyesis.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: First subjective maternal perception of active fetal movements.
- **Timing**: **18–20 weeks** in primigravidae; **16–18 weeks** in multiparae.
- **EDD Estimation**: Quickening date + 22 weeks (primigravida) or + 24 weeks (multipara).
- **Classification**: Presumptive symptom of second-trimester pregnancy.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Quickening is the first maternal perception of active fetal movements occurring between 18–20 weeks in primigravidae and 16–18 weeks in multiparae, serving as a key presumptive symptom of second-trimester pregnancy and an aid for estimating the expected date of delivery.**
