### HIGH-YIELD TOPICS

1. **Mechanism of Normal Labor (LOA Position)**: Sequential cardinal movements — Engagement, Descent, Flexion, Internal Rotation, Extension, Restitution, External Rotation, and Expulsion of shoulders and trunk.
2. **Active Management of Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL)**: Three steps — Uterotonic administration (Oxytocin 10 IU IM within 1 minute of birth), Controlled Cord Traction (CCT / Brandt-Andrews maneuver) during uterine contraction, and Immediate Uterine Massage.
3. **Placental Separation Signs**: Change in uterine shape (discoid to globular), sudden gush of blood per vaginam, lengthening of the umbilical cord outside the vulva, and elevation of the uterine fundus in the abdomen.
4. **Asynclitism**: Anterior vs. posterior asynclitism (Naegele's vs. Litzmann's obliquity) and its engagement mechanics.
