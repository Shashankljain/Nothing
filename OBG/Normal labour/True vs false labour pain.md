# Differentiation Between True and False Labor Pains [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks**

---

### Definition

- **True Labor Pains**: Painful, rhythmic, and progressive uterine contractions accompanied by progressive effacement and dilatation of the cervix, culminating in the descent and expulsion of the fetus.
- **False Labor Pains (Spurious Labor)**: Non-progressive, dull, and irregular uterine contractions occurring 1–2 weeks before true labor due to stretching of the lower uterine segment and cervix, without producing cervical effacement or dilatation.

---

### Key Differentiating Features [⭐ High-Yield]

|Parameter / Feature|True Labor Pains|False Labor Pains (Spurious Labor)|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Pain Character & Location**|Rhythmic, painful, originating in the lower back and radiating to the lower abdomen and thighs.|Dull, spasmodic, confined strictly to the lower abdomen and groin.|
|**Contraction Pattern**|Frequency, intensity, and duration increase progressively over time.|Irregular, infrequent, non-progressive, and static over time.|
|**Cervical Changes**|**Progressive effacement and dilatation of the cervix**.|**No progressive cervical effacement or dilatation**.|
|**Show (Mucus Plug)**|**Present** (expulsion of blood-stained cervical mucus plug).|**Absent**.|
|**Descent & Bag of Waters**|Progressive descent of presenting part; bag of forewaters becomes tense during contractions.|No descent of presenting part; bag of forewaters does not form.|
|**Effect of Sedation / Rest**|Unaffected by sedatives or rest; contractions continue to progress.|**Characteristically relieved by rest, warm baths, or mild analgesics/sedatives**.|

---

### Quick Revision

- **Cervical Impact**: True labor causes **progressive cervical effacement & dilatation**; false labor produces **no cervical change**.
- **Show**: Blood-tinged mucoid 'show' is **present in true labor** and **absent in false labor**.
- **Pain Progression**: True labor pain radiates from **back to abdomen/thighs** with increasing frequency/intensity; false labor remains **dull & confined to lower abdomen**.
- **Analgesic Response**: False labor pain is **easily relieved by rest or sedatives**; true labor is unaffected.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **True labor is distinguished from false labor (spurious labor) by the presence of regular, progressive uterine contractions that increase in frequency and intensity, accompanied by 'show' and progressive effacement and dilatation of the cervix.**
