[[C13- Normal labour]]
#  [⭐ High-Yield]

### Definition

- **Placental Separation**: The physiological process during the third stage of labor where marked myometrial contraction and retraction reduce the placental site area, shearing the inelastic placenta through the deep spongy layer (_zona spongiosa_) of the decidua basalis.

---

### Signs of Placental Separation [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Alteration in Uterine Shape and Height**: The uterus changes from a flattened discoid shape to a firm, hard, **globular** shape. The **fundal height rises** slightly above the umbilicus because the detached placenta descends into the lower uterine segment, pushing the contracted uterine body upward.
- **Permanent Lengthening of the Umbilical Cord**: The visible umbilical cord lengthens outside the vulva. Pushing the uterus upward via suprapubic pressure does not cause the cord to recede back into the vagina (**permanent cord lengthening**).
- **Sudden Gush of Blood**: A sudden, brief **gush of dark vaginal bleeding** occurs as the retroplacental hematoma escapes following detachment.
- **Suprapubic Bulging**: A distinct fullness or bulge appears in the suprapubic region due to distension of the lower uterine segment by the separated placenta.
- **Vaginal Examination Finding (Surest Sign)**: Direct palpation of the detached placenta resting in the lower uterine segment or upper vagina on pelvic (PV) examination.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Uterine Shape & Height**: Uterus becomes **globular, hard, and ballottable**; fundal height rises slightly.
- **Cord Sign**: **Permanent lengthening of the cord** outside the vulva (no recession on suprapubic pressure).
- **Bleeding**: Sudden **gush of dark vaginal blood** (retroplacental hematoma release).
- **Surest Sign**: Direct palpation of the separated placenta in the lower uterine segment or vagina.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **The primary clinical signs confirming complete placental separation in the third stage of labor include a change in uterine contour from discoid to globular, elevation of fundal height, a sudden gush of vaginal blood, and permanent lengthening of the umbilical cord.**
