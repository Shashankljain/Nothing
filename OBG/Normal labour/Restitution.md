# Restitution (Mechanism of Labor) [⭐ High-Yield]

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Restitution** is the visible, passive movement of the fetal head outside the vulva resulting from the spontaneous untwisting of the fetal neck that was sustained during internal rotation.

---

### Key Features [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Mechanism & Cause**: During internal rotation inside the pelvis, the fetal head turns through 1/8th of a circle (45°) while the shoulders rotate by only 22.5°, causing the neck to sustain a 45° torsion. Once the head is delivered by extension, the neck passively untwists to realign at right angles with the bisacromial shoulder line.
- **Degree & Direction**: The head rotates through **1/8th of a circle (45°)** in the direction **opposite** to that of internal rotation.
- **Resulting Position**: The occiput points directly toward the maternal thigh of the corresponding side to which it originally lay at the time of engagement (e.g., toward the left thigh in a Left Occipito-Anterior / L.O.A. position).
- **Distinction from External Rotation**: Restitution is a pure neck-untwisting movement of the head, whereas **external rotation** is a subsequent 45° movement caused by the internal rotation of the fetal shoulders inside the pelvic cavity.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-Up**: Confusing **Restitution** with **External Rotation**.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: **Restitution** is the immediate, passive 45° untwisting of the _fetal neck_ outside the vulva in the direction opposite to internal rotation. **External Rotation** is the subsequent 45° movement of the head in the _same direction as restitution_, reflecting the internal rotation of the _fetal shoulders_ inside the pelvis.

---

### Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: U-N-T-W-I-S-T

- **U**: **Untwisting of the neck** sustained during internal rotation
- **N**: **Neck torsion of 45°** resolved
- **T**: **Thigh alignment** (occiput points toward the maternal thigh of the original position)
- **W**: **Way opposite** to the direction of internal rotation
- **I**: **1/8th of a circle (45°)** rotation
- **S**: **Spontaneous & passive** movement
- **T**: **Triggers next step** (precedes external rotation)

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: Passive 45° untwisting of the fetal neck outside the vulva following delivery of the head by extension.
- **Degree & Direction**: Rotates **1/8th of a circle (45°)** in the **opposite direction** of internal rotation.
- **Anatomical Alignment**: Occiput points toward the maternal thigh corresponding to its original side at engagement.
- **Key Mechanism**: Relieves internal neck torsion before the shoulders rotate internally.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Restitution is the visible, passive 45° rotation of the born fetal head in the direction opposite to internal rotation that relieves neck torsion and aligns the occiput with the maternal thigh corresponding to its original engagement side.**
