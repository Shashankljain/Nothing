# Brandt-Andrews Technique (Controlled Cord Traction - CCT) [⭐ High-Yield]

### Definition

The **Brandt-Andrews technique** (Modified Controlled Cord Traction) is an assisted method of delivering the placenta during the third stage of labor by applying steady, gentle traction on the umbilical cord in a downward and backward direction while simultaneously applying counter-traction upwards on the lower uterine segment with the abdominal hand.

---

### Key Features & Procedure [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Mandatory Pre-requisite**: Adopted **ONLY when the uterus is hard and contracted** following the administration of a parenteral uterotonic (Inj. Oxytocin 10 IU IM) in Active Management of Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL).
- **Abdominal Hand (Suprapubic Counter-Traction)**: The palmar surface of the fingers of the non-dominant hand is placed on the lower abdomen above the symphysis pubis at the junction of the upper and lower uterine segments, pushing the body of the uterus **upward and backward** toward the umbilicus.
- **Vaginal Hand (Cord Traction)**: The dominant hand holds the clamped umbilical cord at the vulval introitus, exerting steady, gentle tension in a **downward and backward direction** along the axis of the pelvic inlet.
- **Prevention of Acute Uterine Inversion**: The upward counter-traction exerted by the abdominal hand elevates the uterine body away from the pelvic brim, preventing accidental acute uterine inversion during cord traction.
- **Placental Expulsion & Membrane Stripping**: As the placenta emerges through the introitus, it is held in both hands and twisted slowly in a cupped fashion to strip and peel off the chorioamniotic membranes intact without leaving retained fragments.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Core Action**: Downward cord traction paired with upward suprapubic counter-traction on the uterus.
- **Key Safety Rule**: Perform **ONLY when the uterus is hard & contracted** to prevent cord avulsion or inversion.
- **Abdominal Hand**: Pushes lower uterine segment **upward and backward** toward the umbilicus.
- **Primary Benefit**: Prevents acute uterine inversion and shortens third-stage delivery time.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **The Brandt-Andrews technique is the standardized method of controlled cord traction (CCT) employed in AMTSL where downward traction on the umbilical cord is combined with upward suprapubic counter-elevation of a contracted uterus to safely deliver the placenta and prevent acute uterine inversion.**
