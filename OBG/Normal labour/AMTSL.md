# Active Management of Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL) [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **5 Marks** (Definition + Objectives, Protocol Flowchart, Comparison, Dosage Table, Complications, Diagram Guide, and Memory Palace)

---

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Third Stage of Labor**: The period of labor commencing immediately after complete expulsion of the fetus and terminating with the expulsion of the placenta and membranes (afterbirths).
- **Active Management of Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL)**: A prophylactic, evidence-based obstetric protocol aimed at exciting powerful myometrial contractions within **1 minute of fetal delivery** through parenteral uterotonics, delayed cord clamping, controlled cord traction (Brandt-Andrews technique), and immediate fundal massage.
- **Duration Impact**: Reduces the average duration of the third stage from 15 minutes (in expectant management) down to **5 minutes**.

---

### 2. Objectives and Advantages [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Primary Objective**: Minimizes maternal morbidity and mortality by preventing atonic **Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH)**.
- **Key Clinical Advantages**:
    1. **60% Reduction in PPH Rate**: Decreases the incidence of primary PPH from 3.0% to 1.5%.
    2. **Minimal Blood Loss**: Reduces average third-stage blood loss to approximately **1/5th**.
    3. **Shortened Duration**: Halves the third-stage duration from 15 minutes to 5 minutes.
    4. **Reduced Transfusion Needs**: Drops maternal blood transfusion requirements from 40% to 15%.
    5. **Decreased Therapeutic Oxytocics**: Reduces the need for secondary therapeutic uterotonics by 50%.
    6. **Prevention of Maternal Anemia**: Preserves hemoglobin and hematocrit levels postpartum.

#### Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Uterotonic Administration (Within 1 Minute)**:
    - Perform an immediate abdominal palpation to rule out an undiagnosed second twin.
    - Administer **Injection Oxytocin 10 IU IM** (preferred choice) into the maternal thigh/deltoid within **1 minute of birth**.
    - _Alternative Regimens (if Oxytocin is unavailable)_: Oral/rectal Misoprostol **600 µg** or Inj. Methergine **0.2 mg IM** (provided maternal blood pressure is normal).
2. **Delayed Cord Clamping (DCC)**:
    - Delay umbilical cord clamping for **1 to 3 minutes post-delivery** (not earlier than 1 minute).
    - _Benefits_: Improves infant iron stores, prevents neonatal anemia, and enhances developmental outcomes.
3. **Controlled Cord Traction (CCT / Brandt-Andrews Technique)**:
    - Adopted **only when the uterus is hard and contracted** following the uterotonic response.
    - Place the palmar surface of the fingers of the non-dominant hand on the lower abdomen above the symphysis pubis at the junction of the upper and lower uterine segments.
    - Exert upward and backward counter-traction (elevation) on the body of the uterus toward the umbilicus.
    - Concurrently, the dominant hand holds the clamped cord at the vulva, exerting steady, gentle downward and backward traction along the pelvic axis.
    - As the placenta emerges through the introitus, hold it in both hands and twist slowly in a cupped fashion to strip the membranes off intact.
4. **Immediate Uterine Massage & Postpartum Vigilance**:
    - Massage the uterine fundus immediately following placental delivery to stimulate persistent myometrial retraction.
    - **Fourth Stage Monitoring**: Palpate the uterine fundus **every 15 minutes for 2 hours** post-delivery to verify that the uterus remains hard and well-retracted, and continuously monitor blood loss and vitals.
    - Meticulously inspect the maternal surface (cotyledons) and fetal surface (membranes) for completeness.

### 6. Complications, Disadvantages, and Pitfalls

- **Slightly Increased Retained Placenta Risk**: AMTSL carries a 1–2% incidence of retained placenta and a corresponding minor increase in manual removal of placenta.
- **Accidental Administration in Undiagnosed Twins**: Giving parenteral uterotonics prior to the birth of an undiagnosed second twin causes uterine tetany and immediate, fatal fetal asphyxia.
- **Risk of Uterine Inversion**: Exerting CCT on an uncontracted, soft uterus or failing to apply suprapubic counter-elevation can pull the uterine fundus downward, causing acute uterine inversion.
- **Avulsion of Umbilical Cord**: Excessive or hasty traction on a thin umbilical cord can snap the cord, requiring manual removal of the placenta.

---

### 7. Clinical Pearl & Exam Trap

### Clinical Pearl

- **The Living Ligatures**: Physiological hemostasis in the third stage is achieved by myometrial contraction and permanent retraction. The interlacing "Figure-of-8" muscle fibers in the middle layer squeeze and physically clamp the maternal blood vessels passing through them, functioning as **"living ligatures"**.

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-Up**: Administering **Inj. Methergine or Oxytocin** immediately upon delivery of the anterior shoulder without verifying twin status.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: Uterotonic administration in AMTSL must occur **ONLY AFTER ruling out an undiagnosed second twin** via abdominal palpation. Administering oxytocics before the second twin is born triggers uterine tetany and fetal death.

- **Core Triad**: Parenteral uterotonic within 1 min, Delayed Cord Clamping (1–3 mins), and Controlled Cord Traction (Brandt-Andrews) with immediate fundal massage.
- **First-Line Drug**: **Inj. Oxytocin 10 IU IM** (safe in hypertension; reduces PPH by 60%).
- **CCT Rule**: Perform CCT **only when the uterus is hard and contracted**, while applying suprapubic counter-elevation to avoid inversion.
- **Mandatory Pre-requisite**: **Rule out an undiagnosed second twin** before giving uterotonics.
- **Efficacy**: Shortens third stage to **5 minutes** and drops PPH incidence to **1.5%**.
