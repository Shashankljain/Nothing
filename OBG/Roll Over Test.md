[[C18-Hypertensive disorder of preg]]
# Roll Over Test [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition

- **Roll Over Test (Supine Pressor Test)**: A clinical screening test performed between **28 and 32 weeks of gestation**to predict the subsequent development of pregnancy-induced hypertension (pre-eclampsia) based on position-dependent blood pressure changes.

---

### Key Clinical Features & Protocol

- **Timing of Test**: Conducted in the antenatal clinic between **28 and 32 weeks** of pregnancy.
- **Procedure**: Blood pressure is measured first with the patient resting on her side (**lateral recumbent position**), and then measured again after the patient rolls onto her back (**supine position**).
- **Positive Test Criteria**: An increase of **≥20 mmHg in diastolic blood pressure** on changing position from side to back indicates a positive test.
- **Predictive Value**: Approximately **33%** of women with a positive roll over test subsequently develop hypertension; a negative test is clinically valuable for ruling out high risk.
- **Current Status**: Classified as an outdated clinical predictor, largely replaced by modern biochemical markers (sFlt-1, PlGF) and uterine artery Doppler velocimetry.
