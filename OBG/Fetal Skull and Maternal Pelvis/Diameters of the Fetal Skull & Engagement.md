# Diameters of the Fetal Skull & Engagement of the Fetal Head [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **5 Marks / Long Answer Depth**

---

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Diameters of the Fetal Skull

- **Fetal Skull Diameters**: Linear dimensions measured between specific anatomical landmarks on the fetal skull vault, classified into **anteroposterior (longitudinal)** and **transverse** diameters. The engaging diameter depends directly on the attitude (degree of flexion or extension) of the fetal head during labor.

#### B. Engagement of the Fetal Head

- **Definition**: Engagement is defined as the passage of the greatest horizontal/transverse plane of the fetal head (**biparietal diameter, 9.5 cm**, in vertex presentation) through the plane of the pelvic inlet (brim).
- **Clinical Criteria**:
    1. **Abdominal Assessment**: The head is engaged when **2/5ths or less** of the fetal head is palpable above the superior border of the symphysis pubis (Rule of Fifths).
    2. **Vaginal Assessment**: The lowest bony portion of the unmolded fetal head reaches or lies below the level of the **ischial spines (Station 0)**.

---

### 2. Classification of Fetal Skull Diameters [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Anteroposterior (AP) Diameters [⭐ High-Yield]

|Diameter|Anatomical Extent|Measurement|Attitude & Presentation|
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|**Suboccipitobregmatic**|Nape of neck (subocciput) to center of bregma.|**9.5 cm (3¾")**.|**Complete Flexion → Vertex Presentation**(most favorable engaging diameter).|
|**Suboccipitofrontal**|Nape of neck to center of sinciput / anterior fontanelle.|**10.0 cm (4")**.|**Incomplete Flexion → Vertex Presentation**.|
|**Occipitofrontal**|Occipital eminence to glabella (root of nose).|**11.5 cm (4½")**.|**Marked Deflexion → Vertex / Occiput Posterior**.|
|**Mentovertical**|Midpoint of chin (mentum) to highest point on sagittal suture.|**14.0 cm (5½")**.|**Partial Extension → Brow Presentation**(largest AP diameter; non-deliverable vaginally at term).|
|**Submentovertical**|Junction of floor of mouth and neck to highest point on sagittal suture.|**11.5 cm (4½")**.|**Incomplete Extension → Face Presentation**.|
|**Submentobregmatic**|Junction of floor of mouth and neck to center of bregma.|**9.5 cm (3¾")**.|**Complete Extension → Face Presentation**.|

#### B. Transverse Diameters [⭐ High-Yield]

1. **Biparietal Diameter (BPD) (9.5 cm / 3¾")**: Distance between the two parietal eminences. Represents the greatest transverse diameter that engages in cephalic presentations.
2. **Super-subparietal Diameter (8.5 cm / 3½")**: Extends from below one parietal eminence to above the opposite parietal eminence; passes the brim during **asynclitism**.
3. **Bitemporal Diameter (8.0 cm / 3¼")**: Distance between the anteroinferior ends of the coronal sutures.
4. **Bimastoid Diameter (7.5 cm / 3")**: Distance between the tips of the mastoid processes (incompressible diameter).

### 3. Etiology & Causes of Non-Engagement at 38 Weeks [⭐ High-Yield]

Engagement normally occurs between **38 and 42 weeks** in primigravidae (or during early labor) and late in the first stage of labor in multigravidae. Failure of engagement in a primigravida at 38 weeks requires evaluation for:

1. **Deflexed Fetal Head**: Brings larger AP diameters (occipitofrontal 11.5 cm) across the brim.
2. **Cephalopelvic Disproportion (CPD)**: Contracted pelvic brim or fetal macrosomia.
3. **Malpresentations & Malpositions**: Occipitoposterior position, brow, or face presentation.
4. **Placenta Previa & Pelvic Tumors**: Fibroids or ovarian masses occupying the lower uterine segment.
5. **Polyhydramnios & High Pelvic Inclination**.
6. **Idiopathic / Functional Causes (20%)**: Poor formation or yielding of the lower uterine segment.

### 5. Clinical Features & Diagnosis of Engagement [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Abdominal Examination [⭐ High-Yield]

1. **Rule of Fifths (Law of 5ths)**:
    - **Unengaged**: 5/5ths, 4/5ths, or 3/5ths of the head is felt above the pubic symphysis.
    - **Engaged**: **2/5ths, 1/5th, or 0/5ths** of the head is palpable abdominally.
2. **First Pelvic Grip (Convergence vs Divergence)**:
    - **Unengaged**: Examining fingers **converge** below the head.
    - **Engaged**: Examining fingers **diverge** when pressed downward into the lower abdomen.

#### B. Vaginal Examination [⭐ High-Yield]

1. **Ischial Spine Landmark**: Lowest bony portion of the unmolded head is felt at or below the level of the **ischial spines (Station 0)**.
2. The distance from the pelvic inlet brim to the ischial spines is **5 cm**, whereas the distance from the biparietal plane of an unmolded head to its vertex is **3 cm**.

### 6. Differential Diagnosis [⭐ High-Yield]

#### Comparison: Engaged Head vs Fixed Head

|Diagnostic Parameter|Engaged Head [⭐ High-Yield]|Fixed Head [⭐ High-Yield]|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Anatomical Definition**|Greatest transverse diameter (biparietal 9.5 cm) **has passed** the pelvic brim.|Head is immotile at the brim, but biparietal diameter **has NOT passed** the brim.|
|**Egg-Cup Analogy**|Egg has passed through the rim of the cup.|Egg rests firmly on top of the cup rim without passing through.|
|**Abdominal Palpation**|**≤2/5ths** head palpable; fingers **diverge**.|**>2/5ths** head palpable; fingers may not diverge.|
|**Vaginal Station**|Vertex at or below **Station 0** (ischial spines).|Vertex **above Station 0** (-1 to -2 cm).|
|**Clinical Significance**|**Excludes inlet disproportion**.|Disproportion at the brim **cannot be excluded**.|

### 7. Clinical Pearl & Exam Trap

### Clinical Pearl

- **Engagement as the Best Pelvimeter**: Engagement of the fetal head is the single most reliable clinical proof that the pelvic inlet is adequate for that specific baby — "The fetal head is the best pelvimeter".

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-Up**: Confusing a "Fixed Head" or a head with a large "Caput Succedaneum" for an engaged head.
- **Distinguishing Fact**:
    - A **fixed head** is immobile but its widest diameter (BPD) remains above the brim.
    - **Caput succedaneum** is soft tissue scalp edema that can reach Station 0 even when the underlying bony skull remains unengaged above the inlet. Always assess the _bony_ landmark, not soft tissue.

### Quick Revision

- **Engagement Definition**: Biparietal diameter (9.5 cm) passes the plane of the pelvic inlet.
- **Abdominal Criteria**: **≤2/5ths** head palpable; examining fingers **diverge**.
- **Vaginal Criteria**: Lowest bony part of head reaches **Station 0** (ischial spines).
- **Favorable Engaging Diameter**: Suboccipitobregmatic (**9.5 cm**) in complete flexion.
- **Non-Deliverable Diameter**: Mentovertical (**14.0 cm**) in brow presentation.
- **Transverse Diameters**: Biparietal (9.5 cm), Super-subparietal (8.5 cm), Bitemporal (8.0 cm), Bimastoid (7.5 cm).
