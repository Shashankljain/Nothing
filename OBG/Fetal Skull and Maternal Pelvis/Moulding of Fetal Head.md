 # Moulding of Fetal Head: Degrees & Importance [⭐ High-Yield]
 
### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition

- **Moulding (Molding)**: Defined as the alteration in the shape of the forecoming fetal head produced by the compression of the engaging diameter with compensatory elongation in the diameter at right angles to it, as it negotiates the resistant birth canal during labor.

---

### Key Clinical Features [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Mechanism**: Under pressure from uterine contractions, the unossified cranial vault flat bones slide at the suture lines; parietal bones overlap adjacent occipital and frontal bones (in LOA position, the right parietal bone overrides the left).
- **Grading of Moulding (Partograph Criteria)**:
    - **Grade 0**: Sutures apposed or separated without touching.
    - **Grade 1 (+)**: Bones touching each other at suture lines, but no overlapping.
    - **Grade 2 (++)**: Bones overlapping at suture lines, but **easily reducible** / separable with digital pressure.
    - **Grade 3 (+++)**: Bones overlapping severely and **fixed / non-reducible** (pathological hallmark of severe Cephalopelvic Disproportion [CPD] or obstructed labor).
- **Physiological Importance**: Mild to moderate moulding (Grades 1 & 2) is a beneficial mechanism that reduces the engaging diameter by up to 4 mm without altering overall brain volume, facilitating smooth passage through the pelvis.
- **Pathological Hazards**: Grade 3 fixed moulding causes extreme elevation of falx cerebri and tentorial shear stress, leading to **tentorium cerebelli tears, subdural hemorrhage, and severe neonatal brain injury**.
- **Clinical & Diagnostic Role**: Normal moulding subsides spontaneously within a few hours to days after birth; the specific shape of moulding provides clinical information regarding the position and attitude occupied by the fetal head in the pelvis.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: Compression-induced alteration in fetal skull shape along the engaging diameter during labor.
- **Grades**: Grade 1 = Touching; Grade 2 = Overlapping but reducible; Grade 3 = Fixed / non-reducible (CPD hallmark).
- **Physiological Role**: Beneficial adaptation enabling head compression (reduces diameter by ~4 mm).
- **Complications**: Severe Grade 3 moulding risks tentorium cerebelli tears and intracranial subdural hemorrhage.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Moulding is the physiological adaptation of the fetal skull vault during labor wherein cranial bones overlap at suture lines to reduce engaging diameters, graded from 1 (touching) to 3 (fixed overlapping, indicating severe cephalopelvic disproportion and risk of tentorial tear).**
