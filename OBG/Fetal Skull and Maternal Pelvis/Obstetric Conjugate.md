[[C9 -Fetal Skull and Maternal Pelvis]]
# Obstetric Conjugate [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Obstetric Conjugate**: Defined as the distance extending from the **midpoint of the sacral promontory** to the **most prominent bony projection in the midline on the inner surface of the symphysis pubis** (located slightly below its upper border).

---

### Key Clinical Features [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Normal Measurement**: Measures **10 cm (4")** in a normal female (gynecoid) pelvis.
- **Obstetric Importance**: Represents the **shortest anteroposterior diameter** of the pelvic inlet through which the fetal head must pass during labor.
- **Indirect Calculation**: Cannot be measured directly on clinical per-vaginal examination; it is inferred indirectly by deducting **1.5 to 2 cm** from the **Diagonal Conjugate** (Obstetric Conjugate = Diagonal Conjugate - 1.5 to 2 cm), depending on the height, thickness, and inclination of the symphysis pubis.
- **Inlet Contraction Threshold**: Pelvic inlet contraction is diagnosed when the obstetric conjugate is **<10 cm**.
- **Severe Disproportion Threshold**: An obstetric conjugate **<7.5 cm (3")** indicates severe cephalopelvic disproportion (CPD), where vaginal delivery of an average-size baby is impossible, requiring an elective Cesarean section.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Obstetric Conjugate**: Distance from sacral promontory to inner midpoint of symphysis pubis = **10 cm**.
- **Obstetric Role**: Shortest anteroposterior diameter of the pelvic inlet.
- **Formula**: Obstetric Conjugate = Diagonal Conjugate (12 cm) - 1.5 to 2 cm.
- **Inlet Contraction**: Defined as Obstetric Conjugate **<10 cm** (Severe CPD when **<7.5 cm**).

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **The obstetric conjugate is the shortest 10 cm anteroposterior diameter of the pelvic inlet extending from the midpoint of the sacral promontory to the most prominent bony projection on the inner surface of the symphysis pubis, calculated indirectly by subtracting 1.5 to 2 cm from the diagonal conjugate.**
