[[C9 -Fetal Skull and Maternal Pelvis]]
# Anatomical Conjugate [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Anatomical Conjugate (True Conjugate / Conjugate Vera)**: Defined as the anteroposterior diameter of the pelvic inlet, extending from the **midpoint of the sacral promontory** to the **inner margin of the upper border of the symphysis pubis**.

---

### Key Clinical Features [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Normal Measurement**: Measures **11 cm (4¼")** in a normal female (gynecoid) pelvis.
- **Indirect Calculation**: Cannot be measured directly on clinical per-vaginal examination; it is inferred indirectly by deducting **1.2 cm** from the **Diagonal Conjugate** (Anatomical Conjugate = Diagonal Conjugate - 1.2 cm).
- **Comparison with Inlet Conjugates**:
    - **Obstetric Conjugate (10 cm)**: Distance from sacral promontory to the most prominent bony projection on the inner surface of symphysis pubis; represents the **shortest AP diameter** through which the fetal head must pass.
    - **Diagonal Conjugate (12 cm)**: Distance from sacral promontory to the lower border of symphysis pubis; the only inlet AP diameter clinically measurable on digital per-vaginal examination.
- **Obstetric Significance**: A reduction in the true conjugate indicates **pelvic inlet contraction** (inlet contraction defined when obstetric conjugate is <10 cm or diagonal conjugate is <11 cm), predisposing to non-engagement and Cephalopelvic Disproportion (CPD).

---

### Quick Revision

- **Anatomical Conjugate**: Distance from midpoint of sacral promontory to upper inner margin of symphysis pubis = **11 cm**.
- **Estimation Formula**: Anatomical Conjugate = Diagonal Conjugate (12 cm) - 1.2 cm.
- **Obstetric Conjugate**: Shortest AP diameter = **10 cm** (sacral promontory to inner midpoint of symphysis pubis).
- **Clinical Relevance**: Inability to reach sacral promontory on PV examination indicates an adequate conjugate.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **The anatomical conjugate (true conjugate) is the 11 cm anteroposterior diameter of the pelvic inlet extending from the midpoint of the sacral promontory to the inner margin of the upper border of the symphysis pubis, calculated indirectly by deducting 1.2 cm from the diagonal conjugate.**
