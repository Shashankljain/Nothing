# Management of Hyperemesis Gravidarum [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks**

---

### Definition

- **Hyperemesis Gravidarum (HG)**: A severe type of vomiting of pregnancy characterized by intractable nausea and vomiting in early pregnancy, causing a diagnostic triad of **>5% loss of pre-pregnancy weight, dehydration, and electrolyte imbalance/ketonuria**.

---

### Management [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Hospitalization & Gastrointestinal Rest**: Hospitalize the patient upon meeting criteria (protracted vomiting, ketonuria, or weight loss >5%); withhold oral feeds (NPO) for at least 24 hours after cessation of vomiting while providing bed rest in a quiet, dark room.
- **IV Rehydration & Electrolyte Correction**: Rapidly infuse **1.5 to 3 L/day of Normal Saline or Ringer's Lactate**over 24 hours to correct volume depletion, hyponatremia (<135 mEq/L), and hypokalemia (<3.5 mEq/L) via KCl supplementation.
- **MANDATORY Thiamine Supplementation**: Administer **Inj. Thiamine (Vitamin B1) 100 mg IV daily BEFORE or alongside Dextrose infusion** to prevent fatal Wernicke's Encephalopathy.
- **Stepwise Antiemetic Regimen**:
    - **First-Line**: Oral/IV **Pyridoxine (Vitamin B6 10–25 mg) + Doxylamine (10 mg)**.
    - **Second-Line**: **Metoclopramide (5–10 mg IV/IM)**, Promethazine (12.5–25 mg IV/IM), or **Ondansetron (4–8 mg IV)**.
    - **Third-Line (Refractory)**: **Hydrocortisone 100 mg IV BD** (tapered to oral prednisolone).
- **Diet Restoration & Supportive Care**: Reintroduce dry carbohydrate foods (biscuits, toast) in small, frequent feeds once vomiting ceases; administer IV Proton Pump Inhibitors (Pantoprazole 40 mg IV) for reflux/ulcer prevention and LMWH for thromboprophylaxis.
