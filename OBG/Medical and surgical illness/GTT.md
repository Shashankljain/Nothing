# Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) in Obstetrics [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **5 Marks** (Definition + detailed procedural, diagnostic, and clinical coverage)

---

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- The **Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT)**, specifically the **Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT)**, is a dynamic endo-metabolic diagnostic investigation that assesses the body's capacity to clear an exogenous glucose load from the systemic circulation.
- In obstetrics, it is the primary gold-standard investigation used for screening and diagnosing **Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM)** and detecting unrecognized **pre-gestational overt diabetes**.

---

### 2. Indications & Timing of Testing [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Universal Screening**: Recommended for all pregnant women between **24–28 weeks of gestation** (period of peak surge of placental contrainsulin hormones like hPL, cortisol, and progesterone).
- **High-Risk Screening (At First Antenatal Visit)**:
    - Family history of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in first-degree relatives (parents, siblings, grandparents).
    - Previous delivery of a macrosomic infant (**birth weight ≥4.0 kg**).
    - Previous GDM, unexplained stillbirth, or intrauterine fetal death (IUFD).
    - Maternal obesity (**BMI ≥25 kg/m² or ≥30 kg/m²**) and maternal age **>30 years**.
    - Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS) or pre-existing hypertension (**BP ≥140/90 mmHg**).
    - Present pregnancy complications: **Polyhydramnios**, **recurrent vulvovaginal candidiasis**, or persistent **glycosuria**.
    - _Repeat Rule_: If early screening at the first visit is negative, repeat the test at **24–28 weeks** (minimum time interval between tests: 4 weeks).

---

### 3. Patient Preparation & Prerequisites (Standard Fasting OGTT)

- **Dietary Preparation**: Unrestricted carbohydrate diet (containing at least **150–200 g carbohydrate/day**) for 3 consecutive days prior to the test.
- **Fasting Requirement**: Overnight fast of **8–12 hours** prior to the test (water is permitted, but tea, coffee, smoking, and food are strictly prohibited).
- **Physical Rest**: Patient must remain seated and rested throughout the 2-to-3-hour testing period (avoid vigorous exercise or walking during the test).
- **Sample Collection**:
    - Venous blood sample (**2 mL**) collected in a **sodium fluoride / potassium oxalate vial** (gray-capped tube).
    - _Mechanism_: Sodium fluoride inhibits the enzyme **enolase**, stopping red blood cell glycolysis and preserving true glucose levels.
    - Plasma glucose levels are estimated using the **glucose oxidase-peroxidase method** (plasma glucose values are ≈15% higher than whole blood values).

---

### 4. Testing Protocols & Diagnostic Criteria [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. DIPSI Criteria (Government of India Single-Step Non-Fasting Test)

- **Protocol**: Single-step non-fasting test recommended for universal screening in resource-limited settings.
- **Procedure**:
    - Patient is given **75 g oral anhydrous glucose** dissolved in **300 mL water**, swallowed over **5–10 minutes**, **irrespective of the time of the last meal**.
    - Single venous blood sample is drawn at **2 hours** and analyzed using a plasma-calibrated glucometer.
    - _Vomiting Rule_: If the patient vomits within 30 minutes, repeat the test on another day; if vomiting occurs after 30 minutes, continue the test.
- **Diagnostic Cut-Off Values (2-Hour Venous Plasma Glucose)**:
    - **<140 mg/dL**: Normal Glucose Tolerance.
    - **≥140 mg/dL to 199 mg/dL**: Diagnosed as **Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM)**.
    - **≥200 mg/dL**: Diagnosed as **Pre-Gestational / Overt Diabetes**.

### 6. Factors Influencing Glucose Tolerance Test Results

- **Physiological Factors Decreasing Glucose Tolerance**:
    - Advancing gestational age (peak contrainsulin hormone production by human placental lactogen - hPL).
    - Inactivity or prolonged bed rest.
- **Interfering Factors (False Positive / Elevated Results)**:
    - Acute infection, stress, or fever.
    - Concomitant drugs: Corticosteroids, β-agonists (tocolytics), thiazide diuretics.
- **Interfering Factors (False Negative / Low Results)**:
    - Severe nausea, vomiting, or delayed gastric emptying.
    - Strict carbohydrate deprivation (<100 g/day) immediately preceding the test.

---

### 7. Clinical Pearl & Exam Trap

### Clinical Pearl

- **Glycosuria vs GTT**: Glycosuria occurs physiologically in up to 50% of pregnant women due to an increased Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR) and lower renal threshold for glucose (<180 mg/dL). However, **fasting glycosuria** or **glycosuria on ≥2 occasions after 20 weeks** is ominous and mandates an immediate Glucose Tolerance Test.

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-Up**: Confusing the **DIPSI single-step non-fasting test** with the **IADPSG / Carpenter-Coustan diagnostic OGTT**.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: DIPSI is performed **irrespective of the last meal** and requires only a **single 2-hour value (≥140 mg/dL)** for diagnosis. In contrast, IADPSG requires **strict overnight fasting** and evaluates 3 points (Fasting ≥92, 1-hr ≥180, 2-hr ≥153 mg/dL), where any single elevated value confirms GDM.

### 9. Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: GTT-DIPSI

- **G**: **Glucose dose** = 75 g anhydrous glucose in 300 mL water
- **T**: **Two-hour** single venous plasma sampling
- **T**: **Time of testing** = Universal screening at 24–28 weeks
- **D**: **DIPSI criterion** = GOI single-step non-fasting test
- **I**: **Interpreting cut-off** = ≥140 mg/dL indicates GDM
- **P**: **Plasma Fluoride vial** = Sodium fluoride inhibits glycolysis
- **S**: **Seven-five grams** used irrespective of previous meal
- **I**: **Immediate insulin** if 2-hour value ≥200 mg/dL (overt diabetes)

### Quick Revision

- **Timing**: Universal screening between **24–28 weeks gestation**; early screening at 1st visit for high-risk cases.
- **DIPSI Single-Step Test**: **75 g glucose** non-fasting; venous plasma drawn at **2 hours**.
- **DIPSI Cut-Offs**: <140 mg/dL (Normal), **≥140 to 199 mg/dL (GDM)**, ≥200 mg/dL (Overt Diabetes).
- **IADPSG Cut-Offs**: Fasting **≥92 mg/dL**, 1-hr **≥180 mg/dL**, 2-hr **≥153 mg/dL** (≥1 value confirms GDM).
- **Fluoride Tube Requirement**: Sodium fluoride preserves glucose by inhibiting red cell enolase activity.

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **The Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) in obstetrics is the definitive diagnostic procedure for carbohydrate intolerance in pregnancy, executed universally at 24–28 weeks using DIPSI single-step non-fasting guidelines (75 g glucose with 2-hour plasma cut-off ≥140 mg/dL) or fasting IADPSG/Carpenter-Coustan criteria to prevent fetal macrosomia and perinatal complications.**
