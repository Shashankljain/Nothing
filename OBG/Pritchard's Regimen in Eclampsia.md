# Pritchard's Regimen in Eclampsia [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition

- **Pritchard's Regimen**: A standardized intramuscular (IM) and intravenous (IV) combination protocol of **Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO₄·7H₂O)** used as the gold-standard first-line anticonvulsant therapy for arresting active convulsions and preventing seizure recurrence in eclampsia.

---

### Key Clinical Features & Protocol

- **Loading Dose (Total 14 g MgSO₄)**:
    - **4 g IV**: Administered as a 20% solution (4 g in 20 mL) slowly over 3–5 minutes at a rate of 1 g/minute.
    - **10 g IM**: Administered as a 50% solution (5 g [10 mL] injected deep IM into the upper outer quadrant of each buttock).
    - _Note_: The loading dose is administered immediately upon clinical diagnosis, **irrespective of maternal renal status**.
- **Maintenance Dose**:
    - **5 g IM** (50% solution) injected deep IM every 4 hours in alternate buttocks.
    - Continued until **24 hours after delivery OR 24 hours after the last seizure**, whichever is later.
- **3 Mandatory Clinical Checks Before Each Maintenance Dose**:
    1. **Patellar Reflex (Knee Jerk)**: Must be **PRESENT**.
    2. **Respiratory Rate**: Must be **≥12 breaths/minute**.
    3. **Urinary Output**: Must be **≥30 mL/hour** (or ≥100 mL over 4 hours).
- **Management of Seizure Recurrence**: Administer an additional **2 g IV bolus** as a 20% solution slowly over 3–5 minutes.
- **Antidote for Hypermagnesemic Toxicity**: **10% Calcium Gluconate 10 mL IV** given slowly over 10 minutes.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Pritchard Loading Dose**: 4 g IV (20% solution over 3–5 min) + 10 g IM (5 g of 50% solution in each buttock).
- **Pritchard Maintenance Dose**: 5 g IM (50% solution) 4-hourly in alternate buttocks for 24 hours postpartum/last fit.
- **3 Mandatory Checks**: Knee jerk present, RR ≥12/min, Urine output ≥30 mL/hr.
- **Recurrence**: 2 g IV bolus over 3–5 minutes.
- **Antidote**: 10% Calcium Gluconate 10 mL IV slowly over 10 minutes.

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Pritchard's regimen is the standardized combination IM/IV Magnesium Sulfate protocol comprising a 14 g loading dose (4 g IV + 10 g IM) and 5 g IM 4-hourly maintenance doses for seizure control in eclampsia, safely monitored via patellar reflex, respiratory rate, and urinary output.**
