
# Management of the Third Stage of Labor [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **5 Marks**

---

### 1. Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Third Stage of Labor**: Begins immediately after the expulsion of the fetus and ends with the complete separation, descent, and expulsion of the placenta and membranes (afterbirths).
- **Duration**: The average duration is about **15 minutes** in both primigravidae and multiparae under physiological/expectant management, which is significantly reduced to **5 minutes** with Active Management of the Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL).
- **Clinical Importance**: The third stage is considered the most crucial stage of labor. A previously uneventful first or second stage can suddenly convert within a minute into a life-threatening emergency, predominantly due to Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH).

---

### 2. Physiology of the Third Stage [⭐ High-Yield]

#### A. Placental Separation

- Following fetal delivery, the upper uterine segment undergoes rapid contraction and permanent muscle shortening (**retraction**), dramatically reducing the placental site surface area.
- Because the placenta is inelastic, this sudden surface reduction creates a shearing force across the attachment site, separating the placenta through the deep spongy layer (_zona spongiosa_) of the decidua basalis.
- **Modes of Placental Separation**:
    1. **Schultze Mechanism (Central - 80%)**: Separation starts centrally; the placenta descends like an inverted umbrella, presenting its shiny fetal surface at the vulva with minimal external pre-expulsion bleeding.
    2. **Mathews-Duncan Mechanism (Marginal - 20%)**: Separation starts at the lower margin; dark blood escapes vaginally prior to delivery, and the rough maternal surface presents first.

#### B. Physiological Hemostasis ("Living Ligatures")

- Hemostasis at the placental site is primarily achieved by myometrial contraction and retraction, which physically compress the criss-crossing, interlacing middle muscle fibers (**"Figure-of-8" fibers or "Living Ligatures"**) around open blood vessels, supplemented by secondary intravascular thrombosis.

---

### 3. Active Management of the Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL) [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Principle**: To excite powerful uterine contractions within **1 minute** of fetal delivery by administering a parenteral oxytocic agent, facilitating rapid placental separation and post-separation myometrial retraction.
- **Key Advantages of AMTSL**:
    - **Reduces Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) by 60%**.
    - Reduces third-stage blood loss to approximately **one-fifth**.
    - Shortens third-stage duration from 15 minutes down to **5 minutes**.
    - Decreases maternal anemia and reduces the need for blood transfusions.

1. **Step 1: Administer Parenteral Uterotonic Agent**:
    
    - Administer **Inj. Oxytocin 10 IU IM** (preferred) within **1 minute** of the delivery of the baby (after ruling out an undiagnosed second twin).
    - _Alternatives if Oxytocin is unavailable_: Tab Misoprostol **600 μg** orally/rectally or Inj. Methergine **0.2 mg IM**.
2. **Step 2: Controlled Cord Traction (CCT / Modified Brandt-Andrews Technique)**:
    
    - Perform delayed cord clamping (≥1 minute post-delivery) and divide the cord.
    - Place a clamp close to the vulval introitus.
    - During a uterine contraction, place the palmar surface of the fingers of the non-dominant hand over the lower abdomen at the junction of the upper and lower uterine segments (above the symphysis pubis), pushing the body of the uterus **upward and backward** toward the umbilicus (counter-traction).
    - Simultaneously, apply steady, gentle downward and backward tension on the clamped cord with the dominant hand until the placenta emerges.
    - As the placenta clears the introitus, hold it in both hands and twist it slowly to peel off the membranes intact without leaving retained fragments.
3. **Step 3: Uterine Massage & Postpartum Vigilance**:
    
    - **Immediate Fundal Massage**: Massage the uterine fundus immediately after placental expulsion until it becomes hard and well-retracted.
    - **Placental Examination**: Meticulously inspect the maternal surface (cotyledons) and fetal surface (membranes) for completeness.
    - **Genital Tract Inspection**: Inspect the vulva, vagina, and perineum to exclude and repair traumatic lacerations.
    - **Postpartum Vigilance**: Palpate the uterine fundus every **15 minutes for 2 hours** post-delivery to ensure the uterus remains hard, and monitor total blood loss.

### 6. Complications of the Third Stage [⭐ High-Yield]

1. **Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH)**: Blood loss ≥500 mL following vaginal delivery; primary cause is myometrial atony.
2. **Retained Placenta**: Non-expulsion of the placenta within 30 minutes of delivery, requiring Manual Removal of Placenta (MROP) under general anesthesia.
3. **Acute Uterine Inversion**: Rare, catastrophic turning inside-out of the uterus caused by cord traction on an uncontracted, atonic fundus without suprapubic counter-traction.
4. **Obstetric Shock**: Secondary to massive hemorrhage or neurogenic pain from visceral traction.
5. **Pulmonary Embolism**: Rare entry of amniotic fluid or air into open uterine venous sinuses.

### 8. Clinical Pearl & Exam Trap

### Clinical Pearl

- **The "Living Ligatures" of the Uterus**: The middle layer of myometrium in the upper uterine segment contains criss-crossing muscle fibers in a **"Figure-of-8" pattern**. As these fibers contract and retract post-delivery, they act as mechanical ligatures, clamping open uteroplacental blood vessels to achieve physiological hemostasis.

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-Up**: Performing Controlled Cord Traction (CCT) without applying suprapubic counter-traction.
- **Distinguishing Fact**: **NEVER pull on the umbilical cord without simultaneous upward counter-traction on the lower uterine segment!** Traction on an uncontracted uterus without suprapubic counter-elevation can pull the fundus down through the cervix, causing catastrophic **Acute Uterine Inversion**.

### 10. Mnemonic / Memory Palace

#### Mnemonic: A-M-T-S-L (Active Management Sequence)

- **A**: **Administer parenteral uterotonic** (Inj. Oxytocin 10 IU IM within 1 minute)
- **M**: **Mandatory check for second twin** prior to uterotonic injection
- **T**: **Traction on umbilical cord (Controlled Cord Traction)** with suprapubic counter-traction
- **S**: **Slowly twist placenta and membranes** upon delivery to keep membranes intact
- **L**: **Late/Immediate uterine Massage** and postpartum vigilance every 15 minutes
