[[MDG]]
The **Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)** were a series of eight international development goals established following the Millennium Summit of the United Nations in September 2000. These goals represented a global commitment to tackle poverty, hunger, ill-health, and environmental degradation by the target year of **2015**.

---

### **I. Health-Related Millennium Development Goals**

While there are eight total MDGs, the relationship between these goals and community health is profound.

- **Quantity:** There are **three** goals directly related to health, but all eight goals have important indirect effects on health status.
- **Health Components:** Of the 18 targets and 48 indicators used to measure progress, **8 targets and 18 indicators**are health-related.

#### **The Direct Health Goals** ★ Important Exam Point

1. **Goal 4: Reduce child mortality.**
2. **Goal 5: Improve maternal health.**
3. **Goal 6: Combat HIV/AIDS, malaria, and other diseases.**

#### **Indirectly Related Goals affecting Health**

- **Goal 1:** Eradicate extreme poverty and hunger (includes indicators for child underweight and dietary energy consumption).
- **Goal 7:** Ensure environmental sustainability (includes access to safe drinking water and basic sanitation).
- **Goal 8:** Develop a global partnership for development (includes access to affordable essential drugs).

---

### **II. Detailed Focus: Goal 4 – Reduce Child Mortality** ★ Important Exam Point

This goal is a critical measure of a community's overall health status and the effectiveness of its primary healthcare system.

- **What was the Target?** To reduce the under-five mortality rate (U5MR) by **two-thirds** between 1990 and 2015.
- **Why is it important?** High child mortality reflects inadequate maternal care, poor nutrition, low immunization coverage, and adverse environmental exposures.
- **Key Indicators used to measure Goal 4:**
    1. **Under-five mortality rate (U5MR):** The probability of a child dying before reaching age five.
    2. **Infant mortality rate (IMR):** The number of deaths of infants under one year of age per 1,000 live births.
    3. **Measles Immunization:** Proportion of 1-year-old children immunized against measles.

#### **India’s Progress and Status**

India made significant strides toward this goal, though regional disparities remain a challenge.

- **Achievement:** By 2020, India's national IMR dropped to **28 per 1,000 live births**.
- **The Kerala Model:** Kerala serves as a yardstick for achievement, reaching an IMR of **6** (comparable to developed nations) due to high female literacy (98.3%) and strong political commitment to social development.
- **Drivers of Success:**
    - **Pulse Polio Immunization:** Launched in 1995, leading to India being declared polio-free in March 2014.
    - **Universal Immunization Programme (UIP):** Aimed at protecting children against vaccine-preventable diseases.

### **High-Yield Exam Points** ★

- **Target Year:** MDGs had a deadline of **2015**, after which they were succeeded by the **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** for 2030.
- **Health Centrality:** Health is at the heart of development; 3 out of 8 goals are direct, and 18 of 48 indicators are health-related.
- **MNTE:** India achieved a major MDG-related milestone by validating **Maternal and Neonatal Tetanus Elimination** in May 2015.

### **Mnemonic for the Eight MDGs**

**"P-E-G-C-M-D-E-D"**

1. **P** – **P**overty and Hunger
2. **E** – **E**ducation (Primary)
3. **G** – **G**ender Equality
4. **C** – **C**hild Mortality (Health Goal)
5. **M** – **M**aternal Health (Health Goal)
6. **D** – **D**iseases (HIV/Malaria) (Health Goal)
7. **E** – **E**nvironmental Sustainability
8. **D** – **D**evelopment Partnership

### **Frequently Asked Viva Questions**

- **Q: Which MDG focuses on water and sanitation?**
    - **A:** Goal 7 (Ensure environmental sustainability).
- **Q: What is the current replacement for the MDGs?**
    - **A:** The **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, which consist of 17 goals.
- **Q: Why was Measles immunization chosen as an MDG indicator?**
    - **A:** Because measles is a highly infectious disease and a major cause of childhood mortality that can be prevented with a simple, cost-effective vaccine.

### **Common Mistakes Students Make**

- **Mixing Goals and Targets:** Students often say there are 17 MDGs. Remember: **MDGs = 8 goals**; **SDGs = 17 goals**.
- **Ignoring Indirect Goals:** Forgetting that Goal 1 (Poverty/Nutrition) and Goal 7 (Water/Sanitation) are vital components of "Community Health" even if they aren't the three "direct" health goals.
- **IMR/U5MR Confusion:** IMR is deaths under 1 year; U5MR is deaths under 5 years. MDG Goal 4 specifically targets the **Under-five** mortality rate.
