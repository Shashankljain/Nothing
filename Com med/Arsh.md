**Strategy for Service Delivery (How the Clinic Works)**

**1. Facility-Based Services (AFHCs)** ★

- **Location:** Established at Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), and District Hospitals.
- **Environment:** Must be "Adolescent Friendly"—meaning private, confidential, and staffed by personnel trained to communicate effectively with young people.
- **Operational Days:** Often designated as fixed-day clinics (e.g., weekly) to ensure dedicated staff availability.

**2. Community-Based Outreach**

- **Peer Educators:** Training local adolescents to act as a bridge between the community and the health facility.
- **Weekly Iron & Folic Acid (WIFS):** Delivered through schools and Anganwadi centres for non-school going girls.
- **Menstrual Hygiene Scheme (MHS):** Distribution of sanitary napkins to rural adolescent girls by ASHAs.

---

**V. Service Delivery Mechanism: Organizational Structure**

- **National Level:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW).
- **State Level:** State RCH Cell.
- **District Level:** District Health Society and Adolescent Health Coordinator.
- **Peripheral Level:** **Adolescent Friendly Health Clinics (AFHCs)** and involvement of ASHAs and Anganwadi Workers (AWWs) for community mobilisation.
