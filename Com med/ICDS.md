**Organizational Structure**

The delivery of ICDS is a collaborative effort between the ==Ministry of Women and Child== ==Development== and the ==Ministry of Health and Family Welfare==:

- **National Level:** Ministry of Women and Child Development.
- **District Level:** District Programme Officer (DPO).
- **Block Level:** Child Development Project Officer (CDPO).
- **Sector Level:** Supervisors (Mukhya Sevika).
- **Village Level:** **Anganwadi Centre (AWC)** manned by an Anganwadi Worker (AWW) and an Anganwadi Helper (AWH)
