[[C7-Diagnosis of Pregnancy]]
# Naegele's Rule [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Naegele's Rule (1812)**: A standard clinical formula used to calculate the Expected Date of Delivery (EDD) based on the first day of the Last Normal Menstrual Period (LMP), assuming a regular 28-day cycle with ovulation occurring on day 14.
- **Basis of Calculation**: Estimates total gestational (menstrual) age as **280 days (40 weeks / 9 calendar months + 7 days)** from the first day of LMP.

---

### Formula & Clinical Application [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Standard Calculation Methods**:
    - **Primary Method**: **EDD = First day of LMP + 7 days + 9 calendar months**.
    - **Alternative Method**: **EDD = First day of LMP + 7 days - 3 calendar months + 1 year**.
    - _Example_: LMP = 1st January 2026 → +9 months = 1st October + 7 days = **8th October 2026**.
- **Adjustments for Cycle Length Variations**:
    - **Cycle >28 days**: Add the difference (Cycle Length - 28 days) to the calculated EDD.
    - **Cycle <28 days**: Subtract the difference (28 days - Cycle Length) from the calculated EDD.
- **Conditions Where Naegele's Rule is Not Applicable** (Requires 1st-Trimester USG Crown-Rump Length [CRL] Dating):
    1. Irregular menstrual cycles.
    2. Conception during lactational amenorrhea.
    3. Conception immediately following oral contraceptive pill (OCP) failure.
    4. Patient uncertain of the exact date of LMP.
- **Assisted Reproductive Technology (IVF) Calculation**:
    - **EDD = Date of Embryo Transfer (ET) + 266 days** (or by treating the day 14 days prior to embryo transfer as the LMP).

---

### Quick Revision

- **Standard Formula**: 1st day of LMP + 7 days + 9 calendar months (Total duration = **280 days / 40 weeks**).
- **Alternative Formula**: 1st day of LMP + 7 days - 3 calendar months + 1 year.
- **Cycle Length Correction**: Add days for cycles >28 days; subtract days for cycles <28 days.
- **Primary Limitation**: Inapplicable in irregular cycles, post-OCP pill failure, lactational amenorrhea, or uncertain LMP (use TVS Crown-Rump Length [CRL]).

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Naegele's rule is a standard clinical method for estimating the expected date of delivery (EDD) by adding 9 calendar months and 7 days to the first day of the last normal menstrual period, corresponding to a total gestational age of 280 days (40 weeks).**
