# Adherent Placenta: Types [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Adherent Placenta (Placenta Accreta Spectrum - PAS / Morbidly Adherent Placenta)**: A life-threatening disorder of placentation defined by the direct anchoring or invasion of chorionic villi into the myometrium, caused by the partial or complete absence of the **decidua basalis** and **Nitabuch's fibrinoid layer**.

---

### Types of Adherent Placenta (FIGO / Clinical Classification) [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Placenta Accreta (Grade 1 — Vera / Adherenta)**: Chorionic villi are **superficially attached directly to the myometrium** without invading the muscle fibers; represents the most common type (~75–80% of cases).
- **Placenta Increta (Grade 2)**: Chorionic villi **deeply invade into the myometrium**, penetrating between uterine muscle bundles (~15% of cases).
- **Placenta Percreta (Grade 3)**: Chorionic villi **penetrate through the entire thickness of the myometrium** up to or beyond the uterine serosa (~5% of cases).
    - _Grade 3a_: Villi invade up to the uterine serosa.
    - _Grade 3b_: Villi invade through the serosa into the **urinary bladder wall** (presenting with hematuria).
    - _Grade 3c_: Villi invade into other adjacent pelvic organs or tissues.
- **Clinical Hallmark**: Complete absence of a physiological cleavage plane, causing **intractable hemorrhage upon attempted manual removal of placenta (MRP)**.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Pathology**: Failure of decidua basalis and Nitabuch's layer formation leads to direct myometrial villous adherence.
- **Accreta (Grade 1)**: Villi superficially attached to myometrium (most common).
- **Increta (Grade 2)**: Villi deeply invade myometrium.
- **Percreta (Grade 3)**: Villi penetrate through serosa ± invade urinary bladder (3b) or pelvic organs (3c).
- **Primary Risk Factors**: Prior Cesarean delivery and co-existing Placenta Previa.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Adherent placenta (Placenta Accreta Spectrum) is a severe disorder of placentation caused by the absence of decidua basalis and Nitabuch's layer, classified by the depth of villous invasion into Placenta Accreta (superficial attachment), Increta (myometrial invasion), and Percreta (serosal and pelvic organ penetration).**
