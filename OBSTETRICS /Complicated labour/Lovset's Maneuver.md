# Lovset's Maneuver in Breech Delivery [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Lovset's Maneuver**: An extra-uterine operative obstetric maneuver widely used in assisted vaginal breech delivery to release extended or nuchal arms, utilizing the inclination of the pelvic birth canal by executing sequential **180° rotations** of the fetal trunk under downward traction [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 554].

---

### Key Technique & Clinical Principles [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Anatomical Principle**: Due to pelvic inclination, when the anterior shoulder is above the symphysis pubis, the posterior shoulder lies lower below the sacral promontory; rotating the trunk **180°** brings the lower posterior shoulder under the pubic arch for easy release [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 555].
- **Timing of Initiation**: Commenced only after the fetal trunk is born up to the umbilicus and the **inferior angle of the anterior scapula is visible** under the pubic arch [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 555].
- **Step 1 (First Shoulder Delivery)**: Fetal pelvis is grasped using the **femoropelvic grip** (thumbs parallel over sacrum); the trunk is rotated **180°** keeping the **back anterior** under downward traction, bringing the posterior shoulder anteriorly under the symphysis to be hooked out at the elbow [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364–365, 556].
- **Step 2 (Second Shoulder Delivery)**: The fetal trunk is then rotated **180° in the reverse direction** (maintaining back anterior) to bring the remaining shoulder under the pubic arch for delivery [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364–365, 556].
- **Advantages over Classical Method**: Requires no intrauterine manipulation, carries minimal fetal trauma risk, effectively releases both extended and nuchal arms, and does not require general anesthesia [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 554].

---

### Quick Revision

- **Indication**: Extended arms or nuchal displacement of arms in breech presentation [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 552].
- **Prerequisite**: Inferior angle of scapula visible under pubic arch; femoropelvic grip applied [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 555].
- **Core Action**: Two sequential **180° body rotations** maintaining **back anterior** under downward traction [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 556].
- **Key Advantage**: Zero intrauterine manipulation required; safer than classical arm extraction [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364, 554].

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Lovset's maneuver is the standard extra-uterine obstetric technique for delivering extended or nuchal arms in assisted vaginal breech delivery, leveraging pelvic inclination by executing two sequential 180° rotations of the fetal trunk under downward traction while maintaining a dorsoanterior orientation.** [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 364–365, 554].
