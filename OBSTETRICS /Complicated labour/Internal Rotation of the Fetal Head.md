# Internal Rotation of the Fetal Head [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Internal Rotation**: A cardinal movement of labor in vertex presentation whereby the fetal head rotates while descending through the pelvic cavity, bringing the occiput anteriorly behind the symphysis pubis to align the long diameter of the head with the anteroposterior diameter of the pelvic outlet [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].

---

### Key Features & Mechanism [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Anatomical Level**: Occurs primarily at the **plane of least pelvic dimensions** (bispinous plane at ischial spines station 0) on the muscular gutter of the pelvic floor (levator ani muscles) [DC Dutta 1, Ch 9, p. 95; Ch 13, p. 128].
- **Degree of Rotation**:
    - **Occipitolateral (LOT/ROT — 70%)**: Occiput rotates anteriorly through **2/8th of a circle (90°)** [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
    - **Oblique Occipitoanterior (LOA/ROA)**: Occiput rotates anteriorly through **1/8th of a circle (45°)** [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
    - **Occipitoposterior (LOP/ROP)**: Favorable long anterior rotation is **3/8th of a circle (135°)** [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 348].
- **Causative Theories / Law of Pelvic Floor**:
    1. **Hart's Rule (Slope of Pelvic Floor)**: The levator ani muscles slope downward, forward, and medially. The well-flexed occiput touches the pelvic floor first and, during elastic recoil after a contraction, is pushed forward toward the midline [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
    2. **Pelvic Shape Adaptation**: Forward inclination of pelvic side walls and long AP outlet diameter force the long head axis into the AP diameter [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
    3. **Law of Unequal Flexibility (Sellheim & Moir)**: Differences in fetal head and trunk flexibility favor anterior rotation in a curved birth canal [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
- **Prerequisites for Rotation**: (1) Well-flexed fetal head, (2) Efficient uterine contractions, (3) Favorable pelvic architecture (gynecoid/anthropoid), and (4) Good levator ani muscle tone [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
- **Neck Torsion**: When the head rotates 90° (2/8th circle), the fetal neck sustains 45° (1/8th circle) of torsion while the shoulders simultaneously rotate 45° into the oblique pelvic diameter [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].

---

### Quick Revision

- **Level of Rotation**: Plane of least pelvic dimensions (ischial spines level) on the levator ani muscle floor [DC Dutta 1, p. 95, 128].
- **Degree of Rotation**: 90° (2/8th circle) in LOT/ROT; 45° (1/8th circle) in LOA/ROA; 135° (3/8th circle) in LOP/ROP [DC Dutta 1, p. 128, 348].
- **Core Mechanism**: **Hart's Rule** (pelvic floor slope + elastic recoil pushing the leading flexed occiput anteriorly) [DC Dutta 1, p. 128].
- **Four Prerequisites**: Well-flexed head + Strong contractions + Favorable pelvis + Normal levator ani tone [DC Dutta 1, p. 128].

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

- **Internal rotation is a pivotal movement of labor occurring at the plane of least pelvic dimensions, driven by the slope and elastic recoil of the levator ani muscle gutter (Hart's rule) on a well-flexed fetal head, rotating the occiput 45° to 90° anteriorly behind the symphysis pubis to align with the anteroposterior diameter of the pelvic outlet.** [DC Dutta 1, Ch 13, p. 128].
