# Burns-Marshall Method in Breech Presentation [⭐ High-Yield]

### Mark Weightage

- **3 Marks / Short Answer Depth**

---

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Burns-Marshall Method**: A standard obstetric maneuver used for the delivery of the aftercoming fetal head in assisted vaginal breech delivery, where the fetal body is allowed to hang unsupported by its own weight to promote head flexion, followed by swinging the trunk in a wide arc upward and forward over the mother's abdomen once the subocciput hinges under the symphysis pubis [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 362–363].

---

### Key Technique & Clinical Steps [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Step 1 (Hanging Phase & Suprapubic Pressure)**: The baby is allowed to hang by its own weight for 1 to 2 minutes; simultaneously, an assistant applies downward and backward suprapubic pressure over the sinciput with the flat of the hand to maintain head flexion [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 362].
- **Step 2 (Hinging under Subpubic Arch)**: Descent proceeds until the nape of the neck (subocciput) appears under the pubic arch, which serves as the fulcrum/hinge point [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 362–363].
- **Step 3 (Upward & Forward Arc Movement)**: The operator grasps the fetal ankles with one finger placed between them, maintains steady traction, and **swings the trunk in a wide arc upward and forward over the maternal abdomen** [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 363].
- **Step 4 (Perineal Protection & Airway Clearance)**: The operator's opposite hand guards the perineum, slipping it successively off the face and brow; once the mouth is born, pharyngeal mucus is aspirated before releasing the rest of the skull [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 363].
- **Critical Timing Constraint**: Nape-to-mouth delivery must be completed within **5 to 10 minutes** of cord emergence to prevent acute intrapartum fetal asphyxia [DC Dutta 1, Ch 26, p. 362].

---

### Quick Revision

- **Indication**: Delivery of the aftercoming head in assisted vaginal breech delivery [DC Dutta 1, p. 362].
- **Pivot Hinge Point**: Subocciput (nape of neck) hinges under the symphysis pubis [DC Dutta 1, p. 362].
- **Core Maneuver**: Hang by weight → Assistant suprapubic pressure → Swing ankles in a wide arc upward over maternal abdomen [DC Dutta 1, p. 362–363].
- **Time Limit**: Deliver mouth within 5 to 10 minutes of cord delivery [DC Dutta 1, p. 362].
