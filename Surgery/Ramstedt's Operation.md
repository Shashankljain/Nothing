# Ramstedt's Operation (Ramstedt's Pyloromyotomy)

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Ramstedt's operation** (pyloromyotomy) is the definitive surgical procedure for infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis (IHPS), involving a longitudinal incision and splitting of the hypertrophied pyloric serosa and circular muscle layer down to the underlying submucosa, thereby relieving gastric outlet obstruction without breaching mucosal integrity.

---

### Key Operative Technique & Steps [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Preoperative Resuscitation Rule**: Pyloric stenosis is a metabolic emergency, not an immediate surgical one; mandatory 24–48 hours of fluid resuscitation with **0.9% Normal Saline + 0.15% KCl in 5% Dextrose** is required to correct hypochloraemic, hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis before anesthesia.
- **Surgical Approaches**: Performed laparoscopically or open via a **supraumbilical / right upper quadrant incision**.
- **Seromyotomy Incision**: A longitudinal incision is made along the avascular serosa of the hypertrophied pyloric "tumor," extending from the duodenal fornix onto the gastric antrum.
- **Muscle Splitting**: The thickened circular muscle fibers are gently spread open using a pyloric spreader or blunt forceps until the intact, pliable submucosa bulges out freely along the entire channel.
- **Proximal Extent Rule**: The muscle division **must extend onto the gastric antrum**; short proximal incisions leave residual hypertrophied muscle and are the leading cause of early recurrence.
- **Postoperative Protocol**: Intact submucosa is verified by squeezing gastric air to check for leaks; oral feeds (breast milk or formula) are safely re-established within 24 hours.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Indication**: Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis (IHPS) presenting at 2–6 weeks of age.
- **Core Principle**: Longitudinal division of hypertrophied pyloric circular muscle leaving the submucosa intact.
- **Critical Technical Point**: The myotomy incision must extend onto the gastric antrum to prevent incomplete obstruction relief and early recurrence.
- **Postoperative Feeding**: Intravenous fluids continued until oral feeding is re-established within 24 hours.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Ramstedt's operation (pyloromyotomy) is the definitive surgical procedure for infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis, performed following fluid and electrolyte resuscitation by longitudinally incising and spreading the hypertrophied pyloric circular muscle down to the intact submucosa from the duodenal fornix onto the gastric antrum._
