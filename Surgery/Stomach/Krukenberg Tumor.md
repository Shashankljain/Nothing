# Krukenberg Tumor

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

A **Krukenberg tumor** refers to a metastatic ovarian malignancy, classically presenting as bilateral ovarian involvement originating from a primary gastrointestinal or extra-genital adenocarcinoma.

---

### High-Yield Exam Bullets [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Primary Sites of Origin**: Most commonly arises secondary to primary gastric adenocarcinoma (**Stomach > Breast > Colorectal**).
- **Histopathological Hallmark**: Pathologically characterized by mucin-secreting **signet-ring carcinoma cells**, wherein intracytoplasmic mucin accumulation displaces the nucleus to the cell periphery.
- **Etiopathogenetic Theories of Spread**:
    - _Modern / Preferred Theory_: **Retrograde lymphatic spread** through the submucosal and retroperitoneal lymphatic networks.
    - _Classic / Traditional Theory_: **Transcoelomic spread** ("drop metastases") following serosal breach and transperitoneal seeding into the pelvic cavity.
- **Clinical Context**: Serves as an indicator of advanced systemic disease, frequently co-existing with other metastatic signposts of gastric cancer, such as Virchow's node, Sister Mary Joseph's nodule, and Blumer's shelf.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-up**: Misdiagnosing a Krukenberg tumor as a primary ovarian cystadenocarcinoma.
- **The Distinguishing Fact**: Primary ovarian mucinous tumors are typically unilateral, whereas **Krukenberg tumors present as bilateral metastatic ovarian masses characterized histologically by signet-ring cells, requiring upper gastrointestinal endoscopy to locate the underlying primary gastric malignancy**.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: Metastatic ovarian carcinoma presenting classically as bilateral ovarian masses.
- **Primary Source**: Gastric adenocarcinoma is the most common primary site (Stomach > Breast > Colorectal).
- **Pathology**: Mucin-secreting signet-ring carcinoma cells with peripherally displaced nuclei.
- **Mechanism of Spread**: Retrograde lymphatic spread (latest theory) or transcoelomic drop metastasis (old theory).

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_A Krukenberg tumor is a metastatic ovarian malignancy characterized pathologically by bilateral ovarian infiltration with signet-ring cell adenocarcinoma arising predominantly from a primary gastric carcinoma via retrograde lymphatic or transcoelomic spread._
