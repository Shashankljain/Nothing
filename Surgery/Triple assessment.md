# Triple Assessment of Breast Disease

### Definition

**Triple assessment** is a systematic, multimodal diagnostic protocol utilized for the evaluation of symptomatic breast disease (breast lump, nipple discharge, or nodularity). It combines clinical evaluation, radiological imaging, and histopathological tissue sampling to achieve a diagnostic accuracy and positive predictive value approaching **100% (99.9%)**.

- **1. Clinical Examination**: Detailed clinical history and physical examination of both breasts and axillary nodal basins using the **Dial Clock Method** to document lump location, size, mobility, and skin/nipple changes.
- **2. Radiological Imaging**:
    - **Targeted Breast Ultrasound (USG)**: First-line imaging modality in women **<40 years** (dense glandular tissue) and during pregnancy; differentiates solid from cystic lesions.
    - **Digital Mammography**: First-line imaging in women **≥40 years** (Craniocaudal [CC] and Mediolateral Oblique [MLO] views); reports findings using the **BI-RADS** classification (0 to 6).
- **3. Tissue Sampling (Histopathology)**:
    - **14-Gauge Core Needle Biopsy (Tru-Cut Biopsy) [⭐ High-Yield]**: **Investigation of Choice (IOC)** for solid breast lumps. Removes intact tissue cores to distinguish invasive carcinoma from ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) and enables Immunohistochemistry (IHC) marker assessment (ER, PR, HER2/neu, Ki-67).
    - _Fine Needle Aspiration Cytology (FNAC)_: Obsolete/not used routinely for primary breast mass diagnosis due to high false-negative rates and inability to evaluate basement membrane invasion.

---

### Clinical Pearl

When all three components of Triple Assessment (Clinical + Imaging + Core Biopsy) yield concordant benign or malignant findings, the diagnostic accuracy reaches **99.9%**, enabling definitive therapeutic decision-making without requiring open surgical excisional biopsy.

---

### Exam Trap

- **The Mix-up**: Recommending Fine Needle Aspiration Cytology (FNAC) as the primary tissue diagnostic tool in Triple Assessment.
- **The Distinguishing Fact**: FNAC provides cytological details only and **cannot evaluate basement membrane breach or differentiate invasive ductal carcinoma from ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS)**. **14-Gauge Core Needle Biopsy is the Investigation of Choice** for primary tissue diagnosis.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Components**: Clinical Examination + Radiological Imaging (USG <40 yrs / Mammography ≥40 yrs) + 14G Core Needle Biopsy.
- **Diagnostic Accuracy**: Concordance across all three modalities yields ~100% (99.9%) accuracy.
- **Imaging Strategy**: Ultrasound in women <40 years; Mammography (CC and MLO views) in women ≥40 years.
- **Biopsy Gold Standard**: 14G Core Needle Biopsy is the IOC for histological architecture and IHC profiling.
