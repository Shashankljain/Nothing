[[C15- Venous disease]]

### Definition

**Bisgaard's method of treatment** (Bisgaard regime) is a classic multimodal conservative management protocol designed for the treatment and healing of chronic venous leg ulcers and lipodermatosclerosis by counteracting chronic ambulatory venous hypertension.

---

### Key Components / Steps [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Patient Education & Mobility Exercises**: Educating the patient on skin hygiene, avoiding prolonged standing, and performing regular ankle joint flexion-extension exercises to restore calf muscle pump function.
- **Limb Elevation**: Periodic elevation of the lower limb above heart level during rest to reduce capillary hydrostatic pressure, decrease symptoms, and facilitate lymph drainage.
- **Graduated Compression Therapy**: Application of Class III elastic compression stockings (25–35 mmHg) or a four-layer compression bandage system (delivering 35–40 mmHg pressure at the ankle) to accelerate wound healing.
- **Surgical / Endovenous Intervention (Mainstay)**: Expeditious superficial venous ablation (EVLT / RFA) or saphenofemoral ligation to eliminate axial venous reflux and prevent ulcer recurrence.
- **Pharmacotherapy (Pentoxifylline)**: Oral Pentoxifylline (400 mg 8-hourly) as the primary approved vasoactive agent to increase RBC deformability, decrease blood viscosity, and improve microvascular tissue perfusion.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Indication**: Conservative management of chronic venous leg ulcers and lipodermatosclerosis.
- **Core Triad**: Elevation + Compression (35–40 mmHg) + Ankle mobility exercises.
- **Medical Adjunct**: Pentoxifylline (400 mg 8-hourly) to enhance microvascular flow.
- **Definitive Care**: Ablation of refluxing superficial veins to halve healing time and reduce re-ulceration.
