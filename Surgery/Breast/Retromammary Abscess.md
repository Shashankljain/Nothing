[[C31-Breast]]
# Retromammary Abscess

### Definition

A **retromammary abscess** is a deep-seated breast abscess located in the retromammary cellular space—the loose areolar tissue layer situated between the posterior surface of the mammary gland and the anterior fascia of the pectoralis major muscle.

---

### Etiology & Pathogenesis

- **Underlying Causes**:
    1. _Extension of Intramammary Abscess_: Rupture or deep extension of a deep-seated intramammary abscess through the deep fascial layer of the breast into the retromammary space.
    2. _Chest Wall & Rib Pathology_: Tuberculous disease (cold abscess) arising from underlying ribs, costal cartilages, or intercostal lymph nodes (_Mycobacterium tuberculosis_).
    3. _Empyema Necessitans_: Deep intrapleural empyema tracking outward through the chest wall into the retromammary space.

---

### Clinical Features

- **Key Symptoms**: Deep-seated, continuous throbbing chest and breast pain; marked high-grade fever with chills/rigors; feeling of heaviness in the breast.
- **Physical Signs**:
    - **"Breast on a Pedestal" Appearance**: The entire intact breast is pushed forward (projected anteriorly) and elevated away from the chest wall by the tense fluid collection behind it.
    - **Absence of Superficial Skin Signs**: Overlying breast skin appears largely normal or only minimally hyperemic, with **no early surface fluctuation** due to the thick overlying glandular tissue.
    - **Pectoral Movement Pain**: Extreme pain on active contraction or stretching of the pectoralis major muscle (e.g., when the patient presses her hand against her hip or moves her arm).

---

### Diagnosis & Management

- **Investigation of Choice**: **Targeted Breast Ultrasound (USG)** confirms a fluid collection in the retromammary space behind the posterior capsule of the breast gland.
- **Surgical Treatment (Gaillard Thomas Incision)**:
    - **Procedure**: Drained via a curvilinear **submammary crease incision (Gaillard Thomas incision)** along the inferior fold of the breast.
    - **Steps**: The breast tissue is elevated off the pectoralis major fascia to enter the retromammary space, loculi are broken using finger/sinus forceps (Hilton's method), pus is evacuated and sent for C&S/GeneXpert, and a corrugated rubber drain is placed.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Location**: Space between posterior breast capsule and pectoralis major fascia.
- **Classic Sign**: Whole breast pushed forward (**"breast on a pedestal"**); pain on pectoral muscle contraction.
- **Surgical Drainage**: **Submammary crease incision (Gaillard Thomas incision)** along the lower fold.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_A retromammary abscess is a deep pus collection in the loose areolar space between the posterior breast parenchyma and the pectoralis major fascia, characterized classically by forward projection of the entire breast ("breast on a pedestal") and managed by surgical drainage via a submammary Gaillard Thomas incision._
