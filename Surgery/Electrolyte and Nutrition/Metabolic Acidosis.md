# Metabolic Acidosis

### Definition

**Metabolic acidosis** is a systemic acid-base disturbance characterized by a primary reduction in serum arterial pH (<7.35) and standard bicarbonate concentration (<22 mmol/L) accompanied by a base deficit (>4 to 6 mmol/L), resulting from the accumulation of endogenous organic acids (such as lactic acid) or excessive loss of bicarbonate.

---

- **Surgical Etiology [⭐ High-Yield]**: Driven primarily by tissue hypoperfusion and cellular hypoxia in shock (hypovolaemic, septic, cardiogenic, or obstructive), acute mesenteric ischemia, crush injury, severe acute pancreatitis, gas gangrene, renal failure, or metabolic poisoning (cyanide/carbon monoxide).
- **Pathophysiology & Lethal Triad**: Cellular oxygen deprivation forces a switch from aerobic to anaerobic respiration, producing lactic acid; in trauma and severe shock, severe acidosis combines with hypothermia and coagulopathy to form the life-threatening **"lethal triad" (trauma-induced coagulopathy)**.
- **Compensatory Mechanism**: Hypoxia and acidemia trigger compensatory hyperventilation (increased respiratory rate and minute ventilation) to blow off CO₂ and generate a compensatory respiratory alkalosis.
- **Diagnostic & Prognostic Markers**: Measured via arterial blood gas (ABG); a **base deficit >6 mmol/L** or **serum lactate >5 mmol/L** indicates severe occult hypoperfusion, high morbidity, and increased risk of multiple organ failure and death.
- **Management Principles**: Managed by urgent fluid resuscitation and oxygenation to restore microvascular tissue perfusion, definitive surgical control of underlying pathology (e.g., control of bleeding or bowel ischemia), and Damage Control Surgery (DCS) in physiologically exhausted patients.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: Systemic acid-base deficit with arterial pH <7.35, standard HCO₃⁻ <22 mmol/L, and base deficit >4–6 mmol/L.
- **Primary Cause in Surgery**: Tissue hypoperfusion and anaerobic metabolism producing lactic acid during shock, severe trauma, or sepsis.
- **Lethal Triad Component**: Acidosis + Hypothermia + Coagulopathy in severe trauma/hemorrhage.
- **Compensation**: Hyperventilation to excrete CO₂ (compensatory respiratory alkalosis).
- **Treatment Focus**: Re-establish tissue perfusion via fluid resuscitation and damage control surgery rather than routine sodium bicarbonate infusion.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Metabolic acidosis is a systemic acid-base disorder defined by a pH <7.35 and base deficit >4 mmol/L, caused in surgical practice by tissue hypoperfusion and cellular anaerobic respiration with lactic acid accumulation, requiring rapid hemodynamic resuscitation to restore microvascular perfusion._
