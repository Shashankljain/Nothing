# Vagotomy

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Vagotomy** is the surgical transection of the vagus nerve trunks or their specific gastric branches to abolish neurogenic (cephalic) stimulation of gastric parietal cells, thereby reducing maximal gastric acid output by approximately 50% in acid-peptic disease.

---

### Key Exam Bullets [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Primary Indications**: Intractable or complicated duodenal ulcers and Johnson Type II / Type III gastric ulcers associated with acid hypersecretion.
- **Truncal Vagotomy (TV) + Drainage**: Complete transection of both anterior and posterior vagal trunks at the distal esophagus. Because it denervates the antropyloric sphincter and causes gastric stasis, it **mandates a concurrent drainage procedure** (e.g., Heineke–Mikulicz pyloroplasty or gastroenterostomy).
- **Highly Selective Vagotomy (HSV / Parietal Cell Vagotomy)**: Divides only the nerve fibers supplying the parietal cell-bearing mass (body and fundus) while preserving the **nerves of Latarjet** and terminal **crow's foot** supplying the antrum and pylorus; **no drainage procedure is required**.
- **Criminal Nerve of Grassi**: The first branch of the posterior vagal trunk supplying the gastric fundus; failure to divide this nerve during vagotomy is the primary cause of recurrent ulceration.
- **Post-Vagotomy Complications**: **Postvagotomy diarrhea** (≈5% in TV due to extragastric visceral denervation), **gallstone formation** (secondary to gallbladder denervation via the hepatic branch), and recurrent ulceration (highest in HSV at 2–10%, lowest in TV + antrectomy at 1%).

---

### Quick Revision

- **Core Principle**: Transection of vagal parasympathetic input to reduce parietal cell acid production by ≈50%.
- **Truncal Vagotomy**: Requires a drainage procedure (pyloroplasty) due to pyloric denervation and gastric stasis.
- **Highly Selective Vagotomy**: Denervates parietal cell mass only, preserving the crow's foot; no drainage needed.
- **Major Sequelae**: Postvagotomy diarrhea, gallstones (TV), and recurrent ulceration if the criminal nerve of Grassi is missed.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Vagotomy is an acid-reducing surgical procedure involving the transection of the vagus nerves or their gastric branches to abolish cephalic acid secretion, classified into truncal, selective, and highly selective techniques based on the preservation of pyloric motility and extragastric visceral branches._
