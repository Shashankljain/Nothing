# Immunological Basis of Transplantation

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Transplant immunology** encompasses the cellular and humoral immune responses elicited when recipient T lymphocytes and B lymphocytes recognize non-self Human Leukocyte Antigens (HLA) on a donor allograft, initiating effector pathways that mediate hyperacute, acute, or chronic allograft rejection.

---

### Immunological Mechanisms of Rejection [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC / HLA System)** [⭐ High-Yield]: Encoded on chromosome 6, highly polymorphic Class I (HLA-A, -B, -C on all nucleated cells) and Class II (HLA-DR, -DP, -DQ on antigen-presenting cells) glycoproteins act as primary target antigens. Mismatches at the HLA-DR locus exert the strongest immunogenic effect, followed by HLA-B and HLA-A.
- **Antigen Presentation Pathways (Direct vs. Indirect)** [⭐ High-Yield]:
    - _Direct Pathway_: Donor passenger APCs present intact donor HLA directly to recipient T cells; drives acute cell-mediated rejection during the first 6–12 weeks.
    - _Indirect Pathway_: Recipient APCs process shed donor HLA into peptide fragments and present them on self-MHC Class II; drives long-term chronic antibody-mediated rejection.
- **Three-Signal T-Cell Activation Cascade** [⭐ High-Yield]:
    - _Signal 1_: Engagement of T-cell receptor (TCR) with the MHC-antigen complex.
    - _Signal 2 (Co-stimulation)_: Interaction between CD28 on the T cell and CD80/CD86 on the APC.
    - _Signal 3_: Interleukin-2 (IL-2) binding to up-regulated CD25 (IL-2 receptor), triggering clonal T-cell proliferation and CD8⁺ cytotoxic differentiation.
- **Cell-Mediated vs. Antibody-Mediated Effector Mechanisms** [⭐ High-Yield]:
    - _Cell-Mediated Rejection (CMR)_: Infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells release perforin (creates membrane pores) and granzymes (activates caspases), inducing cell apoptosis and interstitial lymphocytic tubulitis.
    - _Antibody-Mediated Rejection (AMR)_: Preformed or de novo Donor-Specific Antibodies (DSAs) bind graft endothelial cells, triggering complement activation, peritubular capillary C4d deposition, and microvascular thrombosis.

---

### Quick Revision

- **HLA System**: Encoded on chromosome 6; HLA-DR mismatches produce the strongest immunogenic rejection response.
- **Direct vs Indirect**: Direct presentation (donor APCs) mediates early acute CMR; indirect presentation (recipient APCs) mediates chronic AMR.
- **Three Activation Signals**: Signal 1 (TCR-MHC), Signal 2 (CD28-CD80/86 co-stimulation), Signal 3 (IL-2-CD25 binding).
- **Cytotoxic Mechanisms**: Perforin/granzyme release by CD8⁺ T cells (CMR) vs DSA binding with C4d complement deposition (AMR).

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_The immunological basis of transplantation rests on T-cell and B-cell recognition of foreign donor HLA antigens via direct or indirect presentation pathways, initiating a three-signal activation cascade that drives cell-mediated perforin/granzyme destruction or antibody-mediated complement-dependent vascular rejection._
