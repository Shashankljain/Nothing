# Principles of Organ Transplantation

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Organ transplantation** is the surgical transfer of a viable organ (allograft or autograft) from a donor to a recipient to restore lost physiological function in end-stage organ failure.

---

### Core Principles of Organ Transplantation [⭐ High-Yield]

- **ABO Compatibility & HLA Histocompatibility Matching** [⭐ High-Yield]: Absolute ABO blood group compatibility between donor and recipient is mandatory to prevent complement-mediated hyperacute rejection. Tissue typing matches Human Leukocyte Antigens (HLA) at the HLA-A, HLA-B, and HLA-DR loci, where minimizing mismatches significantly reduces immune recognition and prolongs graft survival.
- **Organ Preservation & Ischemia Reduction** [⭐ High-Yield]: Donor organs are preserved between retrieval and implantation using static cold storage at 4 °C with preservation solutions (such as University of Wisconsin solution) or hypothermic/normothermic machine perfusion to suppress metabolism and prevent cellular ischemia-reperfusion injury.
- **Immunological Activation Pathways**: T-lymphocyte activation against foreign graft antigens requires three distinct signals: Signal 1 (T-cell receptor recognition of MHC-antigen complex), Signal 2 (co-stimulation via T-cell CD28 binding to APC CD80/86), and Signal 3 (IL-2 binding to CD25), driving clonal expansion of cytotoxic CD8+ T cells.
- **Dual-Phase Immunosuppressive Therapy** [⭐ High-Yield]: Clinical immunosuppression comprises an **induction phase** using intravenous antibodies (e.g., anti-CD25 basiliximab or antithymocyte globulin) and a **maintenance phase** utilizing triple therapy (a calcineurin inhibitor such as tacrolimus or ciclosporin, an antiproliferative agent such as mycophenolic acid or azathioprine, and corticosteroids).
- **Rejection Surveillance & Treatment**: Rejection is categorized into hyperacute (intravascular thrombosis), acute cell-mediated (tubulitis/interstitial infiltrate treated with high-dose methylprednisolone), acute antibody-mediated (C4d deposition treated with plasma exchange and rituximab), and chronic allograft dysfunction.

---

### Quick Revision

- ABO compatibility and minimal HLA (A, B, DR) mismatches prevent hyperacute and acute allograft rejection.
- Organ preservation relies on hypothermia at 4 °C (UW solution) or machine perfusion to limit ischemic cellular damage.
- T-cell activation requires 3 signals (TCR engagement, CD28-CD80/86 co-stimulation, and IL-2/CD25 signaling).
- Standard maintenance immunosuppression employs triple therapy (calcineurin inhibitor + antiproliferative + steroid).

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

Organ transplantation relies on strict donor-recipient ABO and HLA histocompatibility matching, effective organ preservation to minimize ischemia-reperfusion injury, and targeted dual-phase immunosuppression to prevent graft rejection and restore long-term physiological function.
