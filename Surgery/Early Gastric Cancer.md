[[C43- Stomach]]
# Early Gastric Cancer

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Early Gastric Cancer (EGC)** is defined pathologically as an adenocarcinoma of the stomach confined strictly to the **mucosa (T1a)** or **submucosa (T1b)**, **regardless of the presence or absence of regional lymph node metastases (T1, any N)**.

---

### High-Yield Exam Bullets [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Japanese Morphological Classification [⭐ High-Yield]**:
    - **Type I (Protruded)**: Exophytic tumor projecting prominently into the gastric lumen.
    - **Type II (Superficial)**: Subdivided into **Type IIa** (Elevated), **Type IIb** (Flat), and **Type IIc** (Depressed — the most common variant).
    - **Type III (Excavated)**: Deep ulcerated lesion breaching mucosal continuity.
- **Prognostic Profile**: Characterized by an exceptionally favorable outcome compared to advanced gastric cancer, achieving a **5-year overall survival rate >90%** following appropriate therapy.
- **Diagnostic Gold Standard**: **Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS)** paired with high-definition chromoendoscopy or Narrow-Band Imaging (NBI) is the investigation of choice to precisely define wall layer integrity and differentiate intramucosal (T1a) from submucosal (T1b) depth.
- **Management Protocol [⭐ High-Yield]**:
    - **Intramucosal Cancers (T1a)**: Managed curatively via organ-preserving **Endoscopic Mucosal Resection (EMR)** or **Endoscopic Submucosal Dissection (ESD)** if lesions are well-differentiated, non-ulcerated, and lack lymphovascular invasion.
    - **Submucosal Cancers (T1b)**: Warrant formal **surgical gastrectomy with D1/D2 lymphadenectomy** because submucosal invasion carries a substantial risk (~10%–20%) of regional lymph node metastasis.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Definition**: Cancer limited to gastric mucosa (T1a) or submucosa (T1b), irrespective of lymph node status.
- **Japanese Classification**: Type I (Protruded), Type II (Superficial: IIa elevated, IIb flat, IIc depressed), Type III (Excavated).
- **Survival Outcome**: Highly curable with 5-year survival rates >90%.
- **Definitive Treatment**: EMR/ESD for T1a intramucosal disease; formal gastrectomy + D1/D2 lymph node dissection for T1b submucosal involvement.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Early gastric cancer is an adenocarcinoma strictly limited to the gastric mucosa or submucosa regardless of regional lymph node involvement, categorized by the Japanese morphological classification, staged using endoscopic ultrasound, and achieving a 5-year survival rate exceeding 90% following organ-preserving endoscopic or radical surgical resection._
