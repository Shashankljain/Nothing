# Colonoscopy — Indications

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Colonoscopy** is an endoscopic diagnostic and therapeutic procedure in which a flexible endoscope (colonoscope) is passed transanally to visualize the entire mucosal lining of the large bowel up to the cecum and terminal ileum, allowing direct mucosal inspection, tissue biopsy, and therapeutic interventions.

---

### Indications for Colonoscopy [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Investigation of Lower Gastrointestinal Symptoms**:
    - Unexplained rectal bleeding following non-diagnostic proctoscopy or sigmoidoscopy.
    - Unexplained alteration in bowel habits or abdominal pain related to bowel actions.
    - Chronic diarrhea (>6 weeks duration) following sigmoidoscopy, rectal biopsy, and negative celiac serology.
    - Iron deficiency anemia (performed in combination with upper gastrointestinal endoscopy / OGD).
    - Diagnostic work-up of a right iliac fossa mass when imaging suggests a colonic origin.
- **Colorectal Cancer (CRC) & Polyp Screening and Surveillance**:
    - Screening individuals with a positive family history of colorectal cancer or hereditary polyposis syndromes (e.g., FAP, Lynch syndrome).
    - Post-polypectomy surveillance and post-resection follow-up after colorectal cancer surgery.
    - Primary screening tool in population-based colorectal cancer screening programs or following a positive fecal immunochemical test (FIT).
- **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) Management**:
    - Assessment of the extent and inflammatory activity of Ulcerative Colitis and Crohn's disease, and differentiating between colitic subtypes.
    - Long-term cancer surveillance in patients with longstanding extensive colitis or pancolitis (>10 years duration) or co-existing primary sclerosing cholangitis.
- **Therapeutic Endoscopic Interventions**:
    - Resection of adenomatous or serrated polyps via cold/hot snare polypectomy, Endoscopic Mucosal Resection (EMR), or Endoscopic Submucosal Dissection (ESD).
    - Obliteration of symptomatic colonic angiodysplasias using argon plasma coagulation (APC).
    - Balloon dilatation of benign or anastomotic colonic strictures.
    - Placement of self-expanding metal stents (SEMS) for palliation or pre-operative decompression of malignant large bowel obstruction.
- **Evaluation of Radiological Abnormalities & Miscellaneous Surveillance**:
    - Direct visualization, optical diagnosis, and biopsy of lesions or strictures identified on CT colonography or barium enema.
    - Colorectal screening and surveillance in patients with acromegaly or following ureterosigmoidostomy.

---

### Quick Revision

- **Core Diagnostic Role**: Gold-standard investigation for unexplained rectal bleeding, chronic diarrhea (>6 weeks), iron deficiency anemia, and altered bowel habit.
- **Oncological Surveillance**: Essential for CRC screening in family history, post-polypectomy follow-up, and long-term IBD surveillance (>10 years pancolitis).
- **Therapeutic Capabilities**: Polypectomy, EMR/ESD, stricture balloon dilatation, APC for angiodysplasia, and colonic stenting for obstruction.
- **Quality Benchmark**: Complete examination requires a cecal intubation rate ≥90%, confirmed by visual identification of the appendiceal orifice, triradiate fold, or terminal ileal intubation.

---

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Colonoscopy is a flexible endoscopic procedure offering complete luminal visualization, targeted tissue biopsy, and therapeutic intervention from the rectum to the terminal ileum, serving as the gold-standard investigation for lower gastrointestinal bleeding, altered bowel habit, IBD evaluation, and colorectal cancer screening._
