# Complications of Inguinal Hernia

### Definition [⭐ High-Yield]

**Complications of an inguinal hernia** encompass acute or chronic pathological events arising from structural entrapment of abdominal contents within the hernia sac (irreducibility, mechanical obstruction, strangulation, and infarction) as well as adverse surgical outcomes following hernia repair (chronic groin neuralgia, seroma, ischemic orchitis, and recurrence).

---

### Major Complications [⭐ High-Yield]

- **Irreducibility & Incarceration** [⭐ High-Yield]: Entrapment of herniated bowel loops or omentum within the sac caused by a narrow fibrous neck or intra-saccular adhesions, preventing reduction back into the peritoneal cavity.
- **Mechanical Bowel Obstruction** [⭐ High-Yield]: Occlusion of the intestinal lumen within the trapped bowel loop, presenting with the cardinal quartet of colicky abdominal pain, bilious/feculent vomiting, central abdominal distension, and absolute constipation.
- **Strangulation & Infarction** [⭐ High-Yield]: Tight constriction at the neck selectively impedes low-pressure venous return, producing rapid bowel wall edema, elevated intramural tension, arterial inflow occlusion, hemorrhagic infarction, gangrene, and toxic bowel perforation.
- **Postoperative Chronic Groin Neuralgia** [⭐ High-Yield]: Irritation, entrapment, or surgical transection of the ilioinguinal, iliohypogastric, or genital branch of the genitofemoral nerve by sutures or mesh, causing persistent burning neuralgic pain (>3 months postop) in up to 20% of open repairs.
- **Ischemic Testicular Atrophy & Hernia Recurrence**: Thrombosis of the pampiniform venous plexus or accidental division of the testicular artery during cord dissection leading to testicular infarction, alongside technical failure or mesh migration causing hernia recurrence (<5% in specialist centers) [64–67].

---

### Quick Revision

- **Pathological Complications**: Incarceration (irreducible) → Luminal Obstruction (transit blocked) → Strangulation (vascular ischemia & gangrene).
- **Strangulation Hallmark**: Severe continuous pain, exquisite local tenderness, tense irreducible mass, **absent expansile cough impulse**, and purplish skin discoloration.
- **Chronic Groin Pain**: Neuralgic pain persisting >3 months secondary to ilioinguinal, iliohypogastric, or genitofemoral nerve entrapment.
- **Ischemic Orchitis**: Follows excessive cord dissection causing venous plexus thrombosis or testicular artery laceration.

### One-Line Exam Opener

_Complications of inguinal hernia comprise acute intra-saccular events including irreducibility, mechanical bowel obstruction, and strangulating hemorrhagic infarction of entrapped viscera, alongside postoperative morbidities such as chronic groin neuralgia, ischemic testicular atrophy, and repair recurrence._
