[[Trace evidence]]

🧪🐰 **PRECIPITIN TEST (3-MARK MODEL ANSWER)** 🐰🧪

---

### **1. Definition & Principle** 📚

- **Definition:** An immunological serological test used primarily for **species identification** (determining whether a blood or tissue stain is of ==human or animal origin==).
- **Biochemical Principle:** Based on a classic **antigen-antibody reaction**.
    - A host animal (typically a rabbit) is immunized with human serum protein (antigen), stimulating its immune system to produce anti-human antibodies (**antiserum**).
    - When the harvested antiserum is brought into contact with the questioned stain extract, they diffuse toward each other, forming a visible **insoluble complex (precipitate line)** at the zone of equivalence.

---

### **2. The Classic Method: Ring Precipitin Test** 🧪

- **Procedure:** Anti-human antiserum (dense) is placed in a small capillary tube, and a dilute extract of the questioned bloodstain is gently layered on top.
- **Interpretation:**
    - ★ **Positive (Human Match):** A clear, fine white **ring of precipitate** forms at the interface of the two liquids.
    - **Negative (Non-Human):** No precipitate ring forms.

---

### **3. Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Species Differentiation:** Serves as the standard confirmatory test to prove a crime-scene stain is **human blood or semen** rather than animal blood.
- ★ **Mutilated & Fragmented Remains:** Crucial during autopsies of decomposed bodies, building collapses, or explosions to identify if **isolated fragments of muscles or bone extracts** are of human origin.
- **Alternative Techniques:** Other variants include the **Ouchterlony gel double diffusion** method, **crossed-over electrophoresis**, and the **latex agglutination test**.
