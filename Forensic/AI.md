[[Impotence and sterility]]
### **Basic Concepts** 🧬

#### **Definition**

**Artificial Insemination (AI)** is defined as the artificial introduction of semen into the female genital tract with the purpose of achieving pregnancy. It is a form of **Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)**.

#### **Introduction**

- It is used when natural conception is ==not== possible or advisable due to physical, anatomical, or immunological issues.
- It is a key topic in medical jurisprudence, specifically within family law, paternity disputes, and clinical ethics.

#### **Types of Artificial Insemination**

- **AI-Homologous (AIH):** The **husband's semen** is used.
    - _Consent Required:_ Mutual consent of **Husband and Wife**.
- **AI-Donor (AID):** Semen from an **anonymous donor** is used.
    - _Consent Required:_ Consent of **Wife + Husband + Donor + Donor's Wife** is legally mandatory.
- **AIHD:** Artificial Insemination using **pooled semen** (husband's semen combined with donor's semen).

### **Donor Eligibility Criteria (for AID)** 👨‍⚕️

To minimize genetic and legal complications, a semen donor must meet the following criteria:

1. Must be **married**.
2. Should have at least **one healthy child** (proving biological fertility).
3. **No Rh incompatibility**.
4. Must be **unrelated** to the recipient couple.
5. Age must be **less than 40 years**.
6. Preferably of the **same race** as the couple.

---

### **Medico-Legal Importance** ⚖️

The forensic and legal implications of artificial insemination are extensive and heavily tested:

1. **Nullity of Marriage:** If AI was performed because of the husband’s impotency, but the marriage was never naturally consummated, **nullity of marriage** can still be ==granted== in court on grounds of impotency.
2. **Divorce:** If the wife undergoes AI without her husband's written consent, it constitutes mental cruelty and is a valid ground for the husband to file for **divorce**.
3. **Legitimacy:** A child born via consensual AI (with the husband's consent) is legally considered the **legitimate child**of the couple.
4. **Adultery:** AI is **not considered adultery** because no physical coitus or sexual union occurs between the female and the donor.
5. **Incest Risk:** A remote risk of ==accidental== ==incest== exists if offspring from the same donor's semen marry each other without knowing their biological relationship. To prevent this, semen from a single donor is restricted to a **==maximum of 10 children==**.
6. **Litigation:** If a child born via AI has congenital defects or hereditary diseases, the couple can **sue the doctor** for negligence if proper donor screening was ==not== performed.

### **Relevant Law (Must Include) 📜**

#### **1. Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023**

- ★ **Section 116 BSA (Presumption of Legitimacy):** Any child born during the continuance of a valid marriage is presumed to be legitimate unless non-access is proven. In the case of consensual AI, this presumption protects the child’s legal status.
- **Section 39 BSA (Expert Opinion):** Medical reports and DNA testing are admissible as expert evidence in court.

#### **2. Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**

- **Section 130 BNS (Consent and Assault):** Performing AI or extracting bodily fluids without proper informed consent is punishable as criminal assault.
- **Section 228 / 229 BNS (Fabricating Evidence):** Falsifying donor profiles or medical records is punishable under laws governing the fabrication of false evidence.

#### **3. Surrogacy & ART Regulation Acts (2021, 2022)**

- **Commercial surrogacy is banned** in India; only **altruistic surrogacy** is permitted (where only medical/hospital expenses and insurance are covered for up to 3 years).
- **Eligibility for Surrogacy:** Intending female must be **23–50 years**, male **26–55 years**, and they must be an Indian married couple with **5 years of infertility**.
- **Sex selection is strictly prohibited** and legally punishable.

**Precautions to be Taken by the Doctor** ⚠️

When conducting Artificial Insemination (especially **AI-Donor**), the doctor must adhere to strict clinical, ethical, and legal safeguards to avoid catastrophic legal disputes or professional misconduct charges.

As detailed in **FMT Marrow Page 136 (Impotence, Virginity & Delivery)**, the primary precautions are:

**1. Consent Requirements**

- ★ **Important Exam Point (The Signature Rule):**
    - For **AI-Homologous (AIH)**, written informed consent must be obtained from **both the Husband and Wife**.
    - For **AI-Donor (AID)**, written informed consent is absolutely mandatory from **four parties**: the **Wife**, the **Husband**, the **Semen Donor**, and the **Donor's Wife**. The consent of the donor's wife is essential to prevent future disputes regarding matrimonial discord or inheritance claims.

**2. Donor Selection & Health Screening**

- **Physical & Mental Health:** The donor must be thoroughly screened and certified as **mentally and physically healthy**.
- **Age Limit:** The donor must be young, with **no risk of genetic disease**, and his age must be **under 40 years**.
- **Consanguinity Exclusion:** The donor must **not be a relative** of either spouse to prevent congenital and genetic abnormalities in the offspring.

**3. Biological & Phenotypic Matching**

- **Race & Physical Characteristics:** The donor’s race, complexion, and physical characteristics must **resemble the husband's** as closely as possible to maintain familial harmony and avoid suspicion regarding the child's origin.
- **Blood Group Matching:** The donor’s **blood group must be the same as the husband’s** (or compatible) to prevent Rh incompatibility issues and to maintain the legal presumption of the husband's paternity.

**4. Limiting Offspring (Avoiding Incest Risk)**

- ★ **Important Exam Point (The Single Donor Cap):** Semen from a **single donor must be used to produce a maximum of 10 children**. Exceeding this number increases the risk of accidental consanguineous marriages (incest) among half-siblings in the future.

**5. Professional Boundaries (Role Segregation)**

- **Obstetric Care Restriction:** The **physician administering the artificial insemination must not deliver the child**. The delivery must be handled by another independent obstetrician to prevent allegations of fraud, conflict of interest, or illegal paternity claims against the inseminating doctor.
