[[Identification]]
### **Gustafson’s Method of Age Estimation**

---

#### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition** **Gustafson’s Method** is a forensic dental technique used to estimate the age of an adult individual by observing ==physiological changes== in the teeth that occur with ==advancing== age [1-p108, 4-p35].

**Introduction** Introduced by Gosta Gustafson in 1950, this method is primarily used for identifying deceased individuals or skeletal remains [4-p36]. It is one of the most widely accepted dental methods for age estimation in the adult population, typically between **==25 and 60 years==** of age [2-p10, 4-p35].

**Objective** The main objective is to provide a mathematical estimate of a person’s age at the time of death based on the cumulative "wear and tear" of their teeth [4-p35].

---

#### **The Six Criteria (Mnemonic: APSRTC)**

★ **Important Exam Point:** Gustafson identified six distinct age-related changes in teeth. In university exams, these are often remembered using the mnemonic **APSRTC** [1-p108].

|**Criterion**|**Description**|
|:--|:--|
|**A - Attrition**|The wearing down of the occlusal (chewing) surface due to mastication [4-p37].|
|**P - Periodontosis**|The retraction of the gum margin and loosening of the tooth, exposing the neck and roots [4-p37].|
|**S - Secondary Dentin**|The progressive infilling of the dental pulp cavity, which reduces its size over time [4-p37].|
|**R - Root Resorption**|The loss of cementum and dentin from the root apex, which is considered the least reliable parameter [4-p37].|
|**T - Transparency (Root)**|The sclerosis of dentinal tubules, making the root appear translucent; this is the **most reliable**parameter [1-p108, 2-p12, 4-p37].|
|**C - Cementum Apposition**|The gradual increase in the thickness of cementum around the root due to continuous deposition [4-p37].|

---

#### **Procedure and Scoring**

1. **Selection of Tooth:** The method is most accurate when performed on **anterior teeth** (incisors) [2-p10, 4-p36].
2. **Extraction:** This is a **destructive method** because the tooth must be extracted from the jaw [4-p36].
3. **Sectioning:** A thin longitudinal section (ground section) is prepared through the center of the tooth for microscopic examination [4-p35].
4. **Grading:** Each of the six criteria is assigned a score from **0 to 3** based on the severity of the change (0 = absent, 3 = maximum change) [1-p108, 4-p37, 4-p39].
5. **Calculation:** The scores are totaled, and the final age is calculated using a standard regression formula [4-p37].

---

#### **Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Identification:** It is a vital tool for determining the individuality of unknown dead bodies in criminal cases or mass disasters [4-p79, 4-p102].
- **Corpus Delicti:** Establishing the age of human remains helps prove the "body of the crime" in homicide investigations [4-p79].
- **Adult Age Estimation:** Since ossification centers of bones are usually fused by age 25, dental methods like Gustafson’s become the primary choice for older adults [4-p97].

---

#### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Applicable Age:** Adults, usually **>25 years** [2-p10].
- ★ **Most Reliable Criterion:** **Root Transparency** (Translucency) [1-p108, 2-p12, 4-p37].
- ★ **Least Reliable Criterion:** **Root Resorption** [4-p37].
- ★ **Unsuitable Teeth:** It is generally considered unsuitable for **3rd molars** (wisdom teeth) due to their high variability in development [1-p108, 2-p10].
- ★ **Nature of Method:** It is a **destructive method** as it requires tooth extraction and sectioning [4-p35, 4-p36].

#### **Viva Questions**

1. **What is the age range for Gustafson's method?**
    - _Ans:_ It is used for adults, typically between 25 and 60 years [4-p35].
2. **Which parameter in Gustafson's method is considered the "best"?**
    - _Ans:_ Root Transparency (Translucency) [1-p108, 2-p12].
3. **Why is it called a destructive method?**
    - _Ans:_ Because the tooth must be pulled out and cut into sections to be studied under a microscope [4-p36].
4. **What is the mnemonic for the six criteria?**
    - _Ans:_ **APSRTC** (Attrition, Periodontosis, Secondary dentin, Root resorption, Transparency, Cementum) [1-p108].

---

#### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **Used for:** Adults >25 years.
- **Sample:** Extracted anterior teeth (incisors).
- **Mnemonic:** **APSRTC**.
- **Scoring:** 0 to 3 for each of the 6 criteria.
- **Gold Standard:** Root Transparency is the most accurate indicator of age.
- **Limitation:** Error margin increases after the age of 50 [4-p36].
