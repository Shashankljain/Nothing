[[Medical law]]

1. ★ **Beneficence:** Actively promoting and doing what is in the absolute best interest and well-being of the patient.
2. ★ **Non-Maleficence (****Primum non nocere****):** "First, do no harm"—avoiding any act that can cause unnecessary pain, injury, or death.
3. **Autonomy:** Respecting the patient's absolute right to choose or refuse their own treatment.
4. **Justice:** Treating all patients equally, fairly, and without discrimination based on caste, religion, creed, or nationality
