**PART III: STEP-BY-STEP EXAMINATION PROCEDURE** 📋

A highly structured, examiner-friendly clinical workflow:

```
                            [EXAMINATION WORKFLOW]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                            ▼                            ▼
 [Consent & Prelims]          [Trace Evidence]             [Genital Exam]
• Document written consent.   • Strip on a white sheet.  • Lithotomy position.
• Informed refusal if denied. • Collect clothes & hairs. • Hymenoscope (5 & 7                                                                 o'clock).
```

**1. Consent and Preliminary Documentation**

- Obtain a detailed, signed, written informed consent [41, image/slide].
- ★ **Informed Refusal:** If the victim refuses the examination, the doctor must **document the refusal** clearly in writing; she cannot be forced [image/slide].
- Record her age, two permanent identification marks, and a detailed chronological history of the assault.

**2. Clothing & Trace Evidence Collection**

- Instruct the victim to undress while standing over a **clean white sheet** to catch loose trace evidence (hair, soil, fibers) [image/slide].
- Examine the clothes under filtered **UV light (Wood's Lamp)** to screen for fluorescent seminal stains [328, image/slide].
- Pack each garment separately in **breathable paper bags** (never airtight plastic bags, as moisture promotes fungal decay of semen) [326, image/slide].

**3. General Physical Examination**

- Note down her psychological state (excited, depressed, or cooperative).
- Search for physical injuries (abrasions, bruises, bite marks, defense wounds) on breasts, neck, inner thighs, and hands [249, 383, image/slide].
- ★ **Toluidine Blue Test:** Apply a $1\%$ aqueous solution of Toluidine Blue dye to the posterior fourchette and perineum to **stain and detect fresh, superficial micro-injuries** that are invisible to the naked eye [image/slide].

**4. Local Genital Examination**

- Place the victim in the **lithotomy position** [image/slide].
- ★ **Hymenal Examination:** Use **Glaister-Keen rods** or a **hymenoscope** to inspect the hymen [image/slide].
    - _Tearing Site:_ Acute tears usually occur postero-laterally between the **5 and 7 O'clock positions**[image/slide].
- ★ **Speculum & Colposcope:** Carefully insert a speculum to inspect the vaginal walls and cervix for internal lacerations, foreign bodies, or pooled semen [image/slide].

**1. The 4-Vaginal Swabs Protocol (< 3 Days)**

★ **Important Exam Point:** Within 3 days of the assault, the doctor must collect **four separate vaginal swabs** to perform the following investigations [image/slide]:

```
                                [4 VAGINAL SWABS]
                                       │
         ┌───────────────────┬─────────┴─────────┬───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼                   ▼
    [1. Smear]    [2. Sperm Motility]  [3. DNA Profiling]  [4. Chemical Test]
 • Harris's H&E or   • Checked in saline. • Short Tandem    • Quantitative Acid
   Christmas Tree    • Motile: 6-12 hours  Repeats (STRs)      Phosphatase and
   stains for sperms.  post-coitus.        for individual.     p30 (PSA) assay.
```

1. **Vaginal Smear:** Stained with **Harris's Hematoxylin & Eosin (H&E)** or **"Christmas Tree" stain** to visualize intact spermatozoa [332, image/slide].
2. **Sperm Motility Swab:** Examined immediately in normal saline. At body temperature, spermatozoa retain full motility in the vagina for **6 to 12 hours** [344, image/slide].
3. **DNA Profiling Swab:** Sent for high-resolution **Short Tandem Repeat (STR)** typing to match the suspect's genetic profile2more_horiz.
4. **Chemical/Non-Cellular Swab:** Evaluates:
    - _Acid Phosphatase:_ Undiluted semen has massive levels. High vaginal levels confirm semen deposition5.
    - _Prostate-Specific Antigen (PSA / p30):_ Diagnostic glycoprotein derived from the prostate56.
