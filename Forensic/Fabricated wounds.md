[[Mechanical injuries]]
**Self-inflicted injuries**, also known as **fabricated**, **fictitious**, or **forged wounds**, are a vital topic in forensic traumatology. They represent injuries produced by a person on their own body, or by another with their consent, to support a false charge or to achieve a specific motive.

---

### **1. Basic Concepts**

**Definition** Fabricated wounds are injuries intentionally produced by an individual on their own body (self-inflicted) or by another person with their consent (self-suffered) to ==simulate== a ==criminal offence==.

**Classification**

1. **Self-inflicted wounds:** Inflicted by the person on their own body. Without suicidal intent, this is a form of self-mutilation.
2. **Self-suffered wounds:** Inflicted by another person on the alleged victim with the latter's consent.

**Medico-Legal Importance**

- To **incriminate an enemy** with a false charge of assault or attempted murder to ensure they receive more punishment.
- To **simulate a crime** (e.g., women bringing a false charge of rape).
- To **avert suspicion** by destroying evidence of a real injury that might link a person to a crime.
- To **escape duty** or service (e.g., soldiers or prisoners).
- For **insurance fraud**.

---

### **2. Relevant Law (2023)**

**Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**

- **Section 2(14):** Defines **Injury** as any harm illegally caused to a person.
- **Section 227:** Relates to **Perjury** (giving false evidence).
- **Section 228:** Relates to **Fabricating false evidence**.
- **Section 234:** Punishment for **Issuing false certificates**.

**Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023**

- **Section 39:** **Expert Opinion**. The medical officer's findings identifying these wounds as fabricated are admissible in court.

---

### **3. Characteristics of Fabricated Injuries**

A medical officer must look for specific "classic" features to distinguish these from genuine assault:

- **★ Important Exam Point: Nature of Wounds:** Most commonly **incised wounds** (cuts). Occasionally superficial burns, contusions, or small stab wounds are seen.
- **Severity:** Usually **multiple, shallow, and non-penetrating** (limited to the skin).
- **Site (Sites of Election):** Found on **accessible parts** of the body that the individual can easily reach, such as the top of the head, forehead, neck, outer left arm, front of thighs, or chest.
- **Direction and Arrangement:** Often **grouped, parallel, or in a criss-cross** pattern. They are uniform in shape and follow a linear or slightly curved course.
- **Avoidance of Pain:** Wounds typically **avoid pain-sensitive or vital areas** like the eyes, nose, mouth, ears, or genitals.
- **Clothing:** Usually, the **clothes are not damaged** or the damage does not correspond to the site of the injury (as the person moves clothes aside before inflicting the wound).
- **Defense Wounds:** These are characteristically **absent**.

---

### **4. Medico-Legal Examination & Diagnosis**

The diagnosis is based on the **discrepancy** between the alleged history provided by the victim and the actual physical findings.

**Table: True Bruise vs. Artificial Bruise**

|**Feature**|**True Bruise (Contusion)**|**Artificial Bruise**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Cause**|Blunt force trauma|Juices of marking nut (_Semicarpus anacardium_) or Calotropis|
|**Site**|Any part of the body|Only on **accessible parts**|
|**Margins**|Ill-defined|**Well-defined**|
|**Itching**|Absent|**Present** (due to acrid serum)|
|**Contents**|Extravasated blood|Acrid serum/Blisters|
|**Color Changes**|Red \(\rightarrow\) Blue \(\rightarrow\) Green \(\rightarrow\) Yellow|No sequence; usually dark brown|

---

### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Commonest type of injury fabricated:** **Incised wounds**.
- ★ **Commonest site for self-infliction:** The **left side** of the body (for right-handed persons).
- ★ **Marking Nut (Bhilawa):** The most common substance used in India to create artificial bruises for false accusations.
- ★ **History-Injury Discrepancy:** The hallmark sign of a fabricated injury case.

---

### **Quick Revision Notes**

- **What?** Injuries created to fake a crime.
- **Who?** The individual themselves or a consenting partner.
- **Motive?** Mostly to charge an enemy or avoid duty.
- **Signs:** Superficial, parallel, accessible sites, no cloth damage, no defense wounds.
- **Law:** BNS Section 228 (Fabricating evidence).
