[[Impotence and sterility]]
**Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️

The medico-legal significance of these conditions is tested heavily under civil, criminal, and personal laws in India.

**I. Nullity of Marriage (Annulment) vs. Divorce**

- **Voidable Marriage:** Impotence existing **at the time of marriage** and continuing up to the institution of legal proceedings makes a marriage **voidable** (it can be annulled by the court, rendering it legally non-existent from the start).
- **Grounds for Divorce:** If impotence or frigidity develops **after the marriage** has been consummated, it does not annul the marriage but acts as a valid ground for legal separation or **divorce** under personal laws (e.g., the Hindu Marriage Act).

**II. Presumption of Legitimacy and Paternity Disputes**

- If a husband can prove he is sterile (via semen analysis showing absolute azoospermia) or impotent, he can legally ==dispute== the paternity of a child born to his wife and rebut the legal presumption of legitimacy.

**III. Defence Against Rape Accusations**

- ★ **Important Exam Point:** Under modern criminal law, a claim of impotence is **not a valid defense against a charge of rape**.
- _Why?_ The legal definition of rape does _not_ require full penile erection or ejaculation; the slightest penetration of the vulva, oral cavity, urethra, or anal canal by the penis, any body part, or an object constitutes rape.

**IV. Claims for Compensation**

- An individual may claim civil damages/compensation if they suffer from acquired impotence or sterility due to:
    - ==Industrial== or ==road traffic accidents== (e.g., pelvic fractures causing neurogenic or vasculogenic impotence).
    - ==Surgical== negligence (e.g., failed vasectomy/tubectomy, accidental ligation of the vas deferens, or pelvic nerve damage during surgery).

**V. Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART) and Surrogacy**

- ==Persistent== ==sterility== is the primary legal justification for couples seeking Artificial Insemination (AIH/AID), IVF, or Surrogacy, which are strictly regulated by Indian law to prevent commercial exploitation.

**3. Relevant Law (Must Include) 📜**

**A. Hindu Marriage Act (HMA), 1955**

- **Section 5:** Lays down the conditions for a valid marriage.
- **Sections 11 & 12 (Void vs. Voidable Marriages):**
    - _Void Marriages (Section 11):_ Legally ==invalid== from the ==very beginning== (e.g., bigamy, or marriages within prohibited degrees of relationship like father-daughter or mother-son).
    - _Voidable Marriages (Section 12):_ Valid ==until== ==declared== otherwise by a court. **Impotency** at the time of marriage is a prime example.
- **Section 13 (Divorce):** Allows ==legal dissolution of marriage== on grounds of post-marital ==physical== or ==mental== ==cruelty== (which can include refusal of intercourse due to frigidity or unconsummated marriage).

**B. Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023**

- ★ **Section 116 BSA (Presumption of Legitimacy):**
    - **Essential Ingredients:** Any child born during the continuance of a valid marriage, or within **280 days** after its dissolution (the mother remaining unmarried), is presumed to be the legitimate child of the husband.
    - **Rebuttal:** This presumption is a "conclusive proof" under law and can _only_ be rebutted by showing that the married parties had **no access** to each other at any time when the child could have been begotten, or by providing scientific proof of **physical impossibility** (such as absolute sterility or impotence of the husband).

**C. Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**

- **Section 63 (Definition of Rape):** Explains that any penetration (penile or non-penile) is sufficient, which is why impotence is legally rejected as a defense.
- **Section 130 (Criminal Force/Assault):** Subjecting an individual to forced medical or physical procedures to test for fertility without informed consent constitutes criminal assault.

**Impotence Quoad Hanc**

- **Definition:** A psychological phenomenon where a man is potent generally but is completely impotent toward **one particular woman** (often due to intense emotional aversion, fear, or stress).
- **Matrimonial Significance:** Legally accepted as a valid form of impotence for the dissolution or annulment of that specific marriage.

**Impotence Quoad Hunc**

- **Definition:** The female equivalent; where a woman is responsive generally but becomes rigid, frigid, or suffers from vaginismus when attempting intercourse with **one particular man**.

**Fecundation Ab Extra**

- **Definition:** "Conception from without." This occurs when ejaculation takes place near the female external genitalia (vulva or thighs) without vaginal penetration, and the highly motile spermatozoa travel up the vaginal canal and cervix to fertilize the ovum.
- **Medico-legal Significance:** A woman can be clinically certified as a **virgin** (with an intact hymen) but still be **pregnant**. This is vital to understand in cases of disputed chastity or paternity.
