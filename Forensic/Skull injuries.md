[[Regional injuries]]
**Skull Fracture:** A structural breach in the physical continuity of the cranial vault (vertex) or the cranial floor (base of the skull), occurring when applied mechanical energy exceeds the elastic limits of the bone tissue

**Medico-Legal Importance (MLI) of Skull Fractures** ⚖️

1. **Reconstruction of Events:** The location of a skull fracture helps determine the site of impact. Injuries at the "hat-brim line" suggest accidental falls, whereas those above this line suggest homicidal blows7.
2. **Identification of the Weapon:** Depressed (signature) fractures are crucial in homicidal trials as they often replicate the exact physical dimensions of the offending weapon (e.g., a hammer claw)7.
3. **Determination of Force:** Severe comminuted or hinge fractures indicate massive applied mechanical force, demonstrating intent to cause grievous harm or death1519.
4. **Chronological Sequencing (Puppe's Rule):** In cases of multiple head impacts, Puppe's Rule states that **a subsequent fracture line will never cross an existing, pre-existing fracture line**21. This allows the pathologist to determine the exact order in which blows were struck21.
