[[Abortion]]
Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) and Criminal Abortion Laws: University Study Guide 🩺📖

This guide is designed for **MBBS students** to master the legal and forensic aspects of pregnancy termination in India, focusing on the **MTP framework** and the transition to the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**.

---

1. Basic Concepts & Introduction 💡

In forensic medicine, the distinction between a legal procedure and a crime is paramount.

- **Legal Abortion (MTP):** Termination performed under the provisions of the MTP Act.
- **Criminal Abortion:** Any termination performed outside the legal parameters, now governed by the **BNS, 2023**.
- **Core Objective:** To balance the reproductive rights of the mother with the protection of the unborn child, while preventing the dangers of "back-alley" abortions.

★ **Important Exam Point:** Under **BNS 29**, consent for "offensive acts" is **not valid**. This means even if a woman consents to a criminal abortion, it remains a punishable offense.

---

2. The '6-W' Analytical Framework 🧐

|Question|Clinical/Legal Application|
|---|---|
|**What?**|The intentional termination of pregnancy before the fetus is capable of independent life.|
|**Why?**|To save the life of the mother, prevent fetal anomalies, or address humanitarian grounds.|
|**How?**|Medical or surgical means by a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP).|
|**When?**|Within the specific gestational limits defined by law (20–24 weeks).|
|**Where?**|Government hospitals or places approved by a district-level committee.|
|**Who?**|Qualified RMPs with specific training in Obstetrics and Gynecology.|

---

3. Indications for Termination 🚩

While the MTP Act provides specific categories, the **BNS, 2023** identifies the legal boundaries through the lens of criminal intent:

- **Therapeutic:** To save the life of the mother (**BNS 88** implies exceptions for acts done in "good faith").
- **Eugenic:** Substantial risk of the child suffering from physical or mental abnormalities.
- **Humanitarian:** Pregnancies resulting from sexual offenses (e.g., **Rape**, defined under **BNS 63**).
- **Social:** Failure of contraception (social-economic considerations).

---

4. Legal Framework: The BNS 2023 Transition ⚖️

The transition from IPC to **BNS** is a critical exam topic. The following table outlines the criminal offenses related to abortion as found in the new laws:

**Criminal Abortion Offenses (BNS 88–92)**

|Section (BNS)|Definition/Offense|Key Distinction|
|---|---|---|
|**88 BNS**|Criminal abortion **with** mother's consent|The act is illegal even if she agrees.|
|**89 BNS**|Abortion **without** mother's consent|Higher penalty; act done against her will.|
|**90 BNS**|Death of mother due to abortion|Causal link between the procedure and fatality.|
|**91 BNS**|Act done with intent to prevent a child from being born alive|Intent to cause death after birth or prevent live birth.|
|**92 BNS**|Death of an unborn child (Culpable Homicide)|For example: Hitting a pregnant mother in her abdomen.|

5. Rules for Consent 📝

Consent is the most frequently tested area in Viva Voce.

- **Age of Consent:** Under **BNS 25**, a person must be **> 18 years** to give valid consent for a medical act.
- **Minors/Mentally Ill:** Under **BNS 27**, acts done in good faith for a person **< 12 years** or those with mental illness require the consent of the **guardian**.
- **Invalid Consent (****BNS 28****):** Consent is not valid if obtained under **fear**, **misconception**, or from a person who is **intoxicated** or of unsound mind.
- **Husband's Consent:** Legally, the husband's consent is **NOT required**; the autonomy of the pregnant woman is paramount. ★

---

6. Practitioner Qualifications and Approved Places 🏥

To perform a legal termination, an RMP must meet specific criteria:

- **Qualifications:** Must possess a PG degree or diploma in OBG, or have completed **6 months** of OBG training.
- **Experience:** Requires **1 year** of experience in OBG or having assisted in **25 MTPs** (with at least **5 performed independently**).
- **Approved Places:**
    1. All Government hospitals.
    2. Private facilities approved by a **District Committee** chaired by the CMO.

---

7. Confidentiality, Documentation, and Penalties 🔒

Maintaining the privacy of the patient is a strict legal mandate.

- **Documentation:** Records must be kept securely. Under the BNSS, medical examinations (like those for sexual offenses under **184 BNSS**) must be reported within **7 days**.
- **Confidentiality:** Breach of patient identity (especially in cases of rape) is punishable under **Section 72 BNS** (2 years imprisonment/fine).
- **General Penalty:** Violations of specific reporting procedures can fall under **Section 200 BNS** (1 year imprisonment or fine). ★

---

8. Clinical Correlation & Real-Life Scenarios 🩺

**Scenario A:** A 22-year-old unmarried woman requests an abortion at 10 weeks.

- _Action:_ Consent is valid (**BNS 25**), and termination is legal if performed by a qualified RMP in an approved place.

**Scenario B:** A husband forces a doctor to terminate his wife's pregnancy against her will.

- _Legal Outcome:_ This is a major offense under **BNS 89** (Abortion without mother's consent). The husband and doctor are both liable.

---

9. High-Yield Exam Points & Mnemonics 🏆

**Mnemonics for BNS Abortion Sections**

- **88:** **E**ighty-**E**ight = **E**veryone agrees (Consent given, still a crime if not under MTP).
- **89:** **E**ighty-**N**ine = **N**o consent (Abortion without mother's consent).
- **90:** **N**inety = **N**ear Death (Death of mother due to abortion).

**Viva Questions**

1. **Q:** Is consent of the husband required for MTP? **A:** No.
2. **Q:** What is the age of criminal responsibility in India? **A:** Minimum **7 years** (**20 BNS**).
3. **Q:** What section covers the death of a mother during an abortion? **A:** **BNS 90**.
