[[Identification]]

### **Rule of Hasse**

---

#### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition** The **Rule of Hasse** is a mathematical formula used in forensic medicine to estimate the **gestational age** of a fetus in lunar months based on its **Crown-Heel Length (CHL)** [1-p102, 3-p30].

**Introduction** In cases of abortion, stillbirth, or unidentified fetal remains, determining the age is critical. Since the length of the fetus follows a predictable pattern during development, Hasse's Rule provides a quick and reliable estimation [4-p137].

---

#### **The Formulas**

★ **Important Exam Point:** The rule is divided into two distinct parts based on whether the fetus is in the first or second half of pregnancy [3-p30, 4-p110].

|**Period**|**Formula**|**Calculation Method**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**First 5 Months** (< 5 months)|$\text{Month} = \sqrt{\text{Length (cm)}}$|The age in lunar months is the square root of the length [3-p30, 4-p110].|
|**Last 5 Months** (> 5 months)|$\text{Month} = \frac{\text{Length (cm)}}{5}$|The age in lunar months is the length divided by five [3-p30, 4-p110].|

_Note: The second part is also referred to as the **Modified Morrison’s Rule** in some contexts [3-p30]._

---

#### **Standard Length Table**

Based on this rule, the expected length of a fetus at each month is as follows [3-p30]:

|**Age (Lunar Months)**|**Calculation**|**Length (cm)**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1st Month**|$1 \times 1$|1 cm|
|**2nd Month**|$2 \times 2$|4 cm|
|**3rd Month**|$3 \times 3$|9 cm|
|**4th Month**|$4 \times 4$|16 cm|
|**5th Month**|$5 \times 5$|**25 cm**|
|**6th Month**|$6 \times 5$|30 cm|
|**7th Month**|$7 \times 5$|35 cm|
|**8th Month**|$8 \times 5$|40 cm|
|**9th Month**|$9 \times 5$|45 cm|
|**10th Month**|$10 \times 5$|50 cm|

---

#### **Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Viability Determination:** Helping determine if the fetus was viable (capable of surviving outside the womb), which is generally considered to be at **28 weeks (7 months)** [4-p117, 4-p145].
- **Abortion Cases:** Establishing the age of the fetus is necessary to determine if an induced abortion was legal under the MTP Act or criminal under the BNS [4-p137, 4-p142].
- **Infanticide Investigations:** Proving the age of a deceased newborn to match with suspect medical records [4-p145].
- **Corpus Delicti:** Contributing to the identification of the "body of the crime" when fetal remains are discovered [4-p42].

---

#### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Length Measure:** Always uses **Crown-Heel Length (CHL)**, not Crown-Rump Length (CRL) [3-p30, 4-p102].
- ★ **Cut-off Point:** The mathematical logic changes exactly at the **5th month (25 cm)** [3-p30, 4-p110].
- ★ **Calculation Example:** If a dead fetus is found with a CHL of 25 cm, its approximate age is 5 months [3-p37].

---

#### **Viva Questions**

1. **What is the Rule of Hasse?**
    - _Ans:_ It is a rule to estimate fetal age in lunar months using the crown-heel length [1-p102].
2. **How do you calculate age if the fetus is 16 cm long?**
    - _Ans:_ Since it is <25 cm, we use the square root: $\sqrt{16} = 4$ months [3-p30].
3. **How do you calculate age if the fetus is 40 cm long?**
    - _Ans:_ Since it is >25 cm, we divide by 5: $40 / 5 = 8$ months [3-p30].
4. **What length signifies a full-term fetus (10 months)?**
    - _Ans:_ 50 cm [3-p30].

---

#### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **Formula (<5 months):** Month² = Length (cm).
- **Formula (>5 months):** Month × 5 = Length (cm).
- **Measurement:** Crown to Heel.
- **Key Age:** 5 months = 25 cm.
- **Full Term:** 10 months = 50 cm.
