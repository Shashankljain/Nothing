## **Basic Concepts & Definitions** 📚

### **Definitions**

- ★ **DNA Fingerprinting (DNA Profiling/Typing):** A state-of-the-art laboratory technique used to determine the probable identity of an individual by analyzing specific nucleotide sequences within highly variable regions of their DNA that are unique to them.
- ★ **Uniqueness Rule:** This technique is capable of distinguishing every single human being on Earth with absolute certainty, with the sole exceptions of **identical (monozygotic) twins** and **clones**.
- ★ **Junk DNA (Introns):** Highly variable, non-coding sequences making up about **95% of the human genome**. These regions contain repeating base-pair sequences that act as genetic "stutters," known as **Variable Number of Tandem Repeats (VNTRs)** or **minisatellites**, which form the basis of individualization.

## **Topic-Specific Explanation: The Two Primary Techniques** 🔬

DNA profiling historically and practically relies on two analytical pathways:

### **1. Restriction Fragment Length Polymorphism (RFLP)**

- **Mechanism:** Intact DNA is extracted and sliced at highly specific locations by biological scissors called **Restriction Endonucleases** (e.g., _EcoRI_ from bacteria). The resulting ==fragments== of variable lengths (RFLPs) are ==separated by size== on an *==agarose gel==* via electrophoresis and transferred onto a nylon membrane (**Southern Blotting**). Radioactive DNA probes are applied to hybridize with the VNTRs, producing a unique band pattern on X-ray film (**Autoradiography**).

---

### **2. Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)**

- **Mechanism:** An _in vitro_ enzymatic photocopying process discovered by **Kary B. Mullis** (Nobel Prize, 1993). It rapidly replicates a specific DNA sequence millions of times over.
- ★ **Essential PCR Requirements:** Taq Polymerase (heat-resistant enzyme), specific Primers, Deoxynucleoside Triphosphates (dNTPs), and the DNA template.

### **RFLP vs. PCR: High-Yield Comparison Table** 📊

★ **Important Exam Point:** Replicating this comparison table in a 5-mark question is highly valued by examiners.

|**Forensic Parameter**|**RFLP (Minisatellites)** 🧬|**PCR (Microsatellites / STRs)** 🧬|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1. Sample Size Required**|**Large** (300 to 500 ng of DNA).|**Minuscule** (as little as 25 ng).|
|**2. DNA State Efficacy**|Requires **intact, fresh, undegraded DNA**.|Works exceptionally well on **highly degraded/old DNA**.|
|**3. Use in Decomposed Bodies**|**Not useful**.|**Highly useful**.|
|**4. Time Taken**|Tedious; takes several weeks.|Rapid; completed in hours/days.|
|**5. Blotting Technique**|Requires **Southern Blotting**.|No blotting required (direct capillary electrophoresis).|
|**6. Sensitivity to Contamination**|Relatively **less sensitive**.|**Extremely sensitive**; trace contaminant DNA gets amplified.|

## **Modern Technique of choice: STR Analysis** 🚀

- Modern forensic laboratories have transitioned to **Short Tandem Repeats (STR)** or **microsatellites** (core repeats of 2–6 base pairs).
- **Methodology:** Uses PCR with fluorescently tagged primers to amplify **13 core loci**. The fragments are processed via a laser scanner, displaying results as digital colored peaks (**Electropherogram**).
- ★ **Y-STR Analysis:** Derived solely from the male **Y-chromosome**; highly valuable in sexual assault cases to isolate male perpetrator profiles from mixed male-female vaginal swabs.
- ★ **Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) Profiling:** Useful for degraded bones, skeletal remains, or **hair shafts lacking root bulbs**. Cells contain hundreds of copies of maternal mtDNA compared to only two nuclear copies, drastically increasing recovery success.

## **Specimen Selection, Collection & Preservation** 🧪

Strict protocols must be followed to avoid contamination:

- ★ **Blood (Gold Standard):** Collect **5 mL venous blood** in a purple-topped **EDTA tube**.
    - _Trap:_ **Never use Heparin** as an anticoagulant because heparin actively inhibits Taq polymerase during PCR.
- **Buccal Swabs:** A convenient, non-invasive alternative.
- **Skeletal / Decomposed Remains:**
    - _Early Decomposition:_ Collect **spleen or muscle**.
    - _Advanced Decomposition/Skeletonization:_ **Femur bone marrow** or **healthy molars/canines** (the dense dentin shell preserves pulp DNA).
- ★ **Whatman FTA® Cards:** Cellulose-based dry storage matrix treated with weak bases and chelating agents.
    - _Mechanism:_ Lyses cells and denatures proteins. DNA remains completely protected from nucleases and microbial decay, allowing **room temperature storage for over 50 years**.

## **Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Important Exam Point: Civil Cases (Disputed Parentage):**
    - DNA profiling is the **only method that provides 100% absolute certainty** in resolving disputed paternity, maternity, baby swaps, or inheritance lawsuits. Every band present in a child's DNA profile must have a matching match in either the biological mother or biological father.
- ★ **Criminal Cases:**
    - Individually links suspects to biological stains (blood, semen, saliva, hair) at crime scenes.
    - **Touch DNA:** Highly sensitive PCR can now generate a full profile from **as few as 20 skin cells** left behind on a handled object (e.g., gun trigger, knife handle).
- **Mass Disaster Identification:** Establishing the identity of highly mutilated, fragmented, or charred bodies by comparing their DNA to reference samples from living family members.
- **Exoneration of the Innocent:** It has revolutionized the justice system by clearing wrongly accused suspects.

## **Relevant Law (Latest 2023 Update)** ⚖️

- ★ **Section 39 BSA, 2023 (formerly Sec 45 IEA) – Expert Opinion:** Legally admits expert molecular biology and DNA profiling reports as highly admissible evidence in court [Image 15].
- ★ **Section 116 BSA, 2023 (formerly Sec 112 IEA) – Presumption of Legitimacy:** Birth during a valid marriage is considered conclusive proof of legitimacy [Image 15]. DNA testing is ordered by courts as a scientific rebuttal to prove "non-access" or biological exclusion [Image 15].
- ★ **Section 144 BNSS, 2023 (formerly Sec 125 CrPC) – Maintenance Claims:** DNA profiling is legally mandated to resolve parentage disputes before the court awards child support/maintenance [Image 15].
- ★ **Section 53(1) BNSS, 2023 (formerly Sec 53(1) CrPC):** Empowers the investigating police officer to request a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) to perform a **compulsory medical examination of the accused**, including blood/fluid collection for DNA typing [Image 25].
