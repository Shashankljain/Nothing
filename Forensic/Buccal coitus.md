# 👄🍆 **BUCCAL COITUS (COITUS PER OS) — 3-MARK EXAM GUIDE** 🍆👄

### **1. Definition & Synonyms** 📚

- **Buccal Coitus:** Also known as **Coitus per os**, **oral sex**, **mouth coitus**, or historically as the **Sin of Gomorrah**[image/slide].
- It is a form of unnatural sexual contact involving the penetration of the active partner's penis into the oral cavity (mouth) of the passive partner [image/slide].
- **Two Types:**
    1. ★ **Fellatio:** Oral stimulation/penetration of the male genitalia [image/slide].
    2. ★ **Cunnilingus:** Oral stimulation of the female genitalia [image/slide].

### **2. Legal Status & Latest 2023 Codes** ⚖️

★ **Important Exam Point:** You must highlight the drastic changes under the new laws to secure maximum marks:

- ★ **Consensual Adults (>18 years):** Under the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**, the historic crime of "unnatural offenses" (formerly Section 377 IPC) has been **deleted** [image/slide]. Consensual buccal coitus between adults is **no longer punishable** under Indian law [image/slide].
- ★ **Non-Consensual (On a Woman):** If a man penetrates his penis into the oral cavity/mouth of a woman without her consent, it is legally defined as **Rape under Section 63 BNS, 2023** (punishable by 10 years to life imprisonment under Section 64 BNS) [image/slide].
- ★ **On a Child (<18 years):** Any buccal coitus involving a minor is prosecuted under the **POCSO Act, 2012** as **Penetrative Sexual Assault** [image/slide].

### **3. Medico-Legal Importance & Forensic Examination** 🧪🔍

- ★ **Spermatozoa Recovery (Time Limit):** Non-motile spermatozoa can be recovered from the oral cavity for **2 to 31 hours** after the assault.
- **Physical Injury Signs:** Look for superficial lacerations or bruising of the lips, gums, soft palate, and particularly **tears of the frenulum of the tongue**.
- **Saliva Swabbing:** Swabs from the mouth/lips should be taken to identify **\(\alpha\)-amylase** (confirming saliva presence) and to determine the **secretor status** (ABO antigens) of the assailant.
