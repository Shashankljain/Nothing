[[Sexual offence]]
**Sodomy** is a significant topic in Forensic Medicine, traditionally classified under "unnatural sexual offences." For your university examinations, you must understand its clinical findings and how the legal landscape has changed with the implementation of the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**.

---

### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition** **Sodomy** (also known as anal sex, buggery, or Greek love) refers to sexual intercourse _per anum_ (through the anus). It can occur between two males (homosexual sodomy) or between a male and a female (heterosexual sodomy).

- **Active Agent:** The person who penetrates (Pederast).
- **Passive Agent:** The person who is penetrated (Catamite, especially if a child).

**Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Criminal Cases:** To prove non-consensual acts (Rape under BNS) or sexual abuse of children (POCSO Act).
- **Civil Cases:** Historically used as grounds for divorce or to determine the "habitual" nature of an individual's sexual practices.

---

### **Relevant Law (2023)**

★ **Important Exam Point:** Standalone "unnatural offences" (formerly Section 377 IPC) have been **deleted** under the BNS.

1. **Consensual Sodomy:** Sexual acts between consenting adults (aged >18 years) are **not punishable** regardless of whether they are natural or unnatural.
2. **Non-consensual Sodomy (Section 63 BNS):** The new definition of **Rape** now explicitly includes the penetration of the **anal canal** of a woman by a man without her consent.
3. **Offences against Children:** Any penetrative sexual act on a child (<18 years), including anal penetration, is strictly governed by the **POCSO Act, 2012** and **BNS 65**.
4. **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023:** Procedural aspects for medical examination remain similar to rape cases (e.g., Section 184 BNSS for victims and Section 52 BNSS for the accused).

---

### **Medico-Legal Examination**

The subject is usually examined in the **Knee-Chest position** to allow for clear visualization of the perianal area.

#### **Findings in a Habitual Passive Agent**

A "habitual" passive agent is someone who frequently engages in anal intercourse. Over time, the anatomy of the anus undergoes chronic changes due to repeated trauma and dilation.

|**Feature**|**Habitual Passive Agent Findings**|
|:--|:--|
|**General Appearance**|The anus appears **funnel-shaped** due to the absorption of perianal fat.|
|**Perianal Skin**|Becomes **thickened, smooth, and keratinized**.|
|**Rugosity**|There is a **loss of mucosal rugosity** (the normal folds around the anus disappear).|
|**Anal Sphincter**|The external sphincter becomes **relaxed or dilated** due to chronic damage.|
|**Reflexes**|**Paradoxical Dilatation:** This is the most characteristic sign. On bimanual traction of the buttocks, the anal sphincter dilates instead of contracting.|
|**Hair**|Perianal hair may be **shaved** for ease of the act.|
|**Pathology**|There may be evidence of chronic fissures, piles, or **prolapse of the rectal mucosa**.|
|**Pain**|Acute pain and tenderness are usually **absent** (unlike a first-time or non-habitual victim).|

### **Specimen Collection**

In cases of alleged non-consensual sodomy, the medical officer must collect:

- **Anal Swabs:** To detect **spermatozoa** (motile or non-motile).
- **Chemical Markers:** To test for **Acid Phosphatase** or **p30 (Prostate Specific Antigen)**.
- **Clothing:** To look for stains of semen, blood, or faecal matter.

### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Lateral Buttock Traction Test:** The primary clinical test to check for **Paradoxical Dilatation** in habitual agents.
- ★ **Tyre Sign:** A bruise/hematoma around the anus seen in forceful, **non-habitual** sodomy; it is usually absent in habitual agents.
- ★ **BNS 63:** Non-consensual anal penetration is now legally prosecuted as **Rape**.

---

### **Viva Questions**

**Q: What is "Paradoxical Dilatation"?** **A:** It is a finding in habitual passive agents where the anal sphincter opens (dilates) when the buttocks are pulled apart, rather than closing (contracting) as it would in a normal individual.

**Q: Is consensual sodomy between two adult men a crime today?** **A:** No, under the BNS 2023, consensual sexual acts between adults are not punishable as the old "unnatural offence" section (IPC 377) has been removed.

**Q: How do you identify a habitual passive agent during an autopsy?** **A:** Look for a funnel-shaped anus, thickened perianal skin, and the absence of mucosal rugae (folds).

---

### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **Definition:** Anal intercourse (_per anum_).
- **Law:** Consensual = No crime; Non-consensual = Rape (Section 63 BNS).
- **Passive Agent Position:** Knee-Elbow.
- **Key Habitual Signs:** Funnel-shaped anus, relaxed sphincter, loss of rugae, and **Paradoxical Dilatation**.
- **Evidence:** Anal swabs for DNA and semen markers (p30/Acid Phosphatase).
