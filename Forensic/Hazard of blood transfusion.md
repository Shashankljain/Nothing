**Classification of Hazards** 📚

Forensic and clinical complications of blood transfusion are broadly divided into immunological reactions and infectious transmissions:

- ★ **Incompatible (Mismatched) Transfusion:** Administering mismatched blood types (e.g., ABO incompatibility) causes acute intravascular hemolysis, severe anaphylactic shock, and sudden iatrogenic death.
- ★ **Neglect of Expiry Dates:** Using blood bags past their expiration dates leads to severe clinical transfusion reactions.
- ★ **Transmission of Pathogenic Organisms:** Transfusing unscreened blood can transmit lethal infections:
    - _Viral:_ **HIV-1 & HIV-2**, **Hepatitis B (HBV)**, and **Hepatitis C (HCV)**. _(Note: Hemophiliacs receiving repeated transfusions are at the highest risk__)._
    - _Bacterial & Protozoal:_ **Syphilis** and **Malaria**.

---

**2. Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️🩺

- ★ **Important Exam Point: Medical Malpractice & Negligence:** Failing to cross-match blood, neglecting expiry dates, or failing to screen donor units for mandatory pathogens (like HIV and HBV) before transfusion is legally classified as **Civil / Professional Negligence**.
