[[Infanticide]]
### **A. Definitions**

- ★ **Infanticide:** It is the unlawful killing of an infant (a child under **one year of age**) [image/slide].
- **Neonaticide:** The killing of a newborn infant within **24 hours of birth** [image/slide].
- **Feticide:** The destruction or killing of a fetus in the womb (intrauterine death) [image/slide].
- **Filicide:** The killing of a child (of any age) by their own parent(s) [image/slide].

### **B. Legal Status in India** ★ **Important Exam Point**

- Unlike English Law (which has a separate "Infanticide Act" with reduced culpability), **Indian law does not recognize infanticide as a separate, distinct offense**.
- The killing of a fully born, living child is legally treated as **Murder under Section 103 BNS, 2023** (formerly Section 302 IPC) and carries the same punishment (death penalty or life imprisonment) [image/slide].

## **2. Methods of Causing Death of Infants** 🩸☠️

Homicidal infant deaths are clinically and forensically classified into two main categories: **Acts of Commission**(active violence) and **Acts of Omission** (willful neglect).

```
                             [METHODS OF INFANTICIDE]
                                        │
         ┌──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                             ▼
   [ACTS OF COMMISSION]                                     [ACTS OF OMISSION]
 (Active Physical Violence)                           (Willful, Fatal Neglect)
 • Smothering / Suffocation                    • Failure to tie umbilical cord
 • Strangulation / Throttling                       • Failure to clear airways
 • Drowning                               • Exposure to cold/heat (Hypothermia)
 • Blunt Force Head Trauma                     • Withholding feeds (Starvation)
 • Poisoning (Opium on nipples)                       • Abandonment / Exposure
 • Puncture of fontanelles with needles        • Failure to rescue from danger
```

---

### **A. Acts of Commission (Active Violence)** 🔨🔪

These represent positive physical actions taken by the perpetrator to actively end the infant's life:

1. ★ **Smothering and Suffocation (Most Common):**
    - _Mechanism:_ Blocking the external respiratory passages (mouth and nose) using a hand, pillow, soft cloth, or face-down placement on bedding [image/slide].
    - _Autopsy findings:_ Perioral abrasions, contusions, and tears of the **upper lip frenulum** (highly characteristic of manual violence) [image/slide].
2. ★ **Strangulation and Throttling:**
    - _Mechanism:_ Constricting the neck using a cord, string, piece of clothing (ligature), or manual pressure of hands [image/slide].
    - _Autopsy findings:_ Ligature furrows, crescent-shaped fingernail abrasions, and **six-penny (discoid) bruises** on the neck [image/slide].
3. ★ **Drowning:**
    - _Mechanism:_ Submerging the infant in a bucket of water, bath, or toilet pan (often occurs immediately after precipitate labor).
    - _Autopsy findings:_ Presence of fine, tenacious white **shaving-lather froth** at the mouth/nose and a positive **diatom/hydrostatic test** [image/slide].
4. ★ **Blunt Force Head Injury (Infant Battering):**
    - _Mechanism:_ Dashed or thrown against a hard surface (floor/wall).
    - _Autopsy findings:_ Multiple skull fractures (linear/eggshell), subdural hematoma, and retinal detachment (**Shaken Baby Syndrome** / Infantile whiplash) [image/slide].
5. ★ **Poisoning:**
    - _Mechanism:_ Direct administration of toxins, or the classic method of **smearing the mother's/wet nurse's nipples with Opium (tincture of opium)**, arsenic, or mercury before breastfeeding [image/slide].
6. ★ **Puncture Wounds (Concealed Violence):**
    - _Mechanism:_ Inserting fine needles, hatpins, or nails through the **anterior fontanelle**, inner canthus of the eye, nostrils, or vagina into vital centers of the brain.
    - _Autopsy findings:_ Tiny, pinprick entry holes that require meticulous reflection of the scalp to detect.

---

### **B. Acts of Omission (Willful Neglect)** ❄️🚫.    (CODES)

These represent a deliberate failure to provide the basic, essential care required to sustain a newborn's life:

1. ★ **Failure to tie and cut the umbilical cord:**
    - _Mechanism:_ Leads to exsanguination (fatal hemorrhage) through the untied, open umbilical vessels.
2. ★ **Failure to clear the air passages at birth:**
    - _Mechanism:_ Letting the child choke on mucus, amniotic fluid, or maternal blood, or failing to remove the embryonic membranes covering the face (**cul-de-sac**), resulting in asphyxiation [image/slide].
3. ★ **Exposure to cold or heat (Exposure):**
    - _Mechanism:_ Leaving the wet, newborn baby unclad and exposed to extreme weather (especially cold winter nights), causing rapid, fatal hypothermia.
4. ★ **Withholding of feeds (Starvation):**
    - _Mechanism:_ Deliberate, total deprivation of milk/water [image/slide].
    - _Autopsy findings:_ Loss of more than **20% of body protein**, complete absence of subcutaneous fat, shrunken shriveled skin, and a **distended gallbladder filled with thick bile** [image/slide].
5. ★ **Failure to rescue from predictable danger:**
    - _Mechanism:_ Failing to lift a baby who has fallen into a bucket, drain, or toilet bowl during delivery, allowing the child to drown.
6. ★ **Failure to provide proper clothing/shelter.**

## **3. Relevant Penal Provisions (LATEST BNS, 2023)**⚖️

- ★ **Section 103 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 302 IPC) — Murder:** Applied when a live-born child is actively killed [103 BNS]. Punished by death or life imprisonment [103 BNS].
- ★ **Section 91 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 315 IPC):** Act done with intent to prevent a child from being born alive, or causing it to die after birth [91 BNS]. Punishable by up to **10 years of imprisonment**, a fine, or both [91 BNS].
- ★ **Section 93 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 317 IPC):**Exposure and abandonment of a child under 12 years of age by a parent or guardian. Punished by up to **7 years of imprisonment**, a fine, or both.
- ★ **Section 94 BNS, 2023 (formerly Sec 318 IPC):**Secret disposal of the dead body of a child (whether born dead or alive) to conceal its birth [94 BNS]. Punished by up to **2 years of imprisonment**, a fine, or both [94 BNS].

## **4. High-Yield Exam Corner & Viva Questions** ★

### **Viva Questions & Model Answers** 💬

**Q1: How do you differentiate a live-born infant from a dead-born (stillborn) infant on autopsy?**  
**Answer:** The gold standard is the **Hydrostatic (Raygat's/Breslau's 1st life) Test**. Respired lungs have a specific gravity of **0.94** and will **float in water**, whereas non-respired lungs have a specific gravity of **1.04** and will **sink** [image/slide]. We also look for air in the stomach/intestines (**Breslau's 2nd life test**) [image/slide].

**Q2: What is "cul-de-sac" in infant autopsy?**  
**Answer:** It refers to an infant born with intact amniotic membranes covering its nose and mouth [image/slide]. If the mother fails to rupture it, the baby suffocates. This can be an act of omission (neglect) or accidental.

**Q3: What are the autopsy features of a starved infant?**  
**Answer:** Marked emaciation, loss of skin elasticity, complete absence of subcutaneous fat, shrunken internal organs (except the brain which is spared), and a **significantly distended gallbladder filled with dark, thick bile**[image/slide].

### **Common Mistakes Students Make** ❌

- _Thinking "Infanticide" has a separate section in the BNS:_Remember to clarify to the examiner that **infanticide is charged as Murder under Section 103 BNS** [103 BNS].
- _Forgetting the Starvation indicator:_ Always mention the **distended gallbladder**—this is a classic, high-scoring autopsy finding highly favored by examiners [image/slide].

### **Mnemonics** 🧠

#### **1. Viability Signs of a Fetus/Newborn: "S-E-V-E-N-T-H"**

- **S** – **S**ternum (ossification centers in 2nd and 3rd segments) [image/slide].
- **E** – **E**yelids (fully open by 28 weeks) [image/slide].
- **V** – **V**iability reached at 28 weeks (capable of independent survival) [image/slide].
- **E** – **E**xternal inguinal ring contains the descended testes [image/slide].
- **N** – **N**ails are thick and reach fingertips [image/slide].
- **T** – **T**alus bone ossification center present [image/slide].
- **H** – **H**air (lanugo) starts disappearing; head hair appears.

#### **2. Acts of Omission: "C-O-D-E-S"**

- **C** – **C**ord untied (fatal bleeding).
- **O** – **O**bstruction of airways left uncleared [image/slide].
- **D** – **D**anger ignored (not rescuing from toilet bowl/bucket).
- **E** – **E**xposure to cold or heat (hypothermia/hyperthermia).
- **S** – **S**tarvation (withholding feeds) [image/slide].
