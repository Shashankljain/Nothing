### **1. Definition** 📚

- **Witness:** A person who gives sworn testimony (evidence) in a court of law regarding facts and/or inferences that can be drawn from them.
- ★ **Important Exam Point:** Competency to give evidence depends solely on the individual's **ability to understand questions** and give rational answers, not on their age.

---

### **2. Classification of Witnesses** 📊

In a court of law, witnesses are primarily classified into two main types, along with special legal subcategories:

```
                              [TYPES OF WITNESS]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                            ▼                            ▼
  [Common Witness]            [Expert Witness]             [Hostile Witness]
 • Lay / Ordinary /       • Skilled professional       • Willfully conceals the
   percipient witness.      expressing opinions.         truth / turns back on
 • "First-hand knowledge" • **Section 39 BSA, 2023**.    prior deposition.
```

#### **A. Common Witness (Lay / Ordinary / Percipient Witness)**

- **Definition:** An ordinary person who deposes in court about facts they have **personally seen, heard, or perceived**through their own senses.
- ★ **The First-Hand Knowledge Rule:** They can only testify to direct facts of which they have first-hand experience (e.g., witnessing a physical assault).
- _Limitation:_ A common witness is **not allowed to express opinions** or volunteer statements.

#### **B. Expert Witness (Skilled Witness) – Section 39 BSA, 2023**

- **Definition:** A person who is specially trained or skilled in a specific branch of science, art, trade, or foreign law, called to assist the court in drawing conclusions from technical facts.
- _Examples:_ Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP), Ballistics Expert, Fingerprint Examiner, Chemical Examiner.
- _Privilege:_ They **can express professional opinions** and volunteer relevant scientific statements.
- ★ **Legal Update:** Governed under **Section 39 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** (formerly Section 45 of the Indian Evidence Act).

#### **C. Hostile Witness**

- **Definition:** A witness who willfully, or due to ulterior motives (bribery, intimidation, or bias), **conceals the truth or gives testimony contrary to their previous statement** made during the police investigation.
- _Legal Outcome:_ The party that called the witness is permitted by the judge to cross-examine their own witness and ask **leading questions**.

### **3. Quick Comparison for University Exams** ⏱️

|**Feature**|**Common Witness** 👤|**Expert Witness** 🥼|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Testimony Basis**|Personal observation (facts).|Specialized scientific training (opinion).|
|**Volunteering Statements**|**Strictly prohibited**.|**Permitted** to explain findings.|
|**Conduct Money**|Cannot claim.|Can claim professional fees.|
|**Overlap Rule**|Can never act as an expert.|Can act as both (e.g., a doctor who witnesses an assault and treats the victim).|
