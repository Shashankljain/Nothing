[[Sexual offence]]
The **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012**, is a  law enacted to provide a ==legal framework== for the ==protection== of children from offences of -
sexual assault, sexual harassment, and pornography. 

---

### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition of a Child**

- Under the POCSO Act, a **child** is defined as any person below the **age of 18 years**.
- The Act is **gender-neutral**, meaning it protects both boys and girls from sexual abuse.

**Objectives**

- To ==protect== the best interests of the child at every stage of the legal process.
- To ensure ==child-friendly== reporting, recording of evidence, investigation, and speedy trial of offences.

**Classification of Offences under POCSO** The Act categorizes sexual abuse into specific types:

1. **Sexual Harassment:** Includes bullying, coercion of a sexual nature, or showing pornography to a child.
2. **Sexual Assault:** An intentional act where a person touches a child sexually without consent or forces them to engage in a sexual act.
3. **Penetrative Sexual Assault:** Insertion of the penis, object, or any body part into the vagina, urethra, anus, or mouth of a child.
4. **Aggravated Penetrative Sexual Assault:** Specific circumstances that increase the gravity of the crime (e.g., committed by a relative, police officer, or causing HIV/grievous hurt).

---

### **Recent Amendments and Punishments**

The **POCSO (Amendment) Bill, 2019**, and the subsequent **BNS, 2023**, have introduced significantly more stringent punishments to deter child abuse.

|**Offence**|**BNS Section / POCSO Provision**|**Punishment (Recent Amendments)**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Rape of a girl <16 years**|**BNS 65(1)**|Min. **20 years** to Life (for remainder of life) + Fine.|
|**Rape of a girl <12 years**|**BNS 65(2)**|Min. **20 years** to Life or **Death Penalty**.|
|**Aggravated Penetrative Sexual Assault**|**POCSO 2019**|**20 years** to Life or **Death Penalty** (if resulting in death or during natural calamity).|
|**Sexual Harassment**|**POCSO 2019**|0–3 years imprisonment + Fine.|
|**Use of child for Pornography**|**POCSO 2019**|Minimum **5 years** imprisonment.|

★ **Important Exam Point:** Under **BNS Section 70(2)**, gang rape of a girl under 18 years is punishable by **Life Imprisonment** or the **Death Penalty**.

### **Medico-Legal Significance and Duties**

**1. Mandatory Reporting**

- Under the POCSO Act, any person (including a doctor) who has apprehension that a sexual offence has been committed against a child **must inform the police**.
- Failure to report is a punishable offence.

**2. Medical Examination (BNSS 184)**

- The examination must be conducted within **24 hours** of receiving information.
- For a girl child, the examination **must be conducted by a female Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP)**.
- **Informed Consent:** Must be taken from the parent or guardian; however, treatment cannot be denied if consent is delayed in emergencies.

**3. Evidentiary Procedures**

- **Evidence Recording:** The child's statement/evidence must be recorded within **30 days** of the offence being reported.
- **Trial Timeline:** The trial should ideally be completed within **one year**.
- **Two-Finger Test:** Strictly banned and considered professional misconduct [Standard Forensic Practice].

---

### **Relevant Law (2023 Updates)**

- **BNS 63:** Defines the act of Rape, which includes penetration of orifices of a child.
- **BNS 65:** Specifically provides aggravated punishments for offences against children under 16 and 12 years.
- **BNSS 184:** Outlines the mandatory procedure for the medical examination of sexual assault victims.
- **BSA 120:** Presumes **absence of consent**; in cases of child victims (<18 years), consent is legally irrelevant (Statutory Rape).

---

### **Clinical Features in Child Victims**

When examining a child under POCSO, doctors look for:

- **Micro-trauma:** Detected using **Toluidine-blue dye**.
- **Hymenal Condition:** Typically **unruptured** in young children due to its deep and elastic nature.
- **Specific Injuries:** The "Tear inside the upper lip near the frenulum" is a characteristic sign of manual violence/abuse.
- **Psychological Signs:** Withdrawal, anxiety, or regression (Rape Trauma Syndrome).

---

### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Age of Consent:** 18 years (consent below this is irrelevant).
- ★ **Death Penalty:** Possible for rape of children <12 years under BNS 65(2) and POCSO 2019.
- ★ **Mandatory Gender:** A female child **must** be examined by a **female doctor**.
- ★ **Safe Kit:** Used to collect 4 vaginal swabs, clothing, and nail scrapings.

---

### **Viva Questions**

**Q1: What is "Statutory Rape"?** **Answer:** It is sexual intercourse with a girl under **18 years of age**. Her consent is legally invalid, making the act rape regardless of her willingness.

**Q2: Who is responsible for reporting a POCSO case found in a hospital?** **Answer:** Any medical professional who suspects child abuse is legally bound to report it to the police or the Special Juvenile Police Unit.

**Q3: Can a doctor be punished for not reporting a POCSO offence?** **Answer:** Yes, failure to report a known offence under POCSO is a criminal act for which the doctor can be prosecuted.

### **Quick Revision Summary**

1. **Child:** Any person <18 years (Gender-neutral).
2. **Reporting:** Mandatory for all citizens/doctors.
3. **Punishment:** BNS 65/70/71 (up to Death Penalty for <12 yrs or Gang Rape).
4. **Exam:** Female RMP mandatory for girls; report in <7 days.
5. **Presumption:** No consent if <18 (BSA 120).
