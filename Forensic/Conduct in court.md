[[legal procedure]]
Conduct of the Doctor as a Witness
- Preparation: Before testifying, the doctor should verify the identification marks and medical records they noted during the original examination
.
- Punctuality: Must attend court on the scheduled date to avoid penalties under Section 208 BNS
.
- Demeanor: Remain calm, professional, and unbiased. Speak clearly and use simple language for the court's understanding
.
- Veracity: Always tell the truth. Giving false information is Perjury (Section 229 BNS) and constitutes Professional Misconduct (Penal Erasure)
.
- Handling Leading Questions: Be alert during cross-examination where leading questions (questions suggesting a "Yes" or "No" answer) are allowed to discredit you
.
- Hostile Witness: If a doctor is "not desirous of telling the truth" or favors the opposite side, the court may declare them a Hostile Witness, allowing their own lawyer to cross-examine them
.
