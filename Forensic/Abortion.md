### **2. Immediate Complications (Causes of Rapid Death)** ⚡

- ★ **Hemorrhage (Most Common Immediate Cause):** Direct laceration of the uterine wall, uterine artery, cervix, or vaginal fornices during unscientific instrumentation. Incomplete abortion leaves retained placental tissue, preventing uterine contraction, which leads to profuse bleeding and hypovolemic shock.
- ★ **Vagal Inhibition (Vasovagal Shock):** Intense, sudden stimulation of sensory nerve endings in the cervix or endometrium (due to cervical dilatation or introduction of cold/acidic fluids) causing instant reflex cardiac arrest on the table.
- ★ **Air Embolism (Most Common Embolism):** Classic complication when a **Higginson's syringe** is used by a quack to pump air or soapy water into the uterus to detach membranes. This forces air directly into the torn, open **uterine venous sinuses**, leading to a fatal "air-lock" in the right side of the heart.
- _Fat & Amniotic Fluid Embolism:_ Soap injection leads to fat emboli; intense uterine pressure forces amniotic fluid into the venous sinuses, triggering DIC.
- _Uterine Perforation:_ Piercing of the soft, gravid uterus by abortion sticks, wire hangers, or curettes, which can also perforate abdominal organs (bladder, rectum).

---

### **3. Delayed Complications (Within 2–3 Days)** ⏱️🦠

- ★ **Septicemia & Pelvic Peritonitis:** The most common delayed cause of death, resulting from unsterile instruments or decomposing retained tissues, leading to generalized sepsis within 48 to 72 hours.
- _Tetanus & Gas Gangrene:_ Spore contamination introduced via dirty botanical **abortion sticks** or unsterile metal instruments.
- _Acute Renal Failure (Acute Tubular Necrosis):_ Triggered by prolonged shock, sepsis, or hemolysis from chemical abortifacients.
- _Systemic Poisoning (Abortifacient Toxicity):_ Toxic absorption of ingested or locally applied agents, such as **Dhatura seeds**, **Oleander**, **Marking Nut juice**, **Lead oleate**, or corrosive acids.

---

### **4. Late / Remote Complications** ⏳

- ★ **Secondary Sterility (Infertility):** Chronic pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) causing tubal blockages or uterine cavity scarring (**Asherman's Syndrome**).
- _Recurrent Miscarriages / Ectopic Pregnancy:_ Due to permanent cervical damage or fallopian tube scarring.

### **5. Relevant Criminal Law (LATEST BNS, 2023 CODES)** ⚖️

Ensure you replace the old IPC sections (312–316) with the newly enacted **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** codes to secure maximum marks:

- ★ **Section 88 BNS (formerly Sec 312 IPC):** Criminal abortion with the mother's consent. Punishable by up to **3 years of imprisonment** (enhanced to **7 years** if she is "quick with child") and a fine.
- ★ **Section 89 BNS (formerly Sec 313 IPC):** Criminal abortion **without** the mother's consent. Punishable by **life imprisonment** or up to **10 years** and a fine.
- ★ **Section 90 BNS (formerly Sec 314 IPC):** Death of the mother caused directly by the abortive act. Punishable by up to **10 years of imprisonment** and a fine.
- ★ **Section 92 BNS (formerly Sec 316 IPC):** Causing the death of an unborn quick child by an act amounting to culpable homicide (e.g., kicking a pregnant woman in the abdomen). Punishable by up to **10 years of imprisonment** and a fine.
