[[Non metallic]]
**PART 1: PHOSPHORUS POISONING** 🌋💡

**Basic Concepts & Classification** 📚

**Definition**

**Phosphorus poisoning** is the acute or chronic toxicity resulting from the systemic absorption or local tissue exposure to elemental phosphorus, a highly active non-metallic protoplasmic toxicant.

**Introduction**

Phosphorus exists in several allotropic forms, primarily **White (Yellow) Phosphorus** and **Red (Amorphous) Phosphorus**. While white phosphorus is highly toxic and clinically significant, red phosphorus is relatively non-toxic.

**The "6-W" Analytical Framework** ❓

1. **What?** Phosphorus poisoning is a metabolic crisis caused by white phosphorus, which acts as a cellular protoplasmic poison.
2. **Why?** Exposure is typically accidental in children (chewing fireworks/eating rat paste) or suicidal/homicidal in adults.
3. **How?** It directly inhibits cellular oxidation, causing rapid fatty degeneration, cellular necrosis, and multiorgan failure.
4. **When?** Acute symptoms occur within minutes of ingestion or dermal contact; chronic features develop over years of occupational inhalation.
5. **Where?** Local damage is seen in the GIT or skin; systemic effects primarily target the Liver (periportal injury), Kidneys, and Myocardium.
6. **Who?** Primarily affects factory workers (historical "Phossy Jaw"), children, or individuals utilizing rat pastes for self-harm.

- **Protoplasmic Toxicity:** Phosphorus interferes directly with cellular oxidation pathways, arresting metabolic processes and leading to cell necrosis (_necrobiosis_), primarily targeting the **periportal region of the liver**.
- **Local Burns:** White phosphorus is highly lipid-soluble, allowing rapid, deep penetration into skin layers, causing severe 2nd and 3rd-degree chemical burns that heal exceptionally slowly.

**Clinical Features** 🩺

**I. Acute Dermal Contact Injury**

- Penetrating, agonizingly painful 2nd and 3rd-degree burns.
- The burned area typically appears necrotic, is yellowish in color, and emits a **distinct garlic-like odor**.
- It may emit luminescent fumes ("smoke") when exposed to air.

**II. Acute Ingestion (The Three-Stage Triad)**

The clinical progression of acute phosphorus ingestion follows three distinct stages:

1. **First Stage (1/2 hour to 6 hours post-ingestion):**
    - Intense burning pain in the mouth, throat, and abdomen, accompanied by severe vomiting and diarrhea.
    - The breath, vomitus, and feces have a **distinct garlic-like odor**.
    - ★ **Important Exam Point: Luminescent "Smoking" Vomitus:** The vomitus and feces are highly diagnostic because they **glow in the dark (phosphorescence)** and release white fumes ("smoke") on contact with air.
2. **Second Stage (1 to 4 days):**
    - A deceptive, **symptom-free latent period** where the patient feels clinically better.
    - However, subclinical toxic hepatitis is silently spreading, marked by progressively elevating liver transaminases.
3. **Third Stage (Multiorgan Failure):**
    - **Gastrointestinal:** Reappearance of severe vomiting, diarrhea, and hematemesis.
    - **Hepatic Failure:** Tender, enlarged hepatomegaly, profound jaundice, and pruritus.
    - **Renal Failure:** Oliguria, hematuria, casts, and complete anuria.
    - **Neurological:** Severe headache, insomnia, confusion, hallucinations, convulsions, and terminal coma.
    - **Electrolyte / Neuromuscular:** Systemic hypocalcemia triggers **carpopedal spasm, tetany, laryngeal stridor**, and opisthotonus.
    - **Hematological:** Coagulopathy leads to widespread purpura, epistaxis, and mucosal hemorrhages.
4. **Cause of Death:**
    - _Within first 24 hours:_ Fatal **cardiac dysrhythmias** secondary to profound hypocalcemia or hyperkalemia.
    - _After 24 hours:_ Fulminant **acute hepatic failure**.

**III. Chronic Phosphorus Poisoning ("Phossy Jaw")**

- Seen after years of industrial exposure to phosphorus fumes.
- ★ **Phossy Jaw (Lucifer's Jaw / Glass Jaw):**
    - Begins as a severe toothache near a decayed tooth, progressing to swelling of the jaw and loosening of teeth.
    - Necrosis of the gums ensues, leading to chronic osteomyelitis and sequestration of the mandible.
    - Multiple draining sinuses form, discharging highly offensive, **foul-smelling pus**.
- Constitutional symptoms include severe cachexia, weight loss, anemia, cirrhosis, and jaundice.

**Autopsy & Postmortem Findings** 🔍🩺

**I. External Findings**

- Marked emaciation, deep jaundice, and **purpuric skin hemorrhages**.
- A distinct garlic-like odor may be detected around the mouth and nostrils.
- ★ **Important Exam Point: PM Staining:** Postmortem hypostasis is characteristically **dark brown** in color.

**II. Internal Findings**

- **Multiple Visceral Hemorrhages:** Widespread petechial ecchymoses in muscles, GIT mucosa, lungs, brain, endocardium, and pericardium.
- **Stomach & Intestines:**
    - Mucosa appears swollen, thickened, softened, and corroded with yellowish or grayish-white patches.
    - The stomach contents are highly acidic, emit a strong garlic odor, and contain **luminous (glowing) particles** under dark conditions.
- **The Liver (Classic Triad):**
    - Enlarged, yellow, extremely soft, greasy, and **easily ruptured on touch**.
    - Histopathology shows diffuse toxic hepatitis with **ballooning degeneration, microvesicular steatosis (fatty change), and extensive necrosis**.
- **Kidneys:** Enlarged, pale, greasy, and yellow due to fatty degeneration.
- **Lungs:** Presence of **fat emboli** in the pulmonary arterioles and capillaries.

**III. Viscera Preservation**

- ★ **Important Exam Point: PAPPA Mnemonic:** Elemental phosphorus is highly soluble in organic solvents. Under the **PAPPA** rule, **Rectified Spirit is strictly contraindicated** as a preservative. The harvested viscera must be preserved solely in a **Saturated solution of common salt**
