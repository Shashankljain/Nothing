[[Sexual offence]]
For an MBBS student, the topic of **Rape** is a critical component of Forensic Medicine, especially with the transition from the Indian Penal Code (IPC) to the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**. Understanding the new sections and stringent punishments is essential for both university exams and clinical practice.

---

### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition (Section 63 BNS, 2023)** A man is said to commit rape if he penetrates his penis, or any object/body part, into the **vagina, oral cavity, anal canal, or urethra** of a woman, or makes her do so with him or another person, under specific circumstances.

**The Seven Conditions of Rape** Under Section 63 BNS, an act is considered rape if it occurs under any of these conditions:
==(I IDC WAU)==
1. **Against her will.**
2. **Without her consent.**
3. **Consent obtained by fear:** Putting her or someone she cares about in fear of death or hurt.
4. **Impersonation:** When the man knows he is not her husband, but she consents believing he is her husband.
5. **Deceitful Means:** Consent obtained by a false promise of marriage (now specifically addressed in BNS).
6. **Incapacity to Consent:** When the woman is unable to understand the nature of the act due to **unsoundness of mind, intoxication**, or administration of any stupefying substance.
7. **Underage (Statutory Rape):** With or without her consent, if the girl is **under 18 years of age**.

### **Relevant Law (BNS, 2023)**

★ **Important Exam Point:** The sections for rape have shifted from IPC 375/376 to **BNS 63/64**.

|**BNS Section**|**Offence Type**|**Punishment**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Section 64(1)**|General punishment for rape|Min. **10 years** to Life Imprisonment + Fine.|
|**Section 64(2)**|Rape by persons in authority (Police, Public servants, Management of hospitals)|Min. **10 years** to Life (for remainder of life) + Fine.|
|**Section 65(1)**|Rape of a girl **<16 years**|Min. **20 years** to Life (for remainder of life) + Fine.|
|**Section 65(2)**|Rape of a girl **<12 years**|Min. **20 years** to Life or **Death Penalty**.|
|**Section 66**|Rape causing **Death or Vegetative State**|**20 years** to Life or **Death Penalty**.|
|**Section 70(1)**|**Gang Rape** (Victim >18 years)|Min. **20 years** to Life (for remainder of life).|
|**Section 70(2)**|**Gang Rape** (Victim <18 years)|**Life Imprisonment** to **Death Penalty**.|
|**Section 71**|**Repeat Offenders**|**Life Imprisonment** to **Death Penalty**.|

### **Recent Amendments and Legal Concepts**
**Major Recent Amendments in BNS, 2023**

★ **Important Exam Point: The sections for rape have changed from IPC 375/376 to BNS 63/64****.**

|**Change/Amendment**|**Description**|
|---|---|
|**Section Consolidation**|Unnatural sexual offences (formerly IPC 377) have been largely deleted or integrated into the broader definition of rape under BNS 63.|
|**False Promise of Marriage**|**Section 69 BNS** now specifically punishes sexual intercourse by "deceitful means" or a false promise of marriage with up to 10 years of imprisonment.|
|**Statutory Age**|The minimum age for valid sexual consent remains **18 years**; any act below this age is rape regardless of consent.|
|**Aggravated Punishment**|Introduction of the death penalty for the rape of a girl **under 12 years** and for certain **gang rape** cases.|
|**Vegetative State**|**Section 66 BNS** provides for 20 years to life imprisonment or death if the rape causes the victim to be in a persistent vegetative state.|

### **Medico-Legal Examination**

#### **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023**

- **Examination of Victim (Section 184 BNSS):**
    - The victim must be sent to a registered medical practitioner (RMP) within **24 hours** of receiving information.
    - The examination **must** be conducted by a **female RMP**.
    - **Informed Consent:** Explicit consent from the woman is required; if she is <18, consent is taken from the guardian.
    - **Reporting:** The medical report must be prepared within **7 days**.
- **Examination of Accused (Section 52 BNSS):**
    - Conducted at the request of a police officer (not below Sub-Inspector).
    - **Reasonable force** can be used to conduct the examination if necessary.

---

### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Legal vs. Medical Proof:** For the law, the proof is **Consent**; for the doctor, the proof is **Sex** (physical evidence of penetration).
- **Presence of Semen:** If the victim is <18 years, the recovery of any component of semen (spermatozoa/p30) is consistent with the commission of a crime because consent is invalid.
- **Vaginal Swabs:** These must be collected within **3 days** for DNA profiling and sperm motility.
- **Istanbul Protocol:** International guidelines for medical professionals concerning the documentation of torture and ill-treatment.

---

### **Common Mistakes Students Make**

1. **Old Sections:** Referring to IPC 375/376 instead of **BNS 63/64**.
2. **Consent Age:** Confusing the age of majority (18) with the older legal standard (16). BNS sets the age of consent at **18 years**.
3. **Examination Gender:** Forgetting that only a **female RMP** can examine a rape victim under BNSS 184.

---

### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **Rape Definition:** BNS 63 (Penetration of vagina/mouth/anus/urethra).
- **Consent:** Irrelevant if <18 years (Statutory Rape).
- **Punishment:** BNS 64 (10 yrs to Life); BNS 65-71 covers aggravated cases (Death penalty possible for child rape/gang rape).
- **Victim Exam:** BNSS 184 (Mandatory female doctor, <24 hours).
- **Accused Exam:** BNSS 52 (Can use reasonable force).
- **Evidence:** BSA 120 (Presumption of no consent).
