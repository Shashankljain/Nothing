| **1. Ovulatory Cycles**      | **Single (same) ovulatory cycle** [Image 140].                                                 | **Two different ovulatory cycles** [Image 140].                                            |

| **6. Autopsy/Birth Finding** | May show **Fetus Papyraceus** (one twin compressed and flattened by the other) [Image 140].    | Shows two live neonates born together with **marked disparities in maturity** [Image 140]. |
