---

### **1. Dead Born Fetus (Intrauterine Death / IUD)** ⚰️

- **Definition:** A fetus that has died **in utero** (inside the uterus) prior to the onset of labour or delivery [`input_file_365.png`].
- **Key Pathological Sign:** At autopsy, it classically shows aseptic sterile autolysis (**maceration**) or **mummification**, proving that death occurred before birth [`input_file_365.png`].

---

### **2. Stillborn Fetus** ⏳

- **Definition:** A viable fetus (gestational age \(\ge\) 28 weeks) that dies in the **birth canal** during the ==process of labour or delivery== [`input_file_364.png`, `input_file_365.png`].
- **Key Criterion:** The child is born dead, having **never breathed** or shown any other signs of life after complete expulsion from the mother [`input_file_365.png`, `input_file_368.png`].

---

### **3. Live Born Fetus (RBD Act, 1969)** 🎉

- **Definition:** The complete expulsion or extraction of a product of conception from the mother, which after such separation **shows any sign of life** [`input_file_368.png`].
- **Key Signs of Life:** Established **respiration**, **active crying**, or **voluntary muscle movements**, irrespective of the gestational age [`input_file_368.png`].
