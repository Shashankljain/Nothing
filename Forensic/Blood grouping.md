[[Trace evidence]]
- **Blood Group (Phenotype):** A classification of blood based on the presence or absence of inherited antigenic substances (proteins, carbohydrates, glycoproteins, or glycolipids) on the surface of red blood cells (RBCs).
- **Agglutinogen (Antigen):** The protein or carbohydrate molecule situated on the RBC membrane (e.g., Antigen A, Antigen B, or Rh Antigen D).
- **Agglutinin (Antibody):** The corresponding immunological protein present in the blood plasma (e.g., Anti-A or Anti-B).

**3. Classification of Blood Group Systems** 📊

There are over 30 recognized human blood group systems, but the ones of primary medico-legal significance include:

```
                            [BLOOD GROUP SYSTEMS]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                            ▼                            ▼
   [ABO System]                 [Rhesus System]              [Other Systems]
 • Discovered by Landsteiner  • Rh positive or negative    • MNS, Kell, Duffy, Kidd,
 • Groups: A, B, AB, O.       • Based on Antigen D.        • Lutheran, and HLA.
```

1. **The ABO System:** Comprises four primary blood groups (A, B, AB, and O) based on the presence of Antigens A, B, or H36.
2. **The Rhesus (Rh) System:** Based on the presence or absence of the **D antigen** on the RBC membrane (Rh-positive or Rh-negative)78.
3. **Other Red Cell Antigen Systems:** Includes **MNS, Kell, Duffy, Kidd, and Lutheran** systems, used as secondary markers in parentage testing29.
4. **The Human Leukocyte Antigen (HLA) System:** Highly polymorphic tissue-typing antigens present on nucleated white blood cells (lymphocytes and granulocytes)9.
5. **Serum Protein and Enzyme Markers:**
    - **Serum Proteins:** Gm and Km systems2.
    - **Red Cell Enzymes:** **Phosphoglucomutase (PGM)** and **Peptidase A (Pep A)**1011.
