# 🧠⚖️ **CIVIL & CRIMINAL RESPONSIBILITY OF THE INSANE** ⚖️🧠

Here is a highly structured, university-examination-oriented model answer updated with the latest criminal codes under the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** [Slide page 4].

---

## **1. BASIC CONCEPTS & DEFINITION** 📚

- ★ **Unsoundness of Mind (Insanity):** Although there is no strict statutory definition of "unsoundness of mind" in Indian law, medically it is characterized by a clinically significant disturbance in an individual's cognition, emotional regulation, or behavior.
- ★ **Responsibility:** In the legal sense, this refers to the **liability of a person for their acts or omissions**, and if these acts are against the law, their liability to be legally prosecuted and punished.
- **The Sane Presumption:** The law legally **presumes every person of the age of discretion is mentally sound (sane)**, until the contrary is proved. The burden of proving insanity as a defense rests entirely on the accused.

---

## **2. CIVIL RESPONSIBILITY: THE "CAMP-WT" MNEMONIC** 🏕️🐯

The question of civil responsibility arises when assessing an insane person's capacity to engage in standard legal and social activities.

### 🧠 **Mnemonic: "CAMP-WT"**

_(Think of an insane person wandering into a legal **CAMP** guarded by **W**ild **T**igers)_

```
                            [CIVIL RESPONSIBILITY]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────┬───────────┼───────────┬────────────────┐
         ▼                ▼           ▼           ▼                ▼
   [C - Contracts]  [M - Marriage] [A - Adoption] [P - Property] [W - Witness] [T - Testamentary]
```

#### **C – Contracts** 📄

- ★ **Rule:** Any contract entered into by an insane person is legally **invalid** if, at the time of making it, they were incapable of understanding its terms and forming a rational judgment as to its effects on their interest.
- ★ **Exception (Lucid Interval):** An insane person **is fully liable** for contracts entered into during a **lucid interval**(a temporary period during which all signs and symptoms of insanity disappear).

#### **M – Marriage & Divorce** 💍

- **Marriage:** Under personal laws (such as the Hindu Marriage Act), a marriage can be declared **null and void** (or voidable) if either party was incapable of giving valid consent due to unsoundness of mind at the time of the ceremony.
- **Divorce:** Severe, incurable insanity is a valid ground for judicial separation or divorce.

#### **A – Adoption** 👶

- **Rule:** Under the Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, an individual of unsound mind is **strictly prohibited** from either taking a child in adoption or giving a child away for adoption.

#### **P – Property & Estate Management** 🏡

- **Rule:** The court may appoint a legal **guardian** to look after the mentally ill person and a **manager** to manage their business or property. The court can also order the sale of their property to pay off debts or cover medical expenses.

#### **W – Witness Competency** 🗣️

- **Rule:** A mentally ill person is **not competent to testify in a court of law** if their illness actively prevents them from understanding the questions put to them and giving rational answers.

#### **T – Testamentary Capacity (Making a Will)** 📜

- **Rule:** Represents the capacity of a person to make a valid, legally binding will.
- ★ **Requirement:** The testator must possess a **"sound disposing mind" (compos mentis)**, which must be certified by a medical practitioner at the exact time the will is signed. A will made during a _lucid interval_ is valid, whereas a will made under active insane delusions is void.

## **3. CRIMINAL RESPONSIBILITY: THE "DNW" MNEMONIC** ⚖️🔨

### ★ **Relevant Law: Section 22 BNS, 2023 (formerly Section 84 IPC)** ★ **Important Exam Point**

This section is the cornerstone of criminal defense on the grounds of insanity:

> _"Nothing is an offense which is done by a person who, at the time of doing it, by reason of unsoundness of mind, is **incapable of knowing the nature of the act**, or what he is doing is **either wrong or contrary to the law**."_

### 🧠 **Mnemonic for Legal Insanity (Sec. 22 BNS): "D-N-W"**

_(Do Not Worry! The law protects an insane person if they meet the **D-N-W** criteria)_

|**Mnemonic Letter**|**Legal Element**|**Medico-Legal Significance**|
|:-:|:--|:--|
|**D**|**D**isease of the mind|The accused must have been laboring under a **defect of reason** arising from a clear mental disease.|
|**N**|**N**ature of the act|The mental defect was so severe that they did **not know the physical nature** of what they were doing.|
|**W**|**W**rong / contrary to law|Even if they knew the physical nature of the act, they **did not know that it was wrong** or forbidden by the law.|

---

### **A. The McNaughten Rules (1843)** 🏛️

The modern legal test of insanity is derived from the landmark English case of **Daniel McNaughten (1843)**, who shot Edward Drummond (secretary to PM Sir Robert Peel) under a paranoid delusion of persecution.

- ★ **The McNaughten Rule/Legal Test of Insanity:** Every man is presumed sane until the defense clearly proves that, **at the time of the crime**, the accused was laboring under such a defect of reason from a disease of the mind that they did not know the nature of their act, or did not know it was wrong.

---

### **B. Differentiating Medical vs. Legal Insanity (Examiner Favorite!)** 📊

★ **Important Exam Point:** Always replicate this comparison table in your theory answers to score full marks:

|**Parameter**|**Medical Insanity** 🩺|**Legal Insanity** ⚖️|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1. Primary Focus**|Focuses on the **prolonged psychiatric illness**and behavioral disturbances of the brain.|Focuses strictly on **cognitive impairment at the exact moment of the offense**.|
|**2. Core Standard**|Diagnosed by a psychiatrist based on clinical symptoms and DSM/ICD criteria.|Assessed by a judge/jury using the **"Right or Wrong"**cognitive test.|
|**3. Nature of Defect**|Can involve emotional instability, hallucinations, and irresistible impulses.|Restricts itself **only to intellectual and cognitive faculties**(reason, memory, and understanding).|
|**4. Law's View**|Any degree of mental illness is considered medical insanity.|Only severe mental illness that completely obliterates the **D-N-W** criteria is recognized as legal insanity.|

## **4. CLINICAL CORRELATION & REAL-LIFE EXAMPLES** 🩺

### **The Case of the "Delusional Defense"**

- **Scenario A (Not Guilty):** Under an insane delusion, a person believes another man is a **wild animal** and shoots him. He is **not criminally responsible** because he was incapable of knowing the physical _nature_ of his act (**N** in **DNW**).
- **Scenario B (Not Guilty):** Under a delusion that a man is actively attempting to murder him, a person kills that man. He is **not criminally responsible** because, if the delusional facts were real, he was acting in lawful self-defense.
- **Scenario C (GUILTY):** Under a delusion that someone has insulted his character or ruined his business, a person kills him. He **is held fully responsible** because, even if the delusional facts were true, the law does not permit anyone to kill in revenge.

## **5. HIGH-YIELD EXAM CORNER & VIVA QUESTIONS** ★

### **Viva Questions & Model Answers** 💬

**Q1: What is the legal status of an "Irresistible Impulse" as a defense in India?**  
**Answer:** Sir, an **Irresistible Impulse** (where a person knows an act is wrong/illegal but cannot control their physical urge to perform it due to mental impairment) **is not recognized as a defense under Section 22 BNS, 2023**. The legal test in India is purely cognitive ("Right or Wrong" test) and does not cover volitional defects.

**Q2: Under what section of the new BNS, 2023 is consent given by an insane person deemed invalid?**  
**Answer:** Sir, under **Section 28 BNS, 2023** (formerly Section 90 IPC), any consent given by a mentally unsound or intoxicated person who cannot understand the nature and consequences of their consent is legally **invalid** [141, Slide page 10].

---

### **Common Mistakes Students Make** ❌

- **Citing Section 84 IPC or Section 90 IPC:** In modern university exams, you must integrate the **BNS, 2023**equivalents. Refer to Section 84 IPC as **Section 22 BNS** [143, Slide page 4], and Section 90 IPC as **Section 28 BNS**[141, Slide page 10].
- **Confusing Lucid Interval with Insanity:** Remember, acts committed during a **lucid interval** are legally **binding and fully punishable** because the individual is temporarily in a sound state of mind.

## **6. 1-MINUTE QUICK REVISION SUMMARY** ⏱️

1. **Civil Responsibility (CAMP-WT):**
    - **C**ontracts (Void unless in a lucid interval).
    - **M**arriage (Voidable/ground for divorce).
    - **A**doption (Strictly prohibited).
    - **P**roperty (Manager/guardian appointed).
    - **W**itness (Not competent if they cannot answer rationally).
    - **T**estamentary Capacity (Requires "sound disposing mind" certified by a doctor).
2. **Criminal Responsibility (DNW):**
    - Governed by **Section 22 BNS, 2023** (McNaughten's Rule / Legal Test of Insanity).
    - Protected only if they suffer from a **D**isease of the mind, do not know the **N**ature of the act, or do not know it is **W**rong/contrary to law.
