### **Medico-Legal Importance of Age**

---

#### **Basic Concepts**

**Definition** In forensic medicine, **Age Estimation** is the determination of the chronological age of an individual (living or dead) based on ==physical==, ==dental==, and ==skeletal== development.

**Introduction** Age is considered one of the **"Primary Characteristics"** of identification (along with sex and stature) because it remains relatively stable and can be determined even after death until skeletonization is complete. Establishing age is a mandatory step in any medico-legal examination to determine ==legal rights, responsibilities, and liabilities.==

---

#### **Medico-Legal Importance: Why is Age Significant?**

Age dictates a person's standing before the law. The importance can be broadly classified into criminal, civil, and social aspects.

★ **Important Exam Point:** The determination of "Majority" (18 years) is the most frequent request in medico-legal practice.

#### **Relevant Law (Updated BNS, 2023)**

University exams now require knowledge of the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)** and **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)**.

|**Age**|**Legal Provision**|**Medico-Legal Significance**|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**< 7 Years**|**Section 20 BNS**|**Doli Incapax:** A child under 7 is not criminally responsible for any act.|
|**7–12 Years**|**Section 21 BNS**|**Maturity of Understanding:** Liability depends on whether the child has attained sufficient maturity to judge the nature of their act [3, 5; 4, 101].|
|**< 12 Years**|**Section 27 BNS**|**Consent:** A child under 12 cannot give valid consent for any act that may cause harm; consent must be given by a guardian.|
|**< 12 Years**|**Section 118 BSA**|**Oath:** A child under 12 years is not required to take an oath before giving evidence in court.|
|**< 14 Years**|**CLPR Act**|**Child Labor:** Prohibition of employment of children below 14 years in any occupation.|
|**< 16 (M) / 18 (F)**|**Kidnapping**|Taking a minor out of the custody of a lawful guardian without consent.|
|**18 Years**|**Majority**|Age of voting, making a valid will, and obtaining a permanent driving license.|
|**18 Years**|**BNS 63**|**Age of Consent for Sex:** Sexual intercourse with a girl below 18, even with her consent, is **Statutory Rape** [3, 6; 4, 101].|
|**21 Years**|**Marriage**|Minimum legal age for marriage for **males** [3, 54; 4, 101].|

|**Age Group**|**Medico-Legal Relevance**|

|**16–18 Years**|**Juvenile Justice:** Juveniles committing "heinous crimes" (e.g., rape/murder) may be tried as adults after psychiatric assessment.|

#### **Medico-Legal Importance in Criminal Investigations**

1. **Corpus Delicti:** In cases of unidentified dead bodies, age estimation helps prove the "body of the crime" and aids in narrowing down missing person searches.
2. **Rape and Sexual Offences:** The age of the victim determines the severity of the punishment under BNS 64/65. If the victim is <18, consent is immaterial [3, 6; 4, 417].
3. **Kidnapping and Abduction:** The law distinguishes between "minors" and "adults" based on specific age cut-offs (16 for males, 18 for females).
4. **Criminal Liability:** Age determines if a person is sent to a reformatory school (Juvenile Home) or a regular prison [3, 32; 4, 101].

---

#### **Medico-Legal Importance in Civil Cases**

1. **Marriage:** Validating the legality of a marriage contract.
2. **Testamentary Capacity:** A person must be of "major" age (18) and sound mind to make a valid will.
3. **Competency of Witness:** While there is **no age limit to testify** (BSA 118), the court must be satisfied that the child understands the questions and can give rational answers [3, 3; 4, 100].
4. **Management of Property:** Only individuals who have attained majority can legally transfer property or enter into contracts.

---

#### **High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Section 20 BNS:** Replaces Section 82 IPC (Infant's immunity from crime).
- ★ **Section 106 BNS:** Relates to death by negligence; age of the practitioner and the victim are noted during the inquiry.
- ★ **18 Years:** The universal age for "Majority" in India for most civil and criminal purposes.
- ★ **Identification:** Age is one of the four primary characteristics that are **unaltered even after death**.

#### **Viva Questions**

1. **Can a 10-year-old child be a witness in court?**
    - _Ans:_ Yes. Under **Section 118 BSA**, any person can testify if they can understand the questions and give rational answers. However, if they are <12, no oath is required [3, 3; 4, 101].
2. **What is the "Age of Consent" for sex in India?**
    - _Ans:_ 18 years for both boys and girls under BNS.
3. **What is 'Doli Incapax'?**
    - _Ans:_ It means "incapable of doing harm." Under **Section 20 BNS**, a child under 7 years is legally incapable of committing a crime.
4. **Why is age estimation important in a case of a dead body found in a forest?**
    - _Ans:_ To establish the **Corpus Delicti** (identity of the victim) and to help police match the remains with missing person reports.

#### **Quick Revision Summary**

- **<7 years:** Total immunity from crime (BNS 20).
- **12 years:** Consent for exam is valid; no oath needed in court.
- **14 years:** Limit for hazardous employment.
- **18 years:** Age of majority, voting, marriage (females), and valid consent for sex/MTP.
- **21 years:** Marriage age for males.
- **Old Age:** Helps in inheritance claims and identifying pension frauds.
