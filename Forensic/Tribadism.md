[[Sexual offence]]
**Tribadism** (also known as **Lesbianism** or **Sapphism**) is defined as a sexual relationship or sexual gratification between two females.

**Introduction**

- **Synonyms:** Lesbianism, Sapphism, Female Homosexuality.
- **Roles:** In some cases, specific roles are identified:
    - **Active Agent:** Referred to as the 'Butch' or 'Dyke'.
    - **Passive Agent:** Referred to as the 'Femme'.
- **Historical Classification:** Traditionally classified under **unnatural sexual offences** (non-peno-vaginal route); however, this classification is largely obsolete under modern law.

---

**Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Civil Cases (Divorce):** Habitual practice of lesbianism may be cited as a ground for **divorce** under personal laws in India.
- **False Allegations:** A woman may falsely charge another with assault or "indecent assault" to settle a personal grudge.
- **Reconstructing the Act:** In cases where injury or death occurs during the act, forensic evidence may help identify the nature of the relationship (e.g., specific bite marks, clothing stains).

---

**Relevant Law (2023)**

★ **Important Exam Point:** The status of "Unnatural Offences" has changed drastically with the implementation of the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023**.

- **Decriminalisation:** Consensual sexual acts between adults (age >18 years) are **not punishable** under the BNS, regardless of whether they are natural or unnatural.
- **Deletion of IPC 377:** The section that specifically criminalised "unnatural offences against the order of nature" has been **deleted** in the BNS.
- **Protection of Children (POCSO):** If any such act involve a child (below 18 years), it is strictly governed and punished under the **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012**.
- **Indecent Assault:** If a woman uses force or criminal force on another woman to outrage her modesty, it may be prosecuted under **Section 74 BNS** [derived from 452].

---

**Medico-Legal Examination**

Examination of females in cases of alleged non-consensual tribadism is rare. Findings are generally non-specific:

- **Signs of Struggle:** Bruises, nail marks, or hair plucking may be seen on the breasts or thighs.
- **Biological Evidence:** Saliva (from bite marks) or other biological stains on clothing may be collected for DNA profiling.
- **Genital Findings:** Unlike sodomy or rape, there are usually no specific chronic anatomical changes to the genitalia in habitual practitioners.

---

**High-Yield Exam Points**

- ★ **Core Definition:** Sexual relationship between two females.
- ★ **Legal Status:** Decriminalised for consenting adults under **BNS 2023**.
- ★ **Terminology:** Active (Butch), Passive (Femme).
