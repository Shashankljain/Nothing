[[Sexual offence]]
**Bestiality** (also known as **Zooerasty** or **Zoophilia**) refers to sexual contact or intercourse between a human being and a lower animal.

**Classification** It was traditionally classified as an **unnatural sexual offence** because the act occurs via a non-peno-vaginal route (typically anal or vaginal penetration of an animal).

---

**2. Medico-Legal Importance**

- **Criminal Investigation:** Physical and biological evidence is used to link a suspect to the act or the animal.
- **Civil Cases:** Habitual bestiality can be used as a strong ground for **divorce** or legal separation.
- **Forensic Identification:** It is one of the few instances where cross-species biological evidence (animal hair/human semen) is the primary focus of the investigation.

---

**3. Relevant Law (2023)**

★ **Important Exam Point: The shift from IPC to BNS.**

- **IPC 377 (Old Law):** Bestiality was specifically criminalised under "unnatural offences."
- **BNS, 2023 Update:** The category of "Unnatural Offences" (formerly IPC 377) has been **deleted** as a standalone section in the new code.
- **Current Status:** While the specific section for unnatural acts is removed, acts involving animals may still be prosecuted under laws related to **cruelty to animals** or other relevant provisions, depending on the circumstances.

---

**4. Medico-Legal Examination**

In cases of alleged bestiality, the medical officer must examine both the suspect (human) and, occasionally, the animal (with the help of a veterinarian).

**Evidence Collection from the Suspect (Human)**

- **Animal Hair:** This is a crucial finding. Animal hair may be found adhered to the genitalia, body, or clothing of the accused.
- **Biological Traces:** The suspect’s clothing or body may show stains of animal blood, feces, or vaginal secretions.
- **Signs of Struggle:** Look for injuries such as bites, scratches, or kicks inflicted by the animal during the act.

**Evidence Collection from the Animal**

- **Human Semen:** The definitive proof of bestiality is the detection of **human spermatozoa** inside the genitalia or anal canal of the animal.
- **Seminal Markers:** Tests for **p30 (Prostate Specific Antigen)** or acid phosphatase can confirm the presence of human seminal fluid in the animal.
