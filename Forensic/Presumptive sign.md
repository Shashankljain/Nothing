# 🤰✨ **PRESUMPTIVE SIGNS OF PREGNANCY (5-MARK SHORT NOTE)** ✨🤰

---

### **1. Basic Concepts & Definition** 📚

- **Definition:** **Presumptive signs** of pregnancy are primarily **subjective symptoms** experienced by the mother, along with some non-specific objective physical changes [Image 11].
- ★ **Important Exam Point (The Ground Rule):** These signs are **highly suggestive but never diagnostic** of pregnancy. They are easily simulated by other physiological, emotional, or pathological conditions (e.g., pseudocyesis, pelvic tumors, or endocrine disorders) [Image 7, Image 11].

#### **I. Local & Genitourinary Signs**(ABCDE)

1. ★ **Amenorrhea (Cessation of Menses):**
    - This is the **earliest clinical sign** of pregnancy [Image 11].
    - _Exam Pitfall:_ Can be caused by emotional stress, severe anemia, malnutrition, or endocrine dysfunction.
2. ★ **Chadwick’s or Jacquemier’s Sign:**
    - _Timing:_ Seen at about the **\(4^{\text{th}}\) week** of gestation [Image 11].
    - _Pathology:_ The mucosa of the vestibule and vagina changes from its normal pink to a **dusty blue/purplish color** [Image 11].
    - _Mechanism:_ Caused by **local venous obstruction** and active hypervascular congestion of the pelvic organs [Image 11].
3. **Breast Changes (The Mammillary Toxidrome):**
    - Enlargement, vascular engorgement, and subjective tenderness/tingling of the breasts.
    - ★ **Montgomery’s Tubercles:** Hyperplasia of the sebaceous glands surrounding the primary areola [Image 11].
4. **Urinary Disturbances:**
    - Increased **frequency of micturition** during the \(1^{\text{st}}\) trimester due to the mechanical pressure of the anteverted, enlarging gravid uterus on the adjacent urinary bladder [Image 11].

#### **II. Systemic & Subjective Signs**

1. ★ **Quickening ("Feeling of Life"):**
    - _Timing:_ Typically felt by the mother at about the **\(16^{\text{th}}\) to \(20^{\text{th}}\) week** of gestation [Image 11].
    - _Clinical Feature:_ The **first maternal perception** of fetal movements in the abdomen, described as a faint "fluttering" sensation [Image 11].
    - _Diagnostic Catch:_ Subjective; can be simulated by flatulence, bowel peristalsis, or psychiatric conditions (Pseudocyesis) [Image 7, Image 11].
(Emesis)2. **Morning Sickness (Emesis Gravidarum):**
    - Nausea and vomiting, classically occurring in the early morning [Image 11]. It is triggered by the rapid rise in human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) and estrogen levels.
(dark)3. **Chloasma Gravidarum (Mask of Pregnancy):**
    - Symmetrical, hyperpigmented brown patches appearing over the forehead, nose, and cheekbones.

### **3. Medico-Legal Importance (MLI)** ⚖️

Even though presumptive signs are not absolute, establishing a working diagnosis of pregnancy is critical under the newly implemented **Indian Criminal Codes (2023)**:

- ★ **Section 456 BNSS, 2023 (formerly Sec 416 CrPC) – Commutation of Death Sentence:**
    - If a woman sentenced to death is found to be pregnant, the High Court **must postpone the execution** and **shall commute the sentence to imprisonment for life** [Image 22].
- **The MTP Act, 2021:** Establishing pregnancy early allows vulnerable women (such as rape survivors or minors) to access safe, legal abortions up to the **\(24^{\text{th}}\) week** [Image 12].
